EXPIRY DAY PLAYBOOK THE MOST DANGEROUS DAY

Expiry day is a losing game for option buyers by default. Time decay (called Theta) removes ₹40 to ₹60 of premium from an At the Money option in a single expiry session. You need a real directional move of more than 0.5 percent in the index just to break even on the decay. Be very deliberate or stay completely out of the market.
WHY EXPIRY IS DIFFERENT FROM OTHER DAYS
TIME DECAY (THETA) RULES EVERYTHING
By 3:30 PM, every single option contract on the expiring series MUST settle. Time value falls to zero. A ₹100 At the Money option at 9:15 AM may be worth only ₹15 by 2:00 PM even if the index price has not moved at all. You are fighting the clock, not the chart.
MAX PAIN ACTS LIKE A MAGNET
Option writers (mostly large institutions) have billions of rupees worth of positions. They actively defend the strike where their collective profit is the maximum (this strike is called Max Pain). The price gets pulled toward Max Pain unless a major news event overrides this gravity.
IMPLIED VOLATILITY COLLAPSE
Implied Volatility drops sharply as the session progresses. Even if your direction is correct, the falling Implied Volatility shrinks your premium. Buying Out of the Money options in the afternoon is like buying a lottery ticket with less than 5 percent chance of winning.
PIN RISK
Near expiry, the price can "pin" (get stuck) at the nearest strike with very heavy Open Interest. At the Money options may flip back and forth between In the Money and Out of the Money repeatedly in the last 30 minutes, creating violent premium swings without giving you any clear direction to trade.
HOUR-BY-HOUR EXPIRY PLAYBOOK
This is the playbook for Nifty expiry (Tuesday). The same logic applies to Sensex expiry on Thursday. All times are in Indian Standard Time.
Time What's Happening Best Trade Avoid
9:15 to 9:45 AM The opening range is forming. Overnight positions get stopped out. Implied Volatility is elevated. Wait. Observe. Mark the Opening Range High and Low. Chasing gap moves, buying based on the first 15-minute candle
9:45 to 11:30 AM The directional phase. If trending moves are going to happen today, they happen here. At the Money scalp on a clear breakout with volume. 20 to 30 point stop loss. Book at 1 to 2 risk-to-reward. Out of the Money options, deep In the Money (illiquid)
11:30 to 1:00 PM Consolidation begins. Volume drops. Max Pain starts pulling the price toward itself. Only top-conviction setups. Exit losing trades quickly. Holding losing trades, averaging down

2:00 to 3:00 PM Final scramble. Option writers defend Max Pain. Sharp reversals are common. EXIT all long option positions New positions, chasing reversals

When to take this trade — The Only Expiry Setup Worth Taking
Setup
It is between 9:45 AM and 11:30 AM (the only safe trading window on expiry day). A clear directional candle with high volume has formed after the opening range. The breakout direction is supported by Open Interest data (for example, Put writers are adding positions for an upward move). India Volatility Index is between 12 and 22 (avoid extreme readings). The current price is more than 50 points away from Max Pain (so the magnet effect is not immediate).
Entry
Buy ONE lot (not more) of an At the Money Call Option (for upward) or Put Option (for downward). The option must have a Delta greater than 0.4 and a bid-ask spread of 1 rupee or less on Nifty.
Stop Loss
Two stops, whichever hits first: (1) Index price moves 20 to 30 points against you. (2) Option premium drops 30 percent from your entry price. No mental stops — set them in your broker's system.
Target
1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio (your reward should be 2 times your risk). Book the entire position there. Do not get greedy on expiry day — the time decay will reverse your gains in 20 to 30 minutes if you wait.
Skip if
It is past 11:30 AM, the price is within 30 points of Max Pain, India Volatility Index is above 25 or below 11, you have already had 2 losing trades today, or there is a major event (Reserve Bank of India announcement, Federal Reserve meeting, election results) happening.
THE EXPIRY-DAY DO RULES
TRADE AT THE MONEY OPTIONS ONLY
At the Money or one strike either side of At the Money. The Delta should be greater than 0.4. These have the tightest bid-ask spreads and the highest Gamma response to index moves. Never buy deep Out of the Money options on expiry day.
SCALP, DO NOT SWING
Expiry is a day for quick 15 to 30 minute scalp trades with a 20 to 30 point stop loss and a 1 to 2 target. Holding for more than 1 hour means losing to time decay. Book the profit fast and move on.
BE FLAT BY 2:30 PM
By 2:30 PM, whatever premium is left is evaporating in 20-minute chunks. Exit ALL long option positions by this time regardless of your profit and loss. You will sleep well that night.
RESPECT MAX PAIN
Check the Max Pain level at 9:15 AM and at every hour after that. If the index is more than 50 points away from Max Pain, expect the price to drift toward it. Trade with this magnet effect (for example, if the index is above Max Pain, the bias for the day is bearish).
REDUCE YOUR POSITION SIZE
Expiry day risk is 2 to 3 times higher than a normal day because of Implied Volatility and time decay. Cut your usual lot count in half. One At the Money lot is plenty. Emotional stakes run high — manage them with smaller size.
KEEP A SEPARATE TRADING LOG
Track your expiry day profit and loss separately from the rest of your trades. Most retail traders discover, after 3 to 6 months of data, that expiry day accounts for more than 60 percent of all their losses.
THE EXPIRY-DAY DO NOT RULES
NEVER BUY OUT OF THE MONEY AFTER 12:00 PM
Out of the Money options after noon are mathematical lottery tickets. Time decay plus Implied Volatility decay destroy them. Even if your direction is correct, the option rarely recovers to a profit.
NEVER AVERAGE A LOSING TRADE
Averaging down (buying more when you are losing) is bad on any day. On expiry day it is catastrophic — time decay works against both legs of your position. Exit at your stop loss, never "give it more time".
NEVER HOLD PAST 3:00 PM
The last 30 minutes are pin-risk chaos. Your At the Money option can swing ₹40 in 2 minutes on no real news. Last-minute moves by big operators dominate. Be out of all positions.
NEVER TRADE WITHOUT A STOP LOSS
A "mental stop loss" on expiry day is self-harm. Set a hard stop loss in the broker's system. Time decay will eat your position while you hesitate. Automate the exit.
NEVER CHASE A REVERSAL
A sharp mid-session reversal on expiry is usually big operator manipulation designed to trap traders chasing the new direction. Let reversals happen. Trade the SECOND leg of the move, not the first.
NEVER TRADE ON EVENT DAYS
If expiry day coincides with a major event (Reserve Bank of India meeting, Federal Reserve announcement, elections, results of major index stocks), stay completely flat. Implied Volatility plus time decay plus event risk equals triple-directional risk. Paper-trade only that day.
SAME-DAY EXPIRY (ZERO DAYS TO EXPIRY) MECHANICS
Same-day expiry means trading options that expire on the SAME day. On Tuesday for Nifty and Thursday for Sensex, the weekly contract has zero days remaining. This is the most asymmetric instrument retail traders have access to — it is capable of producing 500 percent gains or 100 percent losses in just a few hours.
GAMMA EXPLOSION
At the Money options have extreme Gamma on same-day expiry. A 30-point Nifty move can take an At the Money option from ₹60 to ₹150. The same move against you takes it to ₹10. Huge speed in both directions.
TIME DECAY ACCELERATION
Time decay is roughly steady across the day, but the per-hour rate is the highest of the entire week. An At the Money option losing ₹40 in a flat 2-hour window is normal. Every minute spent in a losing trade is expensive.
IMPLIED VOLATILITY FLOOR
Implied Volatility gradually flattens to zero by market close. Buying options in the afternoon rarely benefits from rising Implied Volatility — this lever is largely dead. Direction is the only edge left.
WHO SHOULD TRADE SAME-DAY EXPIRY
Experienced intraday traders only. Never beginners. If you cannot consistently make money on a normal Wednesday, same-day expiry on Tuesday or Thursday will destroy your account.

THE EXPIRY DAY SURVIVAL CHECKLIST

  • If you had 2 or more losing trades in the morning, stop trading for the rest of the day. Expiry day compounds mistakes faster than any other day.
  • If India Volatility Index jumped more than 10 percent from yesterday's close, cut your position size in half or skip the day entirely.
  • If you are in a losing streak (drawdown) for the month, skip expiry day completely. Take a paper-trading day instead.
  • Set your broker's auto-square-off alert for 2:45 PM — force yourself flat before the chaos zone begins.
  • Compare your expiry-day profit and loss to your non-expiry profit and loss every month. If expiry is net negative for 2 months in a row, stop trading expiry day. You are donating money.
