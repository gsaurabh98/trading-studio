EXPIRY DAY PLAYBOOK THE MOST DANGEROUS DAY

Expiry day is negative-sum for option buyers. Theta decay removes ₹40–60 of premium on an ATM option in a single session. You need a genuine directional move >0.5% just to break even on the decay. Be deliberate or stay out.
WHY EXPIRY IS DIFFERENT
THETA IS EVERYTHING
By 3:30 PM, every option on the expiring series must settle. Time value → zero. A ₹100 ATM option at 9:15 AM may be ₹15 by 2:00 PM even if price is unchanged. You are fighting the clock, not the chart.
MAX PAIN MAGNET
Option writers (institutions) have billions of rupees staked. They actively defend the strike where their collective profit is maximum. Price gets pulled toward Max Pain unless a news event overrides.
IV COLLAPSE
IV drops sharply as the session progresses. Even if your direction is right, IV crush shrinks your premium. Buying OTM options in afternoon = lottery ticket with <5% win rate.
PIN RISK
Near expiry, price can "pin" to the nearest heavy-OI strike. ATM options may oscillate between ITM and OTM repeatedly in the last 30 min, creating violent premium swings without directional edge.
HOUR-BY-HOUR EXPIRY PLAYBOOK
This is the playbook for Nifty expiry (Tuesday) — same logic applies to Sensex on Thursday. Times are IST.

THE EXPIRY-DAY DO RULES
TRADE ATM ONLY
ATM or ATM ±1 strike. Delta >0.4. Tightest bid-ask spreads. Highest Gamma response to index moves. Never deep OTM on expiry.
SCALP, DON'T SWING
Expiry is for quick 15–30 min scalps with 20–30 pt SL and 1:2 target. Holding >1 hour = losing to theta. Book fast, move on.
FLAT BY 2:30 PM
By 2:30 PM, whatever premium is left is evaporating in 20-minute chunks. Exit ALL long option positions by this time regardless of P&L. Sleep well.
RESPECT MAX PAIN
Check Max Pain at 9:15 and at every hour. If index is 50+ pts away, expect gravitation. Trade with the magnet (e.g., index above Max Pain = bearish bias for the day).
SIZE DOWN
Expiry day risk is 2–3x a normal day due to IV / theta. Halve your usual lot count. One ATM lot is plenty. Emotional stakes run high — manage them with size.
KEEP A SEPARATE LOG
Expiry day P&L should be tracked separately from other days. Most retail traders discover, after 3–6 months of data, that expiry accounts for 60%+ of their losses.
THE EXPIRY-DAY DON'T RULES
NEVER BUY OTM AFTER 12:00 PM
OTM options after noon are mathematical lottery tickets. Theta + IV decay destroy them. Even a correct direction rarely recovers to a profit.
NEVER AVERAGE A LOSER
Averaging down is bad every day. On expiry it's catastrophic — theta works against both legs. Exit at SL, never "give it time".
NEVER HOLD PAST 3:00 PM
The last 30 minutes are pin-risk chaos. Your ATM option can swing ₹40 in 2 minutes on no real news. Operators' last-minute plays dominate. Be out.
NEVER TRADE WITHOUT STOP
A "mental SL" on expiry is self-harm. Set a hard SL in the system. Theta will eat the position while you hesitate. Automate the exit.
NEVER CHASE REVERSALS
A sharp mid-session reversal on expiry is usually operator manipulation to trap the fresh direction chasers. Let reversals happen. Trade the SECOND leg, not the first.
NEVER TRADE ON EVENTS
If expiry coincides with a major event (RBI, Fed, elections, results of index heavyweights), stay flat. IV + theta + event = triple-directional risk. Paper-trade only.
0 DTE (SAME-DAY EXPIRY) MECHANICS
0 DTE = Zero Days To Expiry = trading options that expire the SAME day. On Tuesday (Nifty) and Thursday (Sensex), the weekly contract becomes 0 DTE. This is the most asymmetric instrument retail has access to — capable of 500% gains or 100% losses in hours.
GAMMA EXPLOSION
ATM options have extreme Gamma on 0 DTE. A 30-point Nifty move can take an ATM option from ₹60 to ₹150. Same move against you takes it to ₹10. Huge velocity in both directions.
THETA ACCELERATION
Theta is roughly linear during the day but per-hour is the highest of the week. An ATM option losing ₹40 in a flat 2-hour window is normal. Every minute in a losing trade is expensive.
IV FLOOR
IV gradually flattens toward zero at close. Buying options in the afternoon rarely profits from IV expansion — the IV lever is largely dead. Direction is the only edge left.
WHO SHOULD TRADE 0 DTE
Experienced intraday traders only. Never beginners. If you can't consistently profit on a Wednesday, 0 DTE will destroy your account on Tuesday / Thursday.

THE EXPIRY SURVIVAL CHECKLIST

  • If you had 2+ losing trades in the morning, stop trading for the day. Expiry compounds mistakes.
  • If India VIX jumped >10% from yesterday, halve your size or skip the day.
  • If you're in drawdown for the month, skip expiry entirely. Take a paper-trading day.
  • Set your broker's square-off alert for 2:45 PM — force yourself flat before the chaos zone.
  • Compare your expiry-day P&L to your non-expiry P&L every month. If expiry is net-negative 2 months running, stop trading expiry. You're donating.
