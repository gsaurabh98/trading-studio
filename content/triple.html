THREE-CANDLE PATTERNS 14 PATTERNS

Why three-candle patterns are the most reliable: Every reversal has three phases — the old trend (candle 1) → indecision and transition (candle 2) → new trend confirmation (candle 3). All three candles must be present for the pattern to be valid. This structure filters out the most false signals, which is why classic trading research (by authors like Bulkowski and Nison) ranks these patterns the highest in reliability.

How to use these patterns: Wait for the third candle to fully close. Enter on the open of the fourth candle. For bullish patterns, place your stop loss below the lowest point of the second candle. For bearish patterns, place your stop loss above the highest point of the second candle. Set your target as either the next major support or resistance level, OR a minimum risk-to-reward ratio of 1 to 2 (your reward should be at least 2 times your risk).
STAR PATTERNS (THE MOST POWERFUL REVERSALS)
MORNING STAR
VERY STRONG REVERSAL
Big red candle, then a small "star" candle, then a big green candle. The classic bottom reversal pattern. Highly reliable when it appears at a support level.
→ BUY CALL
EVENING STAR
VERY STRONG REVERSAL
Big green candle, then a small "star" candle, then a big red candle. The classic top reversal pattern. Highly reliable when it appears at a resistance level.
→ BUY PUT
MORNING DOJI STAR
STRONGEST BULLISH SIGNAL
A Morning Star with a Doji as the middle candle. The Doji shows maximum indecision right before the bullish takeover, making the reversal even stronger.
→ STRONG BUY CALL
EVENING DOJI STAR
STRONGEST BEARISH SIGNAL
An Evening Star with a Doji as the middle candle. The Doji shows maximum indecision right before the bearish takeover.
→ STRONG BUY PUT
ABANDONED BABY (BULLISH)
EXTREMELY RARE
A Doji that gaps away from BOTH neighboring candles (a gap below it AND a gap above it). The rarest and strongest bullish reversal pattern. Very uncommon in Indian markets.
→ STRONG BUY CALL
ABANDONED BABY (BEARISH)
EXTREMELY RARE
A Doji that gaps away from BOTH neighboring candles at the top. The rarest and strongest bearish reversal pattern.
→ STRONG BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Morning Star at Support (textbook bullish reversal)
Setup
A clear Morning Star pattern has formed at a known support level on the 15-minute or hourly chart. The first candle is a large red one. The second candle has a small body that gaps below (or close to) the first candle's close. The third candle is a large green one that closes above the midpoint of the first red candle. Volume on the third candle is at least 1.5 times the average of the last 10 candles.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the fourth candle, only if it opens at or above the third candle's close.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points below the lowest point of the second (star) candle on Nifty. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 35 percent.
Target
Aim for the next resistance level above. Minimum target is 2 times your stop loss distance. Morning Star reversals usually trigger 5 to 10 candle moves, so book half at the first target and trail the stop loss on the rest.
Skip if
The third candle does not close above the midpoint of the first candle (then it is not a valid Morning Star), volume on the third candle is below average, or the broader market trend is strongly downward.
SOLDIER AND CROW PATTERNS (TREND IGNITION)
THREE WHITE SOLDIERS
STRONG UPTREND
Three green candles in a row, where each candle opens inside the previous candle's body and closes higher than the previous candle. A clear sign that an uptrend has started.
→ BUY CALL
THREE BLACK CROWS
STRONG DOWNTREND
Three red candles in a row, where each candle opens inside the previous candle's body and closes lower than the previous candle. A clear sign that a downtrend has started.
→ BUY PUT
ADVANCE BLOCK
BEARISH WARNING
Three green candles in a row, but each one has a smaller body than the previous one and a longer upper wick. The uptrend is running out of steam.
→ Get ready for PUT
DELIBERATION
BEARISH WARNING
Two strong green candles followed by a tiny third green with a wick. The uptrend is stalling and a reversal is likely soon.
→ Get ready for PUT
When to take this trade — Three White Soldiers (trend ignition)
Setup
Three consecutive green candles, each closing higher than the previous one, each opening inside the previous candle's body. The candles should have similar body sizes (no shrinking, no long upper wicks — that turns into the bearish "Advance Block" pattern). Volume should be steady or rising across all three candles.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the fourth candle. If the fourth candle opens lower than the third candle's close, wait one more candle to confirm momentum.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss below the lowest point of the first soldier candle. This is the start of the new uptrend — if price falls below this, the entire setup has failed.
Target
Trend ignition patterns usually run for 5 to 15 candles. Aim for at least 3 times your stop loss distance. Book one-third at 1 times risk, one-third at 2 times risk, and trail the rest with a moving stop loss.
Skip if
The third candle has a long upper wick (this is a Deliberation or Advance Block warning), volume is shrinking instead of growing, the pattern formed against a strong daily downtrend, or the third candle closes right into a major resistance level.
INSIDE / OUTSIDE PATTERNS (REVERSALS WITH CONFIRMATION)
THREE INSIDE UP
CONFIRMED REVERSAL
A Bullish Harami (small green inside large red) followed by a third green candle that confirms the reversal. More reliable than a Harami pattern alone.
→ BUY CALL
THREE INSIDE DOWN
CONFIRMED REVERSAL
A Bearish Harami (small red inside large green) followed by a third red candle that confirms the reversal. More reliable than a Harami pattern alone.
→ BUY PUT
THREE OUTSIDE UP
VERY STRONG BULLISH
A Bullish Engulfing pattern followed by a third green candle that closes even higher. A high-conviction reversal that combines two confirmations.
→ STRONG BUY CALL
THREE OUTSIDE DOWN
VERY STRONG BEARISH
A Bearish Engulfing pattern followed by a third red candle that closes even lower. A high-conviction reversal combining two confirmations.
→ STRONG BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Three Outside Up (engulfing plus follow-through)
Setup
The first candle is red. The second candle is a green Bullish Engulfing candle that fully covers the body of the first one. The third candle is another green candle that closes higher than the second candle's close. The pattern appears at a clear support level. Volume on candles 2 and 3 is above average.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the fourth candle. Because this pattern is already very confirmed, you do not need extra waiting time.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points below the lowest point of the first red candle.
Target
Aim for the next major resistance level. Minimum target is 2.5 times your stop loss distance because this is one of the highest-quality 3-candle patterns. Book half at the first target and trail the stop loss on the rest.
Skip if
The third candle has a long upper wick (sellers were active near the highs), volume is below average on the third candle, or the pattern formed during the lunch hour (12:30 PM to 1:30 PM IST).
RARE THREE-CANDLE PATTERNS
TRI-STAR BULLISH
VERY RARE
Three Doji candles in a row, with the middle one positioned lower than the others. A rare bullish reversal pattern.
→ BUY CALL
UNIQUE THREE RIVER
RARE REVERSAL
A big red candle, then a Hammer-like red candle that makes a new low, then a tiny green candle. A rare bottom reversal pattern.
→ BUY CALL

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Morning Star and Evening Star — The textbook reversal patterns. The middle "star" candle MUST be a small body or a Doji — that small candle IS the indecision moment that signals the reversal.
  • Three White Soldiers and Three Black Crows — Pure trend ignition. Three strong candles in a row in one direction means a new trend has started. The best pattern type for riding a continuation move.
  • Three Inside Up and Three Inside Down — A Harami plus a confirmation candle. The highest reliability of all three-candle patterns because it combines two separate confirmation signals.
Why these work best on 15-minute and higher timeframes: Three candles on a 5-minute chart form in just 15 minutes — often that is just market noise, not a real reversal. Three candles on a 15-minute chart form across 45 minutes of real market activity, which gives the pattern real weight. Trade these patterns mainly on the 15-minute and hourly timeframes.
