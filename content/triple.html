THREE-CANDLE PATTERNS 14 PATTERNS

Why three-candle patterns are the most reliable: Every reversal has three phases — old trend (candle 1) → indecision / transition (candle 2) → new trend confirmation (candle 3). All three candles must be present for the pattern to qualify. This structure filters out the most false signals — which is why trading-book research (Bulkowski, Nison) ranks these highest.

How to use: Wait for candle 3 to close. Enter on candle 4's open. SL below candle 2's low (bullish patterns) or above candle 2's high (bearish). Target = next structural S/R level or 1:2 R:R minimum.

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Morning Star / Evening Star — the textbook reversal patterns. Star candle in the middle must be a small-body or doji — it IS the indecision moment.
  • Three White Soldiers / Three Black Crows — pure trend ignition. Three consecutive strong candles in one direction = new trend confirmed. Best for riding continuation.
  • Three Inside Up / Three Inside Down — engulfing + continuation. Highest reliability because it combines two confirmations.
Why these work best on 15-min and higher: Three candles on a 5-min chart form in 15 minutes — often just noise. Three candles on a 15-min chart form across 45 minutes of real market activity, giving the pattern real weight. Trade these primarily on 15-min and hourly.
