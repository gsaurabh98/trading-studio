INDIA MARKET GUIDE NIFTY / BANKNIFTY / SENSEX

What is this page about? Before any retail trader places a single trade in the morning, they should KNOW the "mood" of the Indian market — is it likely to open up, down, or sideways? Are foreign investors buying or selling? Is the fear-level high or low?

This page teaches you the 4 BIG signals you check at 9:00 AM IST every morning — BEFORE the bell rings:
· Gift Nifty (yesterday’s overnight Nifty signal from Singapore).
· India Volatility Index (the fear meter).
· Foreign and Domestic Institutional flows (who bought and sold yesterday).
· Calendar of upcoming market events (RBI policy, Budget, results, etc).

Read these 4 signals and you walk into the market with a clear plan — not random clicking.
GIFT NIFTY (formerly SGX NIFTY) — PRE-MARKET READING
WHAT IS GIFT NIFTY?
Gift Nifty (formerly known as SGX Nifty) trades BEFORE Indian markets open. It gives you a preview of where Nifty is likely to open today.

Check it at 9:00 AM IST:
Gift Nifty +100 → Nifty likely to open with a gap up → Prepare to buy a Call Option.
Gift Nifty -100 → Nifty likely to open with a gap down → Prepare to buy a Put Option.
Gift Nifty flat → Neutral opening → Wait for the Opening Range Breakout to form.
INDIA VOLATILITY INDEX — THE FEAR INDEX
The India Volatility Index measures the expected volatility of Nifty over the next 30 days.

Below 13: Low fear, market is calm. Options are cheap → good to buy.
13 to 18: Normal zone. A balanced market.
Above 20: High fear, volatile. Options are expensive → avoid buying.
Above 25: Panic zone. Huge swings. Only experts trade here.
FOREIGN AND DOMESTIC INSTITUTIONAL DATA
Foreign Institutional Investors: The big market movers. Check the daily net buying or selling on the National Stock Exchange website.

Foreign Institutions buying: A bullish bias → prefer Call Option setups.
Foreign Institutions selling: A bearish bias → prefer Put Option setups.

Domestic Institutional Investors: Often act counter to the Foreign Institutions. When BOTH are buying = the market is very bullish.
NIFTY vs BANKNIFTY vs SENSEX — WHAT TO TRADE
Major regulatory changes from 2024 to 2026: The Securities and Exchange Board of India now permits only one weekly expiry per exchange. The National Stock Exchange has kept the Nifty 50 weekly (moved from Thursday to Tuesday, effective 02 September 2025). The Bank Nifty, Fin Nifty, and MidCap Nifty weekly options were discontinued from 20 November 2024 — they are now monthly only. The Bombay Stock Exchange has kept the Sensex weekly (moved to Thursday, effective 04 September 2025). The Nifty lot size was revised to 65 from January 2026.

Verify before trading: Lot sizes, expiry days, and margin rules are revised periodically by the Securities and Exchange Board of India, the National Stock Exchange, and the Bombay Stock Exchange. Always confirm the current contract specification on the exchange website — National Stock Exchange Futures and Options Underlyings · Bombay Stock Exchange Contract Specifications.

Volatility Lower

Pattern Quality Excellent
Best For Beginners
Weekly Expiry Every Tuesday
Monthly Expiry Last Tuesday of the month
Cleaner trends, more predictable behaviour. The default instrument for intraday option buyers. Patterns work most reliably here.
BANK NIFTY (National Stock Exchange)
Lot Size 30 units
Volatility Very High
Daily Range 400 to 800 points
Pattern Quality Good (noisier)
Best For Experienced traders
Weekly Expiry Discontinued
Monthly Expiry Last Wednesday of the month
Higher swings = bigger profits, but bigger losses too. Weekly options are gone — only monthly contracts remain. Theta behaviour is different from Nifty. Master Nifty first.
SENSEX (Bombay Stock Exchange)
Lot Size 20 units
Volatility Medium
Daily Range 500 to 900 points
Pattern Quality Good
Best For All experience levels
Weekly Expiry Every Thursday
Monthly Expiry Last Thursday of the month
The only other weekly option available to retail traders after the November 2024 changes. Thursday expiry gives you an alternative Theta-decay cycle to Nifty's Tuesday. Liquidity has improved significantly in 2025 to 2026.

When to take this trade — The Pre-Market Plan from Gift Nifty + India Volatility Index reading
Setup
It is between 8:45 AM and 9:00 AM IST. You check Gift Nifty — it is up by 100 points or more. The India Volatility Index closed yesterday between 12 and 16 (so premiums are reasonable, not inflated). Foreign Institutional Investors were net buyers yesterday. There is no major event scheduled today (no Reserve Bank of India meeting, no Federal Reserve, no Union Budget).
Entry
Wait for the 9:15 AM open. Mark the high and low of the first 15-minute candle (this is your Opening Range). Buy an At The Money Call Option of the current-week expiry only when the price closes ABOVE the Opening Range high on a 5-minute candle, with volume above 1.5 times the recent average.
Stop Loss
5 points below the Opening Range low. On the option premium itself, set a hard stop at 30 percent loss.
Target
Book 50 percent of the position at the next prior swing high. Trail the rest with the 5-minute Volume Weighted Average Price line. Exit the entire position by 2:30 PM IST.
Skip if
Gift Nifty is flat (no clear direction), OR the India Volatility Index opens above 22 (the Implied Volatility crush will hurt you), OR there is a major scheduled event today (the news will override technical setups).
