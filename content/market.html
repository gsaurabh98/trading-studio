INDIA MARKET GUIDE NIFTY / BANKNIFTY / SENSEX

GIFT NIFTY / SGX NIFTY — PRE-MARKET READING
WHAT IS GIFT NIFTY?
Gift Nifty (formerly SGX Nifty) trades before Indian markets open. It gives you a preview of where Nifty is likely to open.

Check at 9:00 AM:
Gift Nifty +100 → Nifty likely to open gap up → Prepare CE
Gift Nifty -100 → Nifty likely to open gap down → Prepare PE
Gift Nifty flat → Neutral opening → Wait for ORB
INDIA VIX — FEAR INDEX
India VIX measures expected volatility of Nifty over next 30 days.

VIX < 13: Low fear, market calm. Options cheap → good to buy.
VIX 13–18: Normal zone. Balanced market.
VIX > 20: High fear, volatile. Options expensive → avoid buying.
VIX > 25: Panic zone. Huge swings. Only experts trade.
FII / DII DATA
FII (Foreign Institutions): Big market movers. Check daily net buying/selling on NSE website.

FII Buying: Bullish bias → prefer CE setups.
FII Selling: Bearish bias → prefer PE setups.

DII (Domestic): Often counter to FII. Both buying = very bullish.
NIFTY vs BANKNIFTY vs SENSEX — WHAT TO TRADE
Major 2024–26 regulatory changes: SEBI now permits only one weekly expiry per exchange. NSE retains Nifty 50 weekly (moved from Thursday to Tuesday, effective 02-Sep-2025). BankNifty, FinNifty & MidCpNifty weekly options discontinued from 20-Nov-2024 — monthly only. BSE retains Sensex weekly (moved to Thursday, effective 04-Sep-2025). Nifty lot size revised to 65 from Jan 2026.

Verify before trading: Lot sizes, expiry days, and margin rules are revised periodically by SEBI / NSE / BSE. Always confirm the current contract specification on the exchange website — NSE F&O Underlyings · BSE Contract Specs.

Lot Size 20 units

The only other weekly option available to retail after Nov-2024 changes. Thursday expiry = alternative theta-decay cycle to Nifty Tuesday. Liquidity improved significantly in 2025–26.
