ⓘ How to Read the Chart — Colour Key & Step-by-Step Workflow ▾

Think of this as a checklist, not “show everything at once”. Start with the colour key below, then toggle each layer on/off as you work through the steps.

Colour & Label Key
Supply & Demand Zones
DEMANDGreen zone — buyers previously overwhelmed sellers; price tends to bounce up from here (support).
SUPPLYRed zone — sellers previously overwhelmed buyers; price tends to drop from here (resistance).
NONEDashed card — no qualifying zone on that side; the reason it was skipped is shown inside the card.
Fair Value Gaps
FVGBullish FVG (blue) — gap left by a fast up-move; price often dips back to fill it, then continues up (support).
FVGBearish FVG (orange) — gap left by a fast down-move; price often pops back to fill it, then continues down (resistance).
Fill %How far a later wick has reached into the gap. 0% = fresh / untouched (strongest); reaching the 50% midpoint counts as fully filled; 90%+ = mitigated, so the box is dropped. A wick to the middle retires the gap — no full fill or candle close needed.
Order Blocks
BULL OBTeal zone — the last down candle before a strong move up that broke structure. Big buyers loaded here; price often bounces when it returns (a buy zone, stop just below).
BEAR OBMagenta zone — the last up candle before a strong move down that broke structure. Big sellers loaded here; price often gets rejected when it returns (a sell / take-profit zone, stop just above).
FRESH / USEDFRESH = price hasn’t come back to the zone yet (strongest). USED (Nx) = price already tapped it N time(s), so it’s weaker. If price ever closes through the zone it’s broken and dropped from view.
with trend / trend flip“with trend” = the move out of the zone continued the trend (more reliable). “trend flip” = it reversed the trend (earlier, higher risk). “+ price gap” = a Fair Value Gap inside the move adds confirmation.
Market Structure
▲ BOSBullish Break of Structure — uptrend continues (closed above a swing high).
▼ BOSBearish Break of Structure — downtrend continues (closed below a swing low).
▲ CHoCHBullish Change of Character — possible turn UP (first higher break against a downtrend).
▼ CHoCHBearish Change of Character — possible turn DOWN (first lower break against an uptrend).
HH / HLHigher High / Higher Low — building bullish structure.
LH / LLLower High / Lower Low — building bearish structure.
Liquidity Sweeps
✖Sweep High (red ✖) — price wicked above equal highs, grabbed buy-stops, then reversed DOWN (bearish bias).
✖Sweep Low (green ✖) — price wicked below equal lows, grabbed sell-stops, then reversed UP (bullish bias).
$$$Liquidity level (amber dotted line) — a cluster of equal highs/lows where stop-loss orders pool.
Step-by-Step Workflow
1
Identify the Zone ZOI + Fib — always on
Look at where the demand/supply zones sit relative to Fibonacci levels.
• A demand zone that lines up with the 61.8%–80% Fib golden pocket = high-probability buy zone.
• A supply zone near the 0%–23.6% Fib level = high-probability sell zone.
This answers: “Where should I look?”
2
Check the Trend Toggle BOS on
Turn on BOS to see if the current market structure supports your trade direction.
• Demand zone below price + trend badge says BULLISH (HH + HL pattern) = the zone is trustworthy.
• Trend is BEARISH (LH + LL pattern) + you see a demand zone = be cautious, it might break.
• A CHoCH label means the trend just flipped — this is the earliest reversal signal.
After checking, you can toggle BOS off again.
3
Refine the Entry Toggle FVG on
ZOI gives you a zone (e.g. ₹950–990), but that’s a 40-point range.
Toggle FVG on to find a precise entry — a Fair Value Gap inside the demand zone narrows it to e.g. ₹965–972.
This is your limit order level.
4
Confirm Smart Money Participation Toggle LIQ on
Before entering, check if there’s been a liquidity sweep near your zone.
• Price swept below equal lows (grabbed stops) and reversed back into the demand zone = smart money loaded their position — highest conviction signal.
The $$$ markers show you where this happened.
In short
✓ ZOI = “Where to look”
✓ BOS = “Which direction”
✓ FVG = “Exact entry price”
✓ OB = “Where big money entered”
✓ LIQ = “Confirmation”
You don’t need all four visible at once. Toggle them on/off as you work through the checklist. More checkmarks = higher probability trade.

Best Timeframes for Fib + ZOI
1W Best
Primary timeframe for swing trades (hold 2–6 weeks). Weekly demand zones are the strongest — they represent institutional accumulation over full weeks.
1D
Good for shorter swings (hold 3–10 days). More zones detected, more frequent signals, slightly less reliable than weekly.
4H
For tighter entries. Zoom into a weekly/daily demand zone, then check 4H for a smaller zone within it — this is your precision entry.
Pro tip: When weekly + daily + 4H zones all overlap at the same Fib pocket — that’s the highest conviction setup you’ll find.

    PER-TIMEFRAME BREAKDOWN

    Every indicator on every timeframe — trend, momentum, volatility, volume, structure, patterns — with a signed contribution score for context. The verdict above is driven by the four-trigger screener, not this score; this panel is here so you can audit why a setup fired.
    i HOW TO READ THIS · WHEN TO BUY
    1. What each block means
    ↗ TREND
    • Price above 20 > 50 > 200 EMA: textbook uptrend — safe to buy on dips.
    • Below 50 EMA: caution — trend is weakening.
    • Below 200 EMA: structurally broken — do not buy.
    • ADX > 25: trend is real, not chop — breakouts likely follow through.
    • ADX < 20: ranging market — breakouts often fail; wait.
    ~ MOMENTUM (RSI & MACD)
    • RSI 50–70: sweet spot — strong but not stretched.
    • RSI > 75: overbought — let it cool, do not chase.
    • RSI < 40: weak / oversold — wait for a turn.
    • MACD above signal + positive histogram: bull bias confirmed.
    • Bullish MACD cross in last 5 bars: early entry trigger.
    ⪑ VOLATILITY (ATR)
    • ATR % < 1.5%: calm stock — tighter stops OK, normal size.
    • ATR % 1.5–3%: normal — default stop = 1.5× ATR below entry.
    • ATR % > 4%: high volatility — widen stop, halve size.
    • Use ATR (not a fixed %) to set the stop — it scales with the stock's own behaviour.
    ||| VOLUME
    • Volume ≥ 1.5× 20-bar average on an up day: institutions buying — "CONFIRMING".
    • Volume < 1× average: retail-only move — "LIGHT", weaker signal.
    • Rising 5-bar volume trend: interest building — bullish backdrop.
    • Breakouts without volume rarely hold — demand confirmation.
    # STRUCTURE (levels)
    • Swing low: anchor your stop just below this level.
    • Swing high: first target (T1) — expect supply here.
    • 20-bar high/low: short-term breakout / breakdown lines.
    • 52-wk range < 25%: near support — better risk-reward to buy.
    • 52-wk range > 75%: stretched — smaller size, tighter trail.
    ◇ PATTERN (last bar)
    • Bullish hammer / engulfing / morning star: entry trigger on the next bar.
    • Bearish or none: wait — no candle confirmation yet.
    • Patterns only count at support or after a shallow pullback — never at the top of a vertical run.
    2. The four entry triggers

    On every bar the engine asks four independent questions. A stock that answers YES to any of them becomes a BUY candidate; one that lights up two or more is an A-grade confluence setup.

    ↱ PULLBACK TO 20 EMA
    • Stock in confirmed uptrend (20 > 50 EMA, rising 50)
    • Close inside the −1.5% … +2.5% band of 20 EMA
    • Bullish candle or close above 5-bar average
    ↟ 20-DAY BREAKOUT + VOLUME
    • Close above prior 20-day high (Donchian)
    • Volume ≥ 1.3× 20-day average
    • Not extended (< 10% above 50 EMA)
    ◇ REVERSAL AT SUPPORT
    • Hammer / Bullish Engulfing / Morning Star / Piercing
    • Within 3% of 50 / 200 EMA or 2% of swing low
    • Weekly not in confirmed downtrend
    ↷ AT SUPPORT (TREND PULLBACK)
    • Daily 50 EMA · Weekly 20 EMA · daily swing low
    • Each support must be rising (no falling knives)
    • Multi-support confluence = strongest signal
    3. Universal gates (applied to every trigger)
    BUY released only when
    • At least one trigger fires
    • Market regime = BULL or NEUTRAL (Nifty above rising 50 DMA, or above flat)
    • Daily ADX ≥ 18 (trending, not chop)
    • Relative Strength vs Nifty not weak (within −3 pp on the last 20 sessions) — weak-RS only blocks the continuation triggers, not the reversal ones
    WAIT when
    • No trigger fired — stock may be in a trend but no actionable entry today
    • Regime BEAR — all longs blocked until Nifty regime flips
    • ADX < 18 — choppy / ranging; even good setups fail their targets
    • Weak RS — stock dragging vs Nifty; continuation triggers dropped
    AVOID when
    • Daily AND Weekly both bearish — confirmed downtrend, cash equity = no trade
    • Daily DOWN with ADX > 25 — strong downtrend, falling knife

    Confidence bands inside BUY: LOW (one trigger, weak score), MEDIUM (one strong trigger or two stacked), HIGH (multi-trigger confluence or single trigger with strong RS tailwind).

    4. How to actually place the trade (cash delivery only)
    1. Open your broker (Zerodha / Upstox / Groww / etc).
    2. Search the stock — product type = CNC (delivery, NOT MIS / intraday).
    3. Place a LIMIT buy at the ENTRY price shown above (or slightly below if you want a better fill on a pullback).
    4. Once filled, immediately place a GTT Stop-Loss sell at the SL level shown.
    5. For T1: place another GTT sell at Target 1 for half the quantity, then trail SL to entry on the remaining half and let it run to T2.
    6. Risk only 1% of capital per trade. Position size = (1% × capital) ÷ (Entry − SL).
    7. Skip the trade if any condition in the SKIP IF list above turns true before your buy fills.

    Signal guide QUICK SCAN

    How to read the PULLBACK, WATCH, AT HIGH, TREND & other signal pills in the sector table.

    Two different layers — don’t mix them up

    SCREEN
    Signal pill in the table
    Does this look like a pullback / breakout / weak setup?
    • Computed from one quote snapshot
    • Inputs: 52-week range position + today’s %
    • Fires for every row in the table
    • Role: triage — worth looking at?
    →
    RECOMMENDATION
    BUY / WAIT below the chart
    Should I actually take this trade right now?
    • Runs only when you click a row
    • Inputs: 250+ daily candles, Monthly + Weekly + Daily
    • Gates: market regime, ADX, SMA44, RR ≥ 1.5
    • Role: trade qualifier — act on it?

    Example: A stock can show PULLBACK 72% in the screen but the recommendation says WAIT. That’s the system working — the shape is a pullback, but the multi-TF gates didn’t fire a clean trigger (e.g. no daily 20-EMA touch, or Nifty regime is NEUTRAL). The screen says “worth a look”; the recommendation says “not yet”. Both can be right.

    The pill is a triage tool, not a buy/sell call

    It scans every row in the sector table using just two inputs and classifies the shape of the price action, so you know whether it's worth clicking in for the full multi-timeframe analysis.

    • Position in 52-week range — 0% = year low, 100% = year high.
    • Today's % change — red day vs green day.

    No SMAs, MACD, volume, or indicators — those come in only when you click a row and the deep analyze() runs.

    Color = the action stance

    Green — analyze now, possibly enter
    Blue — interesting but unproven, monitor only
    Grey — no setup, sit tight
    Orange — trend breaking, avoid longs
    Red — falling-knife risk, hands off

    What the % after the label means

    It's the pill's confidence in its own classification, capped at 78% because no single quote snapshot can ever be definitive. A PULLBACK 72% does not mean "72% chance the trade works" — it means "72% sure this is structurally a pullback setup."

    The real trade-confidence number only appears after you click a row and the deep analyze() runs the monthly Weinstein stage, weekly trend, SMA 44 hard gate, ADX, ATR, S/R, etc.

    Practical workflow when you open a sector

    1. Eyes on green pills first — especially PULLBACK at 70%+. That's a strong stock retracing into its rising MAs — the cleanest swing entry there is.
    2. Grey / Blue → watchlist, don't trade yet. They're saying "no edge right now."
    3. Orange / Red → move on. Don't fight the trend; there are 600+ other stocks.
    4. Hover any pill in the table to see the per-stock reasoning in a tooltip.

    The two principles baked into the logic

    1
    Never chase a stock at its 52-week high.
    Best case it's a real breakout (high risk, needs confirmation); worst case it's the exact local top. The reward/risk is bad either way — so anything at pos ≥ 92% with momentum is capped at BREAKOUT, not STRONG BUY.
    2
    The textbook swing entry is a pullback inside an uptrend — a strong stock (70%+ of its 52w range, above its rising MAs) dipping 1%+ today. Only this combination earns the bright-green PULLBACK pill.
