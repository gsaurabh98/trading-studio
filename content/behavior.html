BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS PSYCHOLOGY FOR OPTION BUYERS

What is behavioural analysis (in simple words)? It is the study of HOW your emotions and quick mental shortcuts make you take BAD trading decisions — without you even noticing.

Technical analysis (charts and patterns) tells you WHAT to do. Behavioural analysis tells you WHY you actually do the OPPOSITE of what the chart says — you panic-exit a winner, you stubbornly hold a loser, you chase a stock after it has already moved 5 percent.

Why this matters more than any chart pattern: The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) studied Indian retail traders from 2021 to 2024. The result?
· 93 out of every 100 retail traders LOST money in Futures and Options.
· Total losses across all retail = ₹1.81 lakh crore.
· Average loss per losing trader = around ₹2 lakh.

The shocking part? The root cause is NOT bad strategy. It is psychology — revenge trading after a loss, fear of missing out on a rally, holding losers hoping they will "come back". These behaviours destroy more trading accounts than any wrong chart signal ever did.

This page teaches you to SPOT the emotion BEFORE it places the trade for you.
THE FEAR AND GREED CYCLE (HOW MARKETS REALLY MOVE)
The simple idea: Every market move is just a story of crowd emotions cycling through 5 phases. The big players (called "operators") understand this cycle and play it like a chess game. Retail traders FEEL the cycle and make the WORST decisions at the WORST moments.

The unfair pattern: Retail traders BUY during GREED (when everyone is happy) and SELL during PANIC (when everyone is scared). Operators do the EXACT opposite — they buy when nobody believes the rally is real, and sell when everyone is sure it will go higher.
1 · DISBELIEF (the bottom)
OPERATORS BUY
Nifty starts rising from a recent bottom. Newspapers and TV are still SCARED. Retail traders say "this rally will fail, it will fall again". Operators are quietly BUYING in size here.
2 · HOPE (early uptrend)
SAFE TO JOIN
The uptrend is now clear. Retail starts to notice. People become careful buyers. This is the SAFEST zone to add long trades.
3 · GREED / EUPHORIA (the top)
DANGER ZONE
Everyone is bullish. Stock tips are flying on WhatsApp. People say "it can ONLY go up from here". Operators are now quietly SELLING to retail. Book your profits.
4 · PANIC (first sharp fall)
TRAP
A sudden fall happens. Retail thinks "buy the dip!" and ADDS more. The fall keeps going. Operators are now shorting; retail is averaging down.
5 · CAPITULATION (final give-up)
OPERATORS BUY AGAIN
Retail finally gives up and sells at a huge loss. Volumes spike. People say "I will NEVER trade again". And right here, operators start buying again — the cycle restarts.
THE 9 COGNITIVE BIASES THAT KILL OPTION BUYERS
These are hard-wired mental shortcuts that our brain uses. You cannot delete them — you can only spot them, name them, and have a rule that overrides them. Every bias listed below has a fix. Read these before every trade.
1 · LOSS AVERSION
#1 KILLER
The pain of losing ₹1 is greater than the joy of making ₹1. You hold losers hoping they recover, and you cut winners early to "lock in the profit". Fix: set the stop loss and target BEFORE you enter, then obey them mechanically.
2 · CONFIRMATION BIAS
BLIND SPOT
You only read news/posts that agree with your trade. Ignored opposite signals kill you. Fix: before entering, write the one thing that would prove me wrong.
3 · RECENCY BIAS
CHART CHASE
"It went up today, it will go up tomorrow." You extrapolate the last 2 candles forever. Fix: always zoom out to the daily chart before entering.
4 · ANCHORING
ENTRY TRAP
You fixate on your entry price instead of the market's current reality. Fix: once in, the entry price is irrelevant — only level-based SL / targets matter.
5 · FEAR OF MISSING OUT
LATE-ENTRY TAX
You see a 2 percent rally and buy Out Of The Money Call Options at the top. The premium is already inflated. Fix: if the move has already happened, wait for a pullback to a key level — never chase.
6 · REVENGE TRADING
ACCOUNT KILLER
After a loss, you double the size to "win it back fast". You skip the analysis. Fix: a mandatory 30-minute cool-off period after any loss greater than 1 percent of your capital.
7 · OVERCONFIDENCE
WINNING STREAK
After 3 to 4 wins, you size up and break rules — one huge loss then wipes out all the gains. Fix: keep a fixed position size regardless of your recent profit and loss.
8 · GAMBLER'S FALLACY
FAKE PATTERN
"It fell 3 days straight, it's due for a bounce." Markets have no memory. Fix: trade setups, not hunches. "Due" is not a signal.
9 · SUNK-COST FALLACY
HOLDING LOSERS
"I've already lost ₹2,000 on this — I can't exit now." The ₹2,000 is already gone. Fix: every minute you hold is a new decision — would you ENTER this trade fresh right now? If not, exit.
THE META-RULE
BREAK THE SPELL
If you can honestly ask "am I in a biased state right now?" you've half-broken the spell. The trap is not noticing. A 10-second pause before every order click catches 80% of bias-driven trades.
MARKET-BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS YOU CAN READ
INDIA VOLATILITY INDEX
FEAR GAUGE
India Volatility Index below 12 = complacency (big moves often follow soon). India Volatility Index above 20 = fear (reversals are common here). Buying options when the India Volatility Index spikes = paying peak-fear tax.
VOLUME SPIKES
EMOTIONAL PEAK
Volume that is 2 to 3 times the average on a fall = panic selling (often near the lows). On a rally = euphoria or exhaustion (often near the highs). Fade these extremes.

RETAIL TRADER vs OPERATOR MINDSET
RETAIL MINDSET — WHAT LOSES MONEY
· Thinks in days and points
· Entries driven by Fear Of Missing Out, tips, and news
· Holds losers, books winners too early
· Averages down on a losing position
· Trades a different size based on the recent profit and loss
· Relies on hope and on "it should bounce"
OPERATOR MINDSET — WHAT MAKES MONEY
· Thinks in weeks and in liquidity
· Entries driven by levels and setups
· Cuts losers fast, lets winners run
· Exits and re-evaluates BEFORE averaging
· Fixed size regardless of any streak
· Relies on a plan and on pre-set exits
5 EMOTIONAL STATES & WHAT TO DO
EXCITED · "MUST TRADE NOW"
FEAR OF MISSING OUT
Excitement = a late-entry tax. Close the app and wait 15 minutes. If the setup is still valid, it will wait. Most setups simply disappear.
ANGRY · AFTER A LOSS
REVENGE RISK
Stop trading for the rest of the session. Write down what went wrong — level, size, rule broken. Resume only tomorrow.
ANXIOUS · POSITION OPEN
OVERSIZED
Anxiety = position too big. Your size should let you sleep. Reduce to a size you can hold calmly; then re-evaluate the trade.
CONFIDENT · AFTER WINS
OVER-SIZE RISK
The dangerous state. One big loss wipes a week of gains. Keep size identical. Don't "press" — that's how streaks end.
BORED · NO SETUP
DISCIPLINE TEST
Boredom = the mind forcing trades. No setup means no trade. Screen other instruments or close the screen for an hour.
CALM · PLAN EXECUTED
OPTIMAL STATE
Your best trades happen here. Pre-marked levels, sized right, SL & target defined. Protect this state — it is the edge.

PRE-TRADE EMOTIONAL CHECKLIST (6 ITEMS)

  • Am I calm right now? — not excited, not angry, not anxious.
  • Am I chasing? — did price already move more than 0.7 percent before I pressed buy?
  • Did I take a loss in the last hour? — if yes, wait 30 minutes.
  • Is my size consistent with my plan? — not bigger because of recent wins or confidence.
  • Do I have a written stop loss and target? — written BEFORE entering, not after.
  • If I am wrong, am I OK with the loss? — if no, the size is too big.

Any "no" = skip the trade. Most losses come from trades you knew you shouldn’t take.

When to take this trade — The "Calm State" Setup, the only state where you should click Buy
Setup
You have completed the 6-item emotional checklist above and answered "yes" to all of them. You are calm, you have not lost in the last hour, your fixed position size is calculated, and you have a written stop loss and target. The market shows a clear technical setup (a candle pattern at support / resistance with volume).
Entry
Pause for 10 seconds before clicking Buy. Re-read the "one thing that would prove me wrong" line you wrote. Place a Limit order at the planned entry price (not a Market order — Market orders are an emotional action). Use only your fixed lot size, the same size you would use after a 5-loss losing streak.
Stop Loss
Place a Stop-Loss-Market order on the broker IMMEDIATELY after the buy fills (within 30 seconds). Use the price level you wrote down BEFORE entering. Never widen the stop after entry — that is the loss-aversion bias talking.
Target
Book 50 percent of the position at the pre-written target (a level that gives you at least a 1 to 2 risk-to-reward). Trail the rest with the previous swing low (for a Call Option) or swing high (for a Put Option). When the trailing stop hits, exit silently — no "just one more candle" thinking.
Skip if
You answered "no" to ANY of the 6 emotional checklist items, OR you took a loss in the last 30 minutes (cool-off rule), OR you are feeling excited / angry / anxious / overconfident, OR you are about to size up because of a winning streak.

RECOVERY PROTOCOL AFTER A BIG LOSS

  • Close the trading terminal. Do not open another trade — the next one is almost always a revenge trade.
  • Write a post-mortem in 3 lines: what the setup was, which rule I broke, what I will do differently next time.
  • Cut your position size in half for the next trading day. Rebuild confidence on smaller wins, not on one revenge trade.
  • Take a full day off if the loss is greater than 5 percent of your capital. Trading through emotional debt only compounds it.
  • Do not tell yourself "I need to make it back". Your only job is to execute the next well-planned trade — the profit and loss is a by-product.

BEGINNER PRIORITY — BUILD THESE IN ORDER

  • Journal every trade — entry, exit, emotion, and mistake. Patterns only appear when they are written down.
  • Fix the position size first — this alone eliminates revenge trading and overconfidence.
  • One setup at a time — master one entry (for example, a hammer candle at support) before adding more setups.
  • Weekly review — which biases hit you this week? Name them. Then fix one per week.

You lose money because in the moment of decision, your emotion overrides the plan. Fix the psychology, and the same technical analysis knowledge starts printing money.
