MORE CANDLESTICK PATTERNS 16 RARE PATTERNS

These patterns appear less frequently but are statistically very reliable (per Bulkowski & Nison). When spotted at key S/R with volume, treat them as high-conviction.
HIGH-RELIABILITY RARE PATTERNS
BULLISH THREE LINE STRIKE
VERY HIGH RELIABILITY
Three consecutive red candles followed by ONE massive green that engulfs all three. Classified by Bulkowski as one of the most reliable bullish reversals (~84% success).
→ STRONG BUY CE
BEARISH THREE LINE STRIKE
VERY HIGH RELIABILITY
Three consecutive green candles followed by ONE massive red that engulfs all three. Extreme bearish reversal — major top signal.
→ STRONG BUY PE
ISLAND REVERSAL (BOTTOM)
MAJOR REVERSAL
Gap down into cluster of candles → gap up out. The "island" is isolated by two gaps in opposite directions. Rare but decisive bottom.
→ STRONG BUY CE
ISLAND REVERSAL (TOP)
MAJOR REVERSAL
Gap up into cluster → gap down out. The island at the top = distribution → reversal. Violent move down follows.
→ STRONG BUY PE
TOWER BOTTOM
BOTTOM REVERSAL
Tall red → cluster of small indecision candles → tall green. The two "towers" flank a basing phase. Clear capitulation → accumulation → breakout.
→ BUY CE
TOWER TOP
TOP REVERSAL
Tall green → cluster of indecision → tall red. Distribution between the towers — smart money selling to retail chasing highs.
→ BUY PE
FRY-PAN BOTTOM
GRADUAL REVERSAL
Slow rounded decline of small candles that gradually shift from red→doji→green. Unlike a sharp V bottom, this is patient accumulation.
→ BUY CE on breakout
DUMPLING TOP
GRADUAL REVERSAL
Inverted fry-pan. Slow rounded top with shrinking green candles → dojis → growing reds. Distribution pattern — slow but reliable top.
→ BUY PE on breakdown
BULLISH MEETING LINES
REVERSAL (WEAK)
Big red, then green opens lower but closes at exactly the same level as the red's close. Similar to piercing but only "meets" not pierces.
→ CE with confirmation
BEARISH MEETING LINES
REVERSAL (WEAK)
Big green, then red opens higher but closes at exactly the same level as the green's close. Weaker than dark cloud cover.
→ PE with confirmation
IDENTICAL THREE CROWS
STRONG DOWNTREND
Stricter version of Three Black Crows — each red opens at exactly the prior close (no gap, no upper wick). Ruthless, mechanical selling.
→ BUY PE
Trap-variant patterns (Last Engulfing Top/Bottom, Nison's traps, fakeouts) are taught in Patterns → Traps & Failures with full setups and SL rules.
TWO-BAR REVERSALS — PIPE PATTERNS
Named by Bulkowski. Two near-identical tall bars that stand out on the chart like a "pipe" sticking out of surrounding price action. Highest historical reliability of any two-bar pattern: ~77% reversal rate on weekly charts. Strongest on weekly / daily, usable on 60-min.
PIPE BOTTOM
HIGH-RELIABILITY REVERSAL
Two adjacent tall candles (one red, one green) making near-identical lows. The lows must protrude below surrounding price — they stick out like pipe legs.

Confirmation: Third-candle close above the higher of the two pipe highs. Volume on second bar ideally > first bar.

Statistics (Bulkowski): 77% reversal follow-through on weekly, ~65% on daily. Avg rally 46% in bull markets.

Target: Project pipe-height (low to highest pipe-high) upwards from breakout.
→ BUY CE on 3rd-candle close above pipe high
PIPE TOP
HIGH-RELIABILITY REVERSAL
Mirror image — two adjacent tall candles with near-identical highs that protrude above surrounding candles. The twin spikes act as exhaustion markers.

Confirmation: Third-candle close below the lower of the two pipe lows.

Statistics: ~75% reversal follow-through on weekly. Used by swing traders as a major top-marker.

Target: Pipe-height projected downwards from breakdown.
→ BUY PE on 3rd-candle close below pipe low
VOLATILITY-CONTRACTION PATTERNS — SETUP FOR BREAKOUTS
These are not directional signals — they are compression markers. They tell you "a big move is coming" but not which way. Pair them with a directional trigger (S/R break, trend) for a high-probability entry.
INSIDE BAR
CONSOLIDATION
What: A candle whose entire range (high AND low) is inside the previous candle's range. Signals temporary pause — market digesting.

Trade: Buy a break of the mother-bar high (CE) or break of mother-bar low (PE). SL = opposite side of the mother bar.

Best when: Appears at S/R, trendline, or after a strong impulse. Multiple consecutive inside bars (IB2, IB3) = extreme compression, explosive break ahead.
→ Trade the mother-bar break
NR4 (NARROW RANGE 4)
VOLATILITY CONTRACTION
What: A candle whose range (high−low) is the narrowest of the last 4 bars. Coined by Toby Crabel; extensively studied by Linda Raschke.

Why it matters: Volatility is mean-reverting — periods of contraction are nearly always followed by expansion. NR4 = "coiled spring" signal.

Trade: Place BUY-stop just above NR4 high, SELL-stop just below NR4 low (one-cancels-other). Whichever breaks first = the direction. Works on daily + 60-min.
→ OCO stops both sides of NR4
NR7 (NARROW RANGE 7)
EXTREME CONTRACTION
What: NR7 = narrowest range of the LAST 7 bars. Stricter cousin of NR4 — rarer, but when it triggers, the expansion is usually larger.

Statistics (Raschke): After NR7, next-day range expands > prior average in ~70% of cases. Break-direction follow-through: ~60% in the break direction.

Inside & NR4 / NR7 combo (ID/NR4, ID/NR7): Candle is BOTH an inside bar AND NR4/NR7. Most powerful compression setup — trade mother-bar break.
→ Wait & trade the break

USING COMPRESSION PATTERNS (INSIDE / NR4 / NR7) IN INDIAN OPTIONS

  • Best time-of-day: 11:00 AM – 1:30 PM (the "dead zone") is where compression patterns form most often. Mark them during lunch, trade the 2:00–3:00 PM expansion.
  • Best contracts: NIFTY / BANKNIFTY on 15-min and 60-min charts. Compression on 5-min is too noisy. Daily compression on F&O stocks often precedes multi-day moves.
  • Risk management: Because SL is defined (mother-bar low/high), you can size precisely with Calculator 1. Compression trades typically have TIGHT SL — you can afford more lots than usual within your risk %.
  • Avoid: Compression pattern into a MAJOR event (budget, RBI policy, US Fed). The break direction is dictated by news, not price structure — 50/50 coin-flip. Stay flat.
