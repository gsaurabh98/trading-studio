SINGLE CANDLE PATTERNS 18 PATTERNS

How to read each card below:
· Drawing = what the candle looks like on a chart
· Colored badge — green = bullish bias, red = bearish, yellow = neutral / reversal
· Description = what the candle tells you about buyer vs seller psychology
· Action line (→ BUY CE / BUY PE / WAIT) = recommended intraday option-buying response

Important for beginners: A pattern by itself is not a trade signal. Always wait for candle close, then confirm with volume, higher timeframe trend, and nearby support / resistance. Patterns at the right location = high probability. Random mid-range patterns = coin-flip.

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Bullish / Bearish Marubozu — unmistakable strong-trend candles. Easy to spot, high reliability when they close at the top / bottom of a visible range.
  • Hammer & Shooting Star — the two most reliable single-candle reversals. Hammer at support = buy the next candle open. Shooting star at resistance = buy puts.
  • Doji — the universal indecision signal. Never trade a doji on its own — it is an alert, not an entry. Wait for the next candle to break one way, then act.
Common beginner mistake: buying the MOMENT you see a pattern. The pattern must appear at a meaningful location (previous day high/low, VWAP, CPR edge, trend-line) with above-average volume. Patterns in no-man's-land mid-range are mostly noise.
