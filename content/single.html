SINGLE CANDLE PATTERNS 18 PATTERNS

How to read each card below:
· Drawing = what the candle looks like on a chart
· Coloured badge — green means bullish (price likely to go up), red means bearish (price likely to go down), yellow means neutral or reversal warning
· Description = what the candle tells you about who is winning the fight between buyers and sellers
· Action line (BUY CALL, BUY PUT, or WAIT) = the recommended intraday option-buying response

Important for beginners: A pattern by itself is not a trade signal. Always wait for the candle to fully close, then check three things: volume (was it above average?), higher timeframe trend (which direction is the daily chart moving?), and the nearest support or resistance level. A pattern that forms at the right location is high-probability. A pattern in the middle of nowhere is just a coin flip.
MOMENTUM CANDLES
BULLISH MARUBOZU
STRONG BULLISH
A full green body with no wicks at all. Buyers were in complete control for the entire candle period.
→ BUY CALL
BEARISH MARUBOZU
STRONG BEARISH
A full red body with no wicks at all. Sellers were in complete control for the entire candle period.
→ BUY PUT
OPENING MARUBOZU (BULLISH)
BULLISH
No lower wick (the candle opened at its lowest price), with a small upper wick. Buyers took control from the very first second of the candle.
→ BUY CALL
OPENING MARUBOZU (BEARISH)
BEARISH
No upper wick (the candle opened at its highest price), with a small lower wick. Sellers took control from the very first second of the candle.
→ BUY PUT
CLOSING MARUBOZU (BULLISH)
BULLISH
No upper wick (the candle closed at its highest price), with a small lower wick. Buyers won the fight by the close, regardless of how the candle started.
→ BUY CALL
CLOSING MARUBOZU (BEARISH)
BEARISH
No lower wick (the candle closed at its lowest price), with a small upper wick. Sellers won the fight by the close, regardless of how the candle started.
→ BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Bullish Marubozu
Setup
A full green Marubozu candle has just closed on your trading timeframe (5-minute or 15-minute). The candle is at least as large as the average of the last 10 candles. Volume on this candle is 1.5 times higher than the recent average. The broader trend (daily chart) is also moving up.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) at the open of the very next candle. Do not wait for a pullback; momentum candles often run hard in the same direction for the next 2 to 4 candles.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss at the low of the Marubozu candle on Nifty. For the option premium, set a hard stop at 30 to 35 percent loss of the option price.
Target
Aim for the next major resistance level above. The minimum target should be 2 times your stop loss distance. Trail your stop loss to breakeven once you are 1 times the stop loss distance in profit.
Skip if
The Marubozu forms during the lunch hour (12:30 PM to 1:30 PM IST), there is a major news event in the next hour, or the candle forms right into a strong resistance level (price might run out of room).
DOJI FAMILY
STANDARD DOJI
INDECISION
The opening price equals the closing price, with roughly equal upper and lower wicks. Perfect indecision — buyers and sellers are evenly matched.
→ WAIT
DRAGONFLY DOJI
BULLISH REVERSAL
Long lower wick with no upper wick. Buyers absorbed all the selling pressure and pushed price back up. Strong signal when this appears at a support level.
→ BUY CALL
GRAVESTONE DOJI
BEARISH REVERSAL
Long upper wick with no lower wick. Sellers rejected the highs and pushed price back down. Strong signal when this appears at a resistance level.
→ BUY PUT
LONG-LEGGED DOJI
EXTREME INDECISION
Very long wicks on both sides. There was massive intraday volatility but no clear winner. The market is confused.
→ WAIT
FOUR-PRICE DOJI
NO MOVEMENT
The open, high, low, and close are all the same price. Zero movement. Usually seen in illiquid stocks or when the market is halted.
→ AVOID
RICKSHAW MAN
MAXIMUM INDECISION
A Doji with equal long wicks and the body sitting exactly in the middle. The highest level of uncertainty in candle patterns.
→ WAIT
When to take this trade — Dragonfly Doji at Support
Setup
A Dragonfly Doji has formed exactly at a known support level (such as the previous day's low, the 20 period Exponential Moving Average, or a Volume Weighted Average Price). The lower wick is very long and the body is tiny at the top.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) only after you see a green confirmation candle close above the high of the Dragonfly Doji. Do NOT enter on the Doji candle itself.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss below the lowest point (the bottom tip of the lower wick) of the Dragonfly Doji. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 40 percent of the option price.
Target
Aim for the next resistance level above. Minimum target is 2 times your stop loss distance. The Dragonfly often signals a multi-candle reversal, so book half the position at the first target and trail the stop on the rest.
Skip if
The Doji forms in the middle of nowhere (not at a known support level), the daily trend is strongly downward, or the confirmation candle never appears within 1 to 2 candles after the Doji.

When to take this trade — Hammer at Support (the most reliable single candle)
Setup
A Hammer candle has formed at a known support level on the 15-minute or hourly chart. The lower wick is at least 2 times the size of the body. The body sits in the upper third of the candle. Volume on the Hammer candle is above the average of the last 10 candles.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) when the very next candle opens above the Hammer's high. The next candle must be green; if it opens lower or red, skip the trade entirely.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points below the bottom tip of the Hammer's lower wick on Nifty. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 40 percent of the option price.
Target
Aim for the next resistance level above. Minimum target is 2 times the size of the Hammer candle (measured from low to high). Book half at the first target and trail the stop on the rest of the position.
Skip if
The Hammer forms in the middle of nowhere (no clear support nearby), the broader market trend is strongly downward, the next candle does not confirm with a green close above the Hammer's high, or the Hammer formed during the lunch hour.
When to take this trade — Shooting Star at Resistance
Setup
A Shooting Star candle has formed at a known resistance level. The upper wick is at least 2 times the size of the body. The body sits at the bottom of the candle. Volume is above average on the Shooting Star candle.
Entry
Buy a Put Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) when the next candle opens below the Shooting Star's low. The next candle should be red. If it opens higher or green, skip the trade.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points above the top tip of the Shooting Star's upper wick. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 40 percent.
Target
Aim for the next support level below. Minimum target is 2 times the size of the Shooting Star candle. Book half at the first target and trail the stop on the rest.
Skip if
The Shooting Star forms in the middle of nowhere, the broader trend is strongly upward, the next candle does not confirm with a red close, or the formation happens during the lunch hour.
INDECISION CANDLES
SPINNING TOP
INDECISION
A small body with roughly equal wicks on both sides. Neither buyers nor sellers were able to dominate.
→ WAIT
HIGH WAVE
HIGH VOLATILITY
A tiny body with extremely long wicks on both sides. A wild, directionless session with high emotion and no clear winner.
→ AVOID
SHORT CANDLE
LOW VOLATILITY
A short body with short wicks. Indicates low volume sideways consolidation or a quiet choppy market.
→ WAIT

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Bullish and Bearish Marubozu — Unmistakable strong-trend candles. Easy to spot, and very reliable when they close at the top or bottom of a visible range.
  • Hammer and Shooting Star — The two most reliable single-candle reversals. A Hammer at support means you can buy the next candle's open with a Call Option. A Shooting Star at resistance means you can buy a Put Option.
  • Doji — The universal indecision signal. Never trade a Doji on its own — it is a warning to pay attention, not an entry signal. Wait for the next candle to break in one direction, then take action.
Common beginner mistake: Buying the MOMENT you see a pattern. The pattern must appear at a meaningful location (previous day high or low, Volume Weighted Average Price line, Central Pivot Range edge, or trendline) with above-average volume. Patterns that form in no-man's-land in the middle of the range are mostly noise and should be ignored.
