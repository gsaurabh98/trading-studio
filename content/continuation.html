CONTINUATION PATTERNS 12 PATTERNS

Continuation versus Reversal — the most important difference:
A reversal pattern says "the trend is about to change direction." A continuation pattern says "the trend is just taking a breath, then continuing in the same direction." Mistaking one for the other is the number-one cause of stop losses being hit.

How to tell them apart:
· Continuation patterns appear inside an existing trend (where higher highs and higher lows for an uptrend, or lower highs and lower lows for a downtrend, are still being made).
· Reversal patterns appear at the end of a trend (after an extended move, usually with fading volume).
· If you are unsure, check the higher timeframe chart. If the larger trend is still intact, treat the current pattern as a continuation.

Why these patterns matter for option buyers: Continuation patterns are where you ADD to a winning position or re-enter after the previous wave booked profit. They also signal "the trend has more energy left in it" — which produces the best risk-to-reward trades of the day.
BULLISH CONTINUATION PATTERNS
RISING THREE METHODS
BULLISH CONTINUATION
A big green candle, then 3 small red candles staying within the green candle's range, then another big green breakout candle. The classic Bull Flag pattern.
→ BUY CALL on 5th candle
MAT HOLD (BULLISH)
BULLISH CONTINUATION
Like the Rising Three Methods, but the second red candle gaps up. Very bullish — the trend will resume strongly.
→ BUY CALL
BULLISH TASUKI GAP
BULLISH CONTINUATION
Two green candles with a gap between them, then a red candle that fails to close that gap. This means the buyers are still in control.
→ BUY CALL
SIDE-BY-SIDE WHITE LINES (BULLISH)
BULLISH CONTINUATION
A green candle, then two more similar green candles side by side at the same price level. Brief consolidation, then the move continues higher.
→ BUY CALL
BULLISH SEPARATING LINES
BULLISH CONTINUATION
A red candle, then a green candle that opens at the SAME price as the red candle's open. The uptrend resumes with force.
→ BUY CALL
GAP UP (CONTINUATION)
BULLISH CONTINUATION
A gap up that happens during an existing uptrend signals acceleration. Do not try to fade (bet against) the gap — ride it instead.
→ BUY CALL
When to take this trade — Rising Three Methods (the textbook bull flag)
Setup
The pattern forms during an existing uptrend (visible higher highs and higher lows on the chart). The first big green candle has volume above average. The 3 small red pullback candles stay within the range of the first green candle (the deepest low does not break the green candle's low). Volume on the pullback candles is BELOW the average (showing healthy consolidation, not real selling).
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) when the fifth candle (a new big green) closes above the high of the first green candle, with volume at least 1.5 times the recent average.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss below the lowest point of the 3-candle pullback. If price falls below this level, the pullback has turned into a real reversal and your trade is wrong.
Target
Use the height of the first green candle as your minimum target, projected from the breakout point. For example, if the first green candle was 80 points tall and the breakout happened at 23,500, your target is 23,580 (80 points up). Book half there and trail the stop on the rest.
Skip if
The pullback breaks below the first green candle's low (then it is a reversal, not a continuation), volume is rising on the red pullback candles (showing real selling pressure), or the pattern forms against a strong daily downtrend.
BEARISH CONTINUATION PATTERNS
FALLING THREE METHODS
BEARISH CONTINUATION
A big red candle, then 3 small green candles within its range, then another big red breakdown candle. The classic Bear Flag pattern.
→ BUY PUT on 5th candle
BEARISH TASUKI GAP
BEARISH CONTINUATION
Two red candles with a gap between them, then a green candle that fails to close that gap. The sellers are still in control.
→ BUY PUT
SIDE-BY-SIDE BLACK LINES
BEARISH CONTINUATION
A red candle, then two more similar red candles side by side at the same price level. The downtrend will resume.
→ BUY PUT
BEARISH SEPARATING LINES
BEARISH CONTINUATION
A green candle, then a red candle that opens at the SAME price as the green candle's open. The downtrend resumes with force.
→ BUY PUT
GAP DOWN (CONTINUATION)
BEARISH CONTINUATION
A gap down that happens during an existing downtrend signals acceleration. Do not try to catch the falling knife (do not buy a Call here).
→ BUY PUT
MAT HOLD (BEARISH)
BEARISH CONTINUATION
The bearish version of Mat Hold. Three small green candles fail to reverse the downtrend, then a big red candle resumes the move down.
→ BUY PUT

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Rising and Falling Three Methods — The textbook continuation pattern. Trend candle, then small counter-trend pullback (3 candles), then the trend continues. Ideal for options — enter on the breakout candle that exits the pullback range.
  • Flag and Pennant patterns — A mini-consolidation after a sharp move. The tighter the pullback, the stronger the continuation will be.
  • Separating Lines — A gap in the trend direction followed by a same-direction candle. Easy to identify and clean to trade.
Rule of thumb: The pullback inside a continuation pattern should NOT exceed 50 percent of the previous trending move. If it does, the pattern is broken — the trend is likely ending, not pausing.
