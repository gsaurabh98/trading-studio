MOMENTUM & VOLATILITY 4 CONFIRMATION TOOLS

Momentum & volatility indicators answer "how fast, and how far?" They are confirmation tools — never standalone signals. RSI & MACD gauge trend strength and divergence; Bollinger Bands & ATR measure volatility for stop sizing and breakout readiness. The rule: entries come from price + trend; momentum confirms; volatility sizes the trade.

RSI (RELATIVE STRENGTH INDEX)
MOMENTUM OSCILLATOR
WHY IT MATTERS: Measures speed and strength of price moves. Extreme readings hint at exhaustion; divergence against price warns of weakening trends before price reverses.

HOW TO READ:
• > 70 = overbought (expect pullback, not crash)
• < 30 = oversold (expect bounce)
• Cross of 50 line = trend bias shift
• Divergence (price higher-high, RSI lower-high) = strongest reversal warning

BUY CE WHEN: RSI bullish divergence at support + bullish candle (engulfing / hammer) + RSI crosses back above 40 + volume.
BUY PE WHEN: RSI bearish divergence at R1/R2 + bearish candle + RSI crosses below 60 + volume.

SL: Beyond the divergence's swing high/low.
TARGET: Nearest S/R; 1:2 RR minimum.
TIMEFRAME: 5-min (period 9) intraday · 15-min / 1-hr (period 14) swing.
AVOID: Strong trends — RSI can stay overbought/oversold for hours; never counter-trade a trending market on RSI alone.
→ Divergence + candle + 50-line cross
MACD
TREND + MOMENTUM
WHY IT MATTERS: Combines direction (line crosses) with strength (histogram). One of the cleanest trend-confirmation tools — rarely wrong when trend + histogram + price align.

HOW TO READ:
• MACD line above signal = bullish momentum
• MACD line below signal = bearish momentum
• Histogram expanding = trend strengthening · shrinking = trend weakening
• Zero-line cross = structural trend shift
• Default: 12, 26, 9

BUY CE WHEN: MACD crosses above signal + histogram turning green & expanding + price above VWAP + bullish candle.
BUY PE WHEN: MACD crosses below signal + histogram red & expanding + price below VWAP + bearish candle.

SL: Below/above the crossover candle's low/high.
TARGET: Nearest S/R; exit on opposite MACD cross or histogram flip.
TIMEFRAME: 15-min / 1-hour intraday · daily for swing. Lags on 1–3 min.
AVOID: Ranging markets (false crosses) · low-volume sessions (lunch hour).
→ Cross + histogram expand + candle
BOLLINGER BANDS
VOLATILITY + MEAN REV
WHY IT MATTERS: Visualises volatility. Bands expand when volatility rises, contract when it collapses — a squeeze almost always precedes a big directional move.

HOW TO READ:
• 20-SMA ± 2 standard deviations
• Band squeeze → volatility collapse → explosive move incoming
• Band walk (price hugs upper/lower band) = strong trend, don't fade
• Touch of band in a range = mean-reversion opportunity

BUY CE WHEN:
• Breakout: After squeeze, price breaks above upper band with volume AND closes above mid-band
• Mean-reversion (range day): Bullish rejection candle at lower band
BUY PE WHEN:
• Breakout: After squeeze, price breaks below lower band with volume + close below mid-band
• Mean-reversion: Bearish rejection at upper band in a range

SL: Opposite band (mean-reversion); opposite side of breakout candle (breakout).
TARGET: Opposite band (mean-reversion) or 1:2 RR (breakout).
TIMEFRAME: 15-min intraday · 1-hr / daily for swing.
AVOID: Post-news gap days (bands distort) · never short into a band walk.
→ Squeeze break + volume + close beyond mid
ATR (AVERAGE TRUE RANGE)
STOP-LOSS SIZING
WHY IT MATTERS: Quantifies volatility in points. The only mathematically sound way to set stop-loss distance — removes the guesswork that kills most retail accounts (fixed 10-pt SLs on a 120-pt ATR day get stopped out in 30 seconds).

HOW TO READ:
• ATR rising = bigger candles, wider stops needed
• ATR falling = market compressed, breakout brewing
• Not a signal tool — it is a sizing tool

STOP-LOSS RULE:
• Intraday SL = 1.5 × ATR from entry
• Swing SL = 2–3 × ATR
TARGET RULE:
• First target = 2 × ATR
• Second target = 3 × ATR (1:2 / 1:3 RR)

TIMEFRAME: Match the trade horizon — 5-min ATR for intraday, daily ATR for swing.
AVOID: Confusing ATR trend with price trend — ATR tells you about range, not direction.
→ Use for SL & target distances, not entries
