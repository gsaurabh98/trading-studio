MOMENTUM & VOLATILITY 4 CONFIRMATION TOOLS

What are momentum and volatility indicators?

· Momentum indicators = tools that tell you HOW FAST price is moving. They are like a speedometer in your car — they tell you if the trend is gaining speed or running out of energy.
· Volatility indicators = tools that tell you HOW FAR price typically moves. They are like a weather forecast — they tell you if the day is calm or stormy.

Important warning: Never enter a trade JUST because a momentum indicator gives a signal. They are CONFIRMATION tools, not entry signals. Always start with price action and trend, then use these to CONFIRM.

The 4 tools every trader should know:
· Relative Strength Index (RSI) — measures if the market is overbought (too high, ready to fall) or oversold (too low, ready to bounce).
· Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) — shows trend strength and warns when a trend is about to reverse.
· Bollinger Bands — show how far price has moved from average. Price tends to come back to the middle.
· Average True Range (ATR) — tells you how big a typical move is. Used to set stop loss size correctly.

Simple rule: Price action and trend tell you WHEN to enter. Momentum CONFIRMS the signal. Volatility tells you HOW BIG to size the trade.

RELATIVE STRENGTH INDEX
MOMENTUM OSCILLATOR
WHY IT MATTERS: Measures the speed and strength of price moves. Extreme readings hint at exhaustion. Divergence against price (when price makes a new high but the indicator does not) warns of a weakening trend before price actually reverses.

HOW TO READ:
• Above 70 means overbought (expect a pullback, not necessarily a crash).
• Below 30 means oversold (expect a bounce).
• A cross of the 50 line indicates a shift in trend bias.
• Divergence (price makes a higher high, but the Relative Strength Index makes a lower high) is the strongest reversal warning available.

BUY CALL WHEN: A bullish divergence forms at a support level, AND a bullish candle (Bullish Engulfing or Hammer), AND the Relative Strength Index crosses back above 40, AND volume confirms.
BUY PUT WHEN: A bearish divergence forms at Resistance 1 or Resistance 2, AND a bearish candle, AND the Relative Strength Index crosses below 60, AND volume confirms.

STOP LOSS: Beyond the swing high or low that created the divergence.
TARGET: The nearest support or resistance. Minimum 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 5-minute chart with period 9 for intraday. 15-minute or 1-hour chart with period 14 for swing trades.
AVOID: Strong trending markets — the Relative Strength Index can stay overbought or oversold for hours. Never trade against a trending market using only the Relative Strength Index.
→ Divergence + candle + 50-line cross
MOVING AVERAGE CONVERGENCE DIVERGENCE
TREND + MOMENTUM
WHY IT MATTERS: Combines direction (line crosses) with strength (the histogram bars). One of the cleanest trend-confirmation tools you can use — rarely wrong when the trend, the histogram, and the price all agree.

HOW TO READ:
• The Moving Average Convergence Divergence line above the signal line means bullish momentum.
• The Moving Average Convergence Divergence line below the signal line means bearish momentum.
• Histogram bars expanding mean the trend is strengthening. Histogram bars shrinking mean the trend is weakening.
• A cross of the zero line indicates a structural trend shift.
• Default settings: 12, 26, 9.

BUY CALL WHEN: The Moving Average Convergence Divergence line crosses above the signal line, AND the histogram bars turn green and start expanding, AND price is above the Volume Weighted Average Price line, AND a bullish candle confirms.
BUY PUT WHEN: The Moving Average Convergence Divergence line crosses below the signal line, AND the histogram bars turn red and start expanding, AND price is below the Volume Weighted Average Price line, AND a bearish candle confirms.

STOP LOSS: Just below or above the crossover candle's low or high.
TARGET: The nearest support or resistance. Exit on an opposite crossover or a histogram colour flip.
TIMEFRAME: 15-minute or 1-hour for intraday. Daily for swing trades. Lags too much on 1 to 3 minute charts.
AVOID: Range-bound markets (you get false crossovers). Also avoid low-volume sessions like the lunch hour.
→ Cross + histogram expand + candle
BOLLINGER BANDS
VOLATILITY + MEAN REVERSION
WHY IT MATTERS: Visualises volatility. The bands expand when volatility rises and contract when it collapses. A squeeze (when bands tighten significantly) almost always comes before a big directional move.

HOW TO READ:
• The bands are calculated as 20 period Simple Moving Average plus or minus 2 standard deviations.
• A band squeeze means volatility has collapsed, and an explosive move is coming.
• A band walk (price hugging the upper or lower band) signals a strong trend. Do not bet against a band walk.
• A touch of the band during a range-bound day is a mean-reversion opportunity (price will likely move back to the middle).

BUY CALL WHEN:
• Breakout play: After a squeeze, price breaks above the upper band with strong volume AND closes above the middle band.
• Mean-reversion play (range day only): A bullish rejection candle forms at the lower band.
BUY PUT WHEN:
• Breakout play: After a squeeze, price breaks below the lower band with strong volume AND closes below the middle band.
• Mean-reversion play: A bearish rejection candle at the upper band during a range.

STOP LOSS: The opposite band (for mean-reversion plays); the opposite side of the breakout candle (for breakout plays).
TARGET: The opposite band (mean-reversion) or 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio (breakout).
TIMEFRAME: 15-minute for intraday. 1-hour or daily for swing trades.
AVOID: Days that opened with a news-driven gap (the bands get distorted). Never short into a band walk.
→ Squeeze break + volume + close beyond mid
AVERAGE TRUE RANGE
STOP LOSS SIZING TOOL
WHY IT MATTERS: Measures volatility in points. This is the only mathematically sound way to set stop loss distance. It removes the guesswork that kills most retail trading accounts (using a fixed 10-point stop loss on a day with 120-point Average True Range will get you stopped out in 30 seconds).

HOW TO READ:
• Average True Range rising means bigger candles, so wider stops are needed.
• Average True Range falling means the market is compressed, and a breakout is brewing.
• This is NOT a signal tool — it is a position sizing tool.

STOP LOSS RULE:
• Intraday Stop Loss = 1.5 times Average True Range from your entry price.
• Swing Stop Loss = 2 to 3 times Average True Range.
TARGET RULE:
• First target = 2 times Average True Range.
• Second target = 3 times Average True Range (1 to 2 or 1 to 3 risk-to-reward).

TIMEFRAME: Match it to your trade horizon. Use the 5-minute chart's Average True Range for intraday trades, daily Average True Range for swing trades.
AVOID: Confusing the Average True Range trend with the price trend. Average True Range tells you about range, not about direction.
→ Use for SL & target distances, not entries
When to take this trade — Bollinger Band Squeeze Breakout (volatility expansion)
Setup
The Bollinger Bands have been narrowing on the 15-minute chart for the past 6 to 10 candles (the bands look pinched together). The Average True Range reading is at its lowest level of the past 5 trading days. Volume has been dropping (the calm before the storm). India Volatility Index is between 12 and 16.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) when a 15-minute candle closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band with volume at least 2 times the recent average. For a downward break, buy a Put Option when a candle closes below the lower band.
Stop Loss
The middle Bollinger Band line. If price closes back inside the bands, the breakout has failed. For the option premium, set a hard stop at 30 to 40 percent loss.
Target
Aim for the option premium to double (a 100 percent gain). Volatility breakout trades often produce 100 to 200 percent option moves in just 1 to 3 candles, because both the price move AND the rise in Implied Volatility help your position.
Skip if
The squeeze is happening close to a major event (Reserve Bank of India meeting, Federal Reserve announcement, results day) where the breakout direction can flip after the news, or the squeeze happens during the lunch hour (12:00 PM to 1:30 PM IST).
