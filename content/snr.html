SUPPORT & RESISTANCE FOUNDATION

WHAT IS SUPPORT & RESISTANCE
SUPPORT LEVEL

A price level where buyers consistently step in and push price UP. The more times price bounces from it, the stronger the support.

RESISTANCE LEVEL

A price level where sellers consistently step in and push price DOWN. The more times price is rejected, the stronger the resistance.

HOW TO IDENTIFY KEY LEVELS
SWING HIGHS & LOWS
Swing Highs = Resistance. Swing Lows = Support. Connect the peaks and troughs on your chart.
ROUND NUMBERS
Nifty 26000, 26500, BankNifty 57000, 58000, Sensex 85000. Round numbers act as psychological S&R. Always mark these.
PREVIOUS DAY HIGH/LOW
Previous Day High (PDH) and Low (PDL) are the most important intraday levels. Mark these before market opens every day.
OPENING RANGE (ORB)
First 15-min candle range = Opening Range. Break above = Buy CE. Break below = Buy PE. High-probability setup.
MOVING AVERAGES AS S&R
20 EMA = dynamic support in uptrend. 50 EMA = stronger support. Candle pattern at EMA = high probability trade.
VOLUME PROFILE / POC
Point of Control (POC) = price where most volume traded. Strong S&R. Candle reversal at POC = very high probability.
SUPPORT BECOMES RESISTANCE (ROLE REVERSAL)

When price breaks below support, that level flips and becomes resistance. Price often returns to test the broken level from below — this is your PE entry point. Same works in reverse for resistance becoming support after a breakout → CE entry.

FINDING THE BEST TIMEFRAME FOR S&R
A key rule: Higher timeframe S/R is stronger than lower timeframe S/R. A level visible on the daily chart will hold better than one visible only on the 5-min chart. Always mark levels top-down — start from the highest TF and work your way to the lowest.
WEEKLY / MONTHLY CHART
LONGEST-TERM S/R
What to mark: All-time highs, 52-week highs/lows, major quarterly swing points, psychological round numbers (26000, 27000, 50000 etc.).

Why it matters: These levels are watched by institutions and FIIs. They rarely fail on first test — 80%+ reaction rate. Drawn once a month.
DAILY CHART
MASTER TIMEFRAME
What to mark: Last 10–15 swing highs & lows, last monthly high/low, open-of-month level, and the most obvious horizontal levels where price has bounced/reversed 2+ times.

Why it matters: This is the MOST IMPORTANT timeframe for intraday option buyers. Daily S/R acts as a magnet for intraday price action. Mark these every night before the next trading day.
60-MIN (HOURLY) CHART
INTRADAY TREND
What to mark: Last 5–7 swing highs & lows of the last 5 trading days. Previous Day High / Low / Close (PDH, PDL, PDC).

Why it matters: Connects daily levels to intraday action. Great for identifying trend direction + where the day's range should end.
15-MIN CHART
SIGNAL TIMEFRAME
What to mark: Today's Opening Range (ORB — first 15-min candle), VWAP, 20 EMA, day's developing high/low.

Why it matters: Where candle patterns form for your actual trade signal. S/R here must align with a higher-TF level to be tradeable.
5-MIN & BELOW
AVOID FOR S/R
Do NOT draw levels from these. 5-min swings are noise — too many fake S/R. They'll confuse you and make every candle look like a "reversal at support".

Correct use: Only for entry fine-tuning AFTER the 15-min + 60-min + daily levels have given you a signal.
CONFLUENCE = A+ SETUPS
GOAL
The magic number is 3: a price level that's a key S/R on 3 different timeframes (e.g. weekly support + daily support + 60-min swing low) is nearly unbreakable on first test.

This is called confluence. Only trade options at confluence levels — rest are noise.

THE TOP-DOWN S/R WORKFLOW (DO THIS EVERY NIGHT, 10 MIN)

  • Step 1 — Weekly chart (1 min): Zoom out. Mark 2–3 horizontal lines at major multi-month highs/lows. Use one colour (e.g. gold).
  • Step 2 — Daily chart (4 min): Mark last 10 swing highs/lows. Mark PDH, PDL, PDC. Mark monthly open. Use a different colour (e.g. orange).
  • Step 3 — 60-min chart (3 min): Mark last 5 swing highs/lows of the past week. Confirm these don't duplicate your daily marks — if they do, make the line thicker (confluence).
  • Step 4 — 15-min chart (2 min): Tomorrow morning, wait for the 9:15–9:30 ORB candle. Add its high and low as TODAY-ONLY intraday levels.
  • Only trade when: Price approaches a level that shows up on 2+ timeframes. Candle signal + level + volume = A+ trade. One timeframe alone = skip.
COMMON MISTAKES WHEN DRAWING S/R
TOO MANY LINES
More lines ≠ more accuracy. If every candle touches a "support", you've drawn too many. Rule: If you can't explain a line in one sentence, delete it. Target: 4–6 lines per timeframe max.
OBSESSING OVER EXACT PRICE
S/R is a zone, not a pixel-perfect line. Draw a faint 10–20 pt band around the level (for Nifty). Price can overshoot by 0.2–0.3% and still respect the zone.
USING ONLY ONE TIMEFRAME
Drawing S/R on 5-min only = noise trading. Drawing only on daily = missing intraday precision. You need at least 2 timeframes marked.
IGNORING OLD LEVELS
A level from 3 months ago is STILL relevant — the market has memory. Don't erase a level just because price went past it; flip it to the opposite role (support → resistance).
