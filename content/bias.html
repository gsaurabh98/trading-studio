BIAS CALCULATOR CE / PE / NO-TRADE

A bias calculator is a 30-second checklist you run before the market opens. You feed it yesterday's Nifty High, Low, and Close prices plus the current spot price, and it applies all the rules from this app (the Central Pivot Range shape and width, where the price sits, India Volatility Index, Put-Call Ratio, and the higher-timeframe trend) to give you one clear answer: STRONG CALL · CALL BIAS · NO TRADE · PUT BIAS · STRONG PUT.
WHAT IT IS
A rules engine, not a predictor. The same Central Pivot Range, Volatility Index, Put-Call Ratio, and trend logic you have already learned, automated into one click.
WHEN TO USE IT
At 9:00 AM every day, before the market opens. Sets your trading bias for the day in 30 seconds. Check it again intraday whenever the spot price moves significantly.
WHAT IT IS NOT
It is not a buy or sell signal. It is not advice. It is not based on live data. Always wait for a candle trigger at the Top Central, Bottom Central, Volume Weighted Average Price, or Fibonacci confluence levels before taking any trade.
When to take this trade — Using the Bias Calculator Output
Setup
You have run the bias calculator at 9:00 AM with yesterday's data and current spot price. The output shows either STRONG CALL or STRONG PUT (not the weaker BIAS levels). The market has opened and the first 15-minute candle has formed.
Entry
For STRONG CALL bias: Wait for price to pull back to a Central Pivot Range level (Top Central, Bottom Central, or a pivot like Resistance 1) and form a green reversal candle. Buy an At the Money Call Option at that point. For STRONG PUT bias: Wait for price to rally up to a pivot level and form a red reversal candle, then buy an At the Money Put Option.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss on the wrong side of the pivot level by 10 to 15 points on Nifty. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 40 percent.
Target
Aim for the next pivot level in your bias direction. Minimum target is 2 times your stop loss distance. Book half there and trail the stop on the rest.
Skip if
The calculator shows NO TRADE or only weak BIAS, intraday news has flipped the sentiment (bias from 9:00 AM is now stale), or the spot price is right in the middle of the Central Pivot Range with no candle trigger yet.

How the score is calculated:
• Central Pivot Range shape (plus or minus 1): Normal gives +1, Inverted gives -1
• Price vs Central Pivot Range band (plus or minus 2): Above gives +2, Inside gives 0, Below gives -2
• Central Pivot Range width (plus or minus 1): Narrow boosts the existing bias, Wide softens it
• India Volatility Index (plus or minus 1): Below 13 boosts the trend, above 20 gives -1 caution
• Put-Call Ratio (plus or minus 1): Above 1.2 gives +1 (Call bias), below 0.8 gives -1 (Put bias), 0.8 to 1.2 is neutral
• Higher timeframe trend (plus or minus 2): Strong Up gives +2, Strong Down gives -2
• Final buckets: 4 or more = STRONG CALL / +1 to +3 = CALL BIAS / 0 = NO TRADE / -1 to -3 = PUT BIAS / -4 or less = STRONG PUT
