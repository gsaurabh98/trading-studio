SCALPING FAST TRADES · 5–30 MIN HOLD · ELITE ONLY

Honest truth first. Scalping is the highest-skill, lowest-forgiveness style in retail options. SEBI data shows ~93% of individual F&O traders lose money, and scalpers sit at the sharpest end of this curve — not because the setups fail, but because brokerage, slippage, bid-ask spread, and emotional errors eat the thin edge. Read the full section before you attempt a single scalp. If you cannot profit on a swing setup, you will not profit scalping.
WHAT IS SCALPING
HOLD TIME
Typically 30 seconds to 15 minutes, rarely beyond 30 min. Exit the moment the setup resolves — win or lose. Overstaying is not scalping.
PROFIT TARGET
Small, fast wins — 10–30 points on the index or 5–15% on premium. Many small scalps stacked across the day beat one big swing.
INSTRUMENT
ATM or ATM±1 strikes only. Delta >0.4 for responsive premium. OTM options are too sluggish; deep ITM are illiquid and costly.
WIN-RATE GOAL
Target 55–65% win-rate with 1:1.5 R:R. Frequency matters more than size. 5–10 clean trades a day > 1 hero trade.

IS SCALPING RIGHT FOR YOU?
YES — YOU CAN SCALP IF
You already profit on swing trades • you have 6+ hours of screen time daily • your broker charges flat ₹20/order • you can exit a losing trade in 30 seconds without hesitation • you have journaled 100+ trades.
MAYBE — PROCEED CAREFULLY
You have a stable intraday process but want faster feedback • you've broken even for 3+ months • you have a second monitor and stable internet • you can accept that 40% of trades will be losers and not tilt.
NO — DON'T SCALP IF
You're new to options • you trade from a phone • your broker charges percentage brokerage • you widen stops when losing • you revenge-trade • you take 5+ seconds to place an order • you're in drawdown.

TOP 5 SCALPING SETUPS (INDIAN INDEX OPTIONS)
1 • OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT (ORB) SCALP
When: 9:30–10:30 AM • Setup: mark first 15-min high/low; break + close above/below with volume • Entry: ATM CE on ORH break / ATM PE on ORL break • SL: premium drops 15% or price re-enters range • Target: 1:2 or opposite ORB level • Win-rate: 55–60%.
2 • VWAP BOUNCE SCALP
When: price trending away from VWAP then returns • Setup: wait for rejection candle (pin bar / engulfing) at VWAP • Entry: ATM CE on bullish rejection / ATM PE on bearish rejection • SL: below/above rejection candle low/high • Target: prior swing high/low • Edge: works best in trending days.
3 • CPR / PIVOT FLIP SCALP
When: price reclaims or loses a major pivot (TC/BC/R1/S1) • Setup: confirm retest of the flipped level • Entry: ATM option in direction of flip • SL: 15 pts below/above pivot • Target: next pivot • Note: narrow CPR days favor this; wide CPR = skip.
4 • 5-MIN EMA PULLBACK SCALP
When: strong trend day • Setup: price pulls back to 20 EMA on 5-min + bullish/bearish engulfing • Entry: ATM option in trend direction • SL: other side of engulfing candle • Target: 1:2 minimum • Best in: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM window.
5 • EXPIRY MOMENTUM SCALP
When: Tue (Nifty) / Thu (Sensex), 9:45–11:30 only • Setup: clean directional candle with volume after ORB • Entry: ATM in breakout direction • SL: 20–30 pts on index / 30% on premium • Target: 1:2 then scratch • Stop trading by noon. See EXPIRY DAY tab.
6 • SUPERTREND FLIP SCALP
When: 5-min Supertrend (10,3) flips • Setup: confirm with volume spike and price closing beyond Supertrend line • Entry: ATM option aligned with flip • SL: opposite side of Supertrend • Target: 1:1.5 • Avoid: choppy range days — Supertrend whipsaws badly.
ENTRY MECHANICS
STRIKE SELECTION
ATM or ATM ±1 strike. Delta 0.45–0.60. Bid-ask spread must be ≤ ₹1 for Nifty, ≤ ₹2 for BankNifty.
TIMING WINDOWS
Best: 9:30–11:30 AM and 2:00–3:00 PM. Avoid 12:00–1:30 (lunch chop) and last 15 min of expiry.
ORDER TYPE
Use limit orders at bid+tick (buy) or ask-tick (sell). Market orders burn 1–2 ticks = ₹65–130 instant loss.
CONFIRMATION
Need 2 of 3: price action (candle close beyond level) + volume (1.5× avg) + direction (VWAP / EMA slope). Never trade on one signal.
EXIT MECHANICS — THE 3-LAYER STOP
Stop Type Trigger When to Use
Premium SL Premium drops 15–25% Fastest, most mechanical. Always active. Hard-coded in the broker.
Price SL Index moves 20–30 pts against Use when underlying structure is clear (breakout zone, pivot).
Time SL No movement in 10–15 min Theta eats flat positions. Scratch and redeploy capital elsewhere.
Target 1 (50%) +15–20% on premium Book half, trail rest to breakeven. Locks in a win.
Target 2 (remainder) +30–50% on premium or trail Let winner run if momentum continues. Exit on first weak candle.
Whichever stop triggers first, exit. Never rationalise holding past any of the three. Mechanical exits are the ONLY thing separating profitable scalpers from donors.

TOP 8 SCALPING MISTAKES
CHASING PRICE
Entering 2–3 candles after the signal means paying the top of the move. Let price come to you or skip the setup.
REVENGE TRADING
After a loss, firing a fresh trade to "get it back" is emotional — not strategic. It's the fastest way to turn 1 loss into 4.
IGNORING COSTS
Not tracking brokerage + STT + slippage means your "₹200 profit" is actually ₹50. Reconcile P&L vs broker statement weekly.
SCALPING OTM OPTIONS
OTMs have low delta and wide spreads — you fight theta AND slippage. ATM only for scalping. Always.
HOLDING LOSERS
"Let me give it 5 more minutes" = the scalper's ruin. A losing scalp past 15 minutes is no longer a scalp. Exit and redeploy.
TRADING LUNCH HOURS
12:00–1:30 PM is dead-zone chop with low volume. False breakouts everywhere. Stay flat or book a loss for the day.
OVERSIZING
Jumping from 1 lot to 3 lots after 2 wins is ego, not strategy. Scale up only after 30-trade samples of consistent P&L.
MOBILE SCALPING
Phone apps have 1–3 sec latency + clumsy order placement. One frozen screen = blown account. Desktop + stable internet is non-negotiable.
DAILY SCALPING ROUTINE
PRE-MARKET (8:45 – 9:15 AM)
1. Check global markets (SGX Nifty, Dow futures) • 2. Read India VIX — plan size • 3. Mark CPR / pivots / previous day high-low • 4. Identify Max OI CE + PE strikes • 5. Check economic calendar (RBI, Fed, results)
OPENING (9:15 – 9:30 AM)
No trades. Observe. Mark opening range high/low. Let the initial spike + retrace happen. Identify day-type (gap-up, gap-down, flat). Don't fight the first 15 minutes.
TRADING (9:30 AM – 3:00 PM)
Execute only A+ setups. Follow 3-layer stops. Track each trade in journal. Stop on 3% daily loss OR 3 losses in a row OR 10 trades — whichever hits first.
POST-MARKET (3:30 – 4:00 PM)
Review every trade — was the setup valid? Was the exit mechanical? Was size correct? Log win-rate, avg R, and cost ratio. Journal one-line notes on what to fix tomorrow.
BEGINNER PRIORITY PATH
If you are under 12 months in options, do NOT scalp live. Follow this ladder — each step must be cleared before the next:
Step What to Do Move on When…
1 • Swing intraday Take 1–2 intraday swing trades a day with structure SL (15-min chart). Profitable for 3 consecutive months.
2 • Paper-scalp Paper-trade ORB & VWAP-bounce scalps on ATM options. Track every cost. Net positive on 100+ paper scalps with realistic costs.
3 • Micro-live Trade 1 lot Nifty, max 3 scalps/day. Strict 1% risk + 3-loss stop. Profitable for 2 months with ≥ 55% win-rate.
4 • Scale size Increase to 2–3 lots and 6–8 trades/day. Keep all risk rules intact. Consistent P&L across different market regimes (trending, range, expiry).
5 • Full scalper Full-time, multi-setup, 5–10 scalps/day. Journaled, rule-bound, boring. You never "arrive" — keep iterating the playbook.

THE SCALPER'S CHECKLIST — BEFORE EVERY TRADE

  • Is this an A+ setup I have journaled wins on? If unsure, skip.
  • Am I trading ATM or ATM ±1 with spread ≤ ₹1 (Nifty)?
  • Is risk ≤ 1% of capital on this trade?
  • Is my 3-layer stop (premium + price + time) pre-defined?
  • Am I in the right trading window (9:30–11:30 or 2:00–3:00)?
  • Have I taken fewer than 3 consecutive losses today?
  • Is my daily loss < 3% and trade count < 10?
  • Am I emotionally calm — not tilted, rushed, or revenging?

If any answer is NO, don't click. The market opens again tomorrow.
