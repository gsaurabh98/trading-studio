SCALPING FAST TRADES · 5–30 MIN HOLD · ELITE ONLY

The honest truth first. Scalping is the highest-skill, least-forgiving style of options trading. Data from the Securities and Exchange Board of India shows that about 93 percent of individual Futures and Options traders lose money, and scalpers sit at the sharpest end of this curve. The setups themselves work fine — the problem is that brokerage fees, slippage (the gap between the price you wanted and the price you actually got), the bid-ask spread, and emotional mistakes eat the thin edge. Read this entire section before you attempt even one scalp trade. If you cannot make money on slower swing trades, you will not make money scalping.
WHAT IS SCALPING
HOLD TIME
Usually 30 seconds to 15 minutes, very rarely beyond 30 minutes. Exit the moment the setup either works or fails — win or lose. Holding longer than planned is not scalping anymore.
PROFIT TARGET
Small, fast wins — about 10 to 30 points on the index or 5 to 15 percent on the option premium. Many small scalp wins stacked through the day beat one big swing trade.
INSTRUMENT
Only At the Money or one strike either side of At the Money. The Delta should be greater than 0.4 so the option premium responds quickly to price moves. Out of the Money options are too slow to move; deep In the Money options are illiquid and cost too much.
WIN-RATE GOAL
Aim for 55 to 65 percent winners with a 1 to 1.5 risk-to-reward ratio (your reward should be 1.5 times your risk). Frequency matters more than size. 5 to 10 clean scalp trades a day is better than one big hero trade.

IS SCALPING RIGHT FOR YOU?
YES — YOU CAN TRY SCALPING IF
You already make money on swing trades, you have at least 6 hours of screen time daily, your broker charges a flat ₹20 per order, you can exit a losing trade in 30 seconds without hesitation, and you have logged 100 or more trades in a journal already.
MAYBE — BE VERY CAREFUL IF
You have a stable intraday trading process but want faster feedback, you have broken even or done slightly better for at least 3 months, you have a second monitor and stable internet, and you can accept that 40 percent of trades will be losers without getting emotionally tilted.
NO — DO NOT SCALP IF
You are new to options, you trade from a phone, your broker charges percentage-based brokerage, you tend to widen your stop losses when you are losing, you take revenge trades, you take more than 5 seconds to place an order, or you are currently in a losing streak (drawdown).

TOP 6 SCALPING SETUPS (INDIAN INDEX OPTIONS)
1 · OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT SCALP
When: Between 9:30 AM and 10:30 AM · Setup: Mark the high and low of the first 15-minute candle (this is the Opening Range). Wait for a candle to close above the Opening Range High or below the Opening Range Low with volume above average. Entry: Buy an At the Money Call Option on a break above the high, or At the Money Put Option on a break below the low. Stop Loss: Exit if the option premium drops 15 percent or if price re-enters the original range. Target: 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio, or the opposite side of the Opening Range. Win-rate: 55 to 60 percent.
2 · VOLUME WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE BOUNCE SCALP
When: Price has been trending away from the Volume Weighted Average Price line, then comes back to touch it. Setup: Wait for a rejection candle (such as a Pin Bar or Engulfing pattern) right at the Volume Weighted Average Price line. Entry: Buy an At the Money Call Option on a bullish rejection (above the line), or At the Money Put Option on a bearish rejection (below the line). Stop Loss: Just below the rejection candle's low (for bullish) or above the rejection candle's high (for bearish). Target: The previous swing high or swing low. Best in: Strong trending days.
3 · CENTRAL PIVOT RANGE FLIP SCALP
When: Price has reclaimed or lost a major pivot level (Top Central, Bottom Central, Resistance 1, or Support 1). Setup: Confirm the move by waiting for a retest of the flipped level. Entry: Buy an At the Money option in the direction of the flip. Stop Loss: 15 points below or above the pivot level. Target: The next pivot level. Note: Days with a narrow Central Pivot Range favor this setup. On days with a wide Central Pivot Range, skip this setup entirely.
4 · 5-MINUTE EXPONENTIAL MOVING AVERAGE PULLBACK SCALP
When: A strong trending day. Setup: Price pulls back to the 20 period Exponential Moving Average on the 5-minute chart, AND a Bullish or Bearish Engulfing pattern forms there. Entry: Buy an At the Money option in the direction of the trend. Stop Loss: The other side of the engulfing candle. Target: Minimum 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio. Best time window: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
5 · EXPIRY MOMENTUM SCALP
When: Tuesday for Nifty or Thursday for Sensex expiry, only between 9:45 AM and 11:30 AM. Setup: A clean directional candle with strong volume after the Opening Range. Entry: At the Money option in the breakout direction. Stop Loss: 20 to 30 points on the index, or 30 percent loss on the option premium. Target: 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio, then exit at breakeven. Stop trading by noon. See the EXPIRY DAY tab for the full playbook.
6 · SUPERTREND FLIP SCALP
When: The 5-minute Supertrend (with settings 10 and 3) flips direction. Setup: Confirm the flip with a volume spike AND price closing beyond the Supertrend line. Entry: At the Money option aligned with the new flip direction. Stop Loss: Opposite side of the Supertrend line. Target: 1 to 1.5 risk-to-reward ratio. Avoid: Choppy range-bound days — Supertrend gives many fake signals (whipsaws) on those days.
When to take this trade — Opening Range Breakout Scalp (the easiest one)
Setup
The market has opened. The first 15-minute candle (9:15 AM to 9:30 AM) has closed. You have marked its high and low on your chart. India Volatility Index is between 12 and 20 (avoid days with extreme volatility). It is currently between 9:30 AM and 10:30 AM (the best time window for this setup).
Entry
Buy an At the Money Call Option (or one strike Out of the Money) when a 5-minute candle closes above the Opening Range High with volume at least 1.5 times the average. For a downward break, buy an At the Money Put Option when a candle closes below the Opening Range Low. Use a limit order at the bid price plus one tick — do not use a market order, you will lose ₹75 to ₹150 in slippage.
Stop Loss
Two stops, whichever hits first: (1) Exit if the option premium drops 15 percent from your entry price. (2) Exit if the index price re-enters the original Opening Range (this means the breakout was fake).
Target
Book half the position at 15 to 20 percent profit on the premium. Trail the stop loss to breakeven on the other half. Final exit at 30 to 50 percent profit on the premium, OR when the opposite side of the Opening Range is reached, OR on the first weak candle.
Skip if
Volume on the breakout candle is below average, the breakout happens during the lunch hour (12:00 PM to 1:30 PM), the bid-ask spread is wider than ₹1 on Nifty, India Volatility Index is above 22 (too dangerous) or below 11 (too slow), or it is the last 15 minutes of expiry day.
ENTRY MECHANICS
STRIKE SELECTION
At the Money or one strike either side of At the Money. Delta should be between 0.45 and 0.60. The bid-ask spread must be 1 rupee or less for Nifty, and 2 rupees or less for BankNifty.
TIMING WINDOWS
Best windows: 9:30 AM to 11:30 AM and 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Avoid 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM (lunch hour chop) and the last 15 minutes of expiry day.
ORDER TYPE
Always use limit orders at bid plus one tick (when buying) or ask minus one tick (when selling). Market orders burn 1 to 2 ticks of slippage, which equals ₹75 to ₹150 instant loss.
CONFIRMATION
Need 2 out of 3 confirmations: price action (candle closes beyond a key level), volume (1.5 times the recent average), and direction (Volume Weighted Average Price slope or Exponential Moving Average slope). Never trade based on a single signal.
EXIT MECHANICS — THE 3-LAYER STOP LOSS
Stop Type Trigger When to Use
Premium Stop Loss Option premium drops 15 to 25 percent The fastest, most mechanical stop. Always active. Hard-coded in your broker.
Price Stop Loss Index moves 20 to 30 points against you Use when the underlying chart structure is clear (breakout zone or pivot level).
Time Stop Loss No movement for 10 to 15 minutes Time decay (Theta) eats flat positions. Exit and redeploy capital elsewhere.
Target 1 (book 50 percent) Plus 15 to 20 percent on the premium Book half the position, trail the rest to breakeven. This locks in a winning trade.
Target 2 (the rest) Plus 30 to 50 percent on the premium, or trail Let the winner run if momentum continues. Exit on the first weak candle.
Whichever stop triggers first, exit immediately. Never make excuses to hold past any of the three stops. Mechanical exits are the ONLY thing that separates profitable scalpers from people who donate money to the broker.

TOP 8 SCALPING MISTAKES
CHASING PRICE
Entering 2 to 3 candles after the signal means buying at the top of the move. Let price come to you, or skip the setup.
REVENGE TRADING
After a loss, taking a fresh trade just to "get the money back" is emotional, not strategic. It is the fastest way to turn 1 loss into 4.
IGNORING COSTS
Not tracking brokerage, Securities Transaction Tax, and slippage means your "₹200 profit" is actually only ₹50. Reconcile your profit and loss with the broker statement every week.
SCALPING OUT OF THE MONEY OPTIONS
Out of the Money options have low Delta (slow movement) and wide bid-ask spreads — you fight time decay (Theta) AND slippage. Always use At the Money options for scalping. Always.
HOLDING LOSING TRADES
"Let me give it 5 more minutes" is the scalper's ruin. A losing scalp held past 15 minutes is no longer a scalp trade. Exit and redeploy your capital.
TRADING DURING LUNCH HOURS
12:00 PM to 1:30 PM is the dead zone — choppy with low volume. Fake breakouts everywhere. Stay flat or you will end the day with a loss.
OVERSIZING THE POSITION
Jumping from 1 lot to 3 lots after just 2 winning trades is ego, not strategy. Only scale up after 30 trades of consistent profit and loss results.
SCALPING ON A MOBILE PHONE
Phone apps have 1 to 3 second latency and clumsy order placement. One frozen screen equals a blown account. A desktop computer with stable internet is non-negotiable.
DAILY SCALPING ROUTINE
PRE-MARKET (8:45 AM to 9:15 AM)
1. Check global markets (Singapore Exchange Nifty, Dow futures). 2. Read the India Volatility Index reading and plan your position size. 3. Mark the Central Pivot Range, pivot levels, and previous day's high and low on your chart. 4. Identify the strikes with the highest Call and Put Open Interest. 5. Check the economic calendar (Reserve Bank of India meetings, Federal Reserve announcements, company results).
OPENING (9:15 AM to 9:30 AM)
Take no trades. Just observe. Mark the high and low of the opening range. Let the initial spike and pullback happen. Identify the day-type (gap up, gap down, or flat opening). Do not fight the first 15 minutes — it is too random.
TRADING SESSION (9:30 AM to 3:00 PM)
Only execute top-quality setups. Follow your 3-layer stop loss rules. Track every single trade in your journal. Stop trading after 3 percent daily loss, OR 3 losses in a row, OR 10 trades total — whichever happens first.
POST-MARKET (3:30 PM to 4:00 PM)
Review every single trade. Was the setup actually valid? Was the exit mechanical? Was the position size correct? Log your win-rate, average risk-to-reward ratio, and your cost-to-profit ratio. Write one-line notes on what to fix tomorrow.
BEGINNER PRIORITY PATH
If you have less than 12 months of options trading experience, do NOT scalp with real money. Follow this step-by-step ladder — each step must be cleared before moving to the next:
Step What to Do Move on When…
1 · Swing intraday trades Take 1 to 2 intraday swing trades a day with a structure-based stop loss on the 15-minute chart. Profitable for 3 months in a row.
2 · Paper-trade scalps Paper-trade Opening Range Breakout and Volume Weighted Average Price bounce scalps using At the Money options. Track every cost. Net positive on 100+ paper-traded scalps with realistic costs included.
3 · Micro-live trading Trade 1 lot of Nifty, maximum 3 scalp trades per day. Strict 1 percent risk per trade and 3-loss daily stop. Profitable for 2 months with at least 55 percent win-rate.
4 · Scale up size Increase to 2 to 3 lots and 6 to 8 trades per day. Keep all the risk rules intact. Consistent profit and loss across different market conditions (trending, range-bound, expiry).
5 · Full scalper Full-time, multi-setup, 5 to 10 scalps per day. Journaled, rule-bound, and boring. You never truly "arrive" — keep iterating the playbook forever.

THE SCALPER'S CHECKLIST — BEFORE EVERY TRADE

  • ✓ Is this a top-quality setup that I have logged winning trades on before? If unsure, skip the trade.
  • ✓ Am I trading At the Money or one strike either side of At the Money, with a bid-ask spread of 1 rupee or less on Nifty?
  • ✓ Is my risk on this trade 1 percent or less of my total capital?
  • ✓ Have I pre-defined my 3-layer stop loss (premium, price, and time)?
  • ✓ Am I in the right trading window (9:30 AM to 11:30 AM, or 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM)?
  • ✓ Have I had fewer than 3 consecutive losses today?
  • ✓ Is my daily loss less than 3 percent and my trade count less than 10?
  • ✓ Am I emotionally calm — not tilted, rushed, or trying to take revenge?

If any answer is NO, do not click the button. The market will open again tomorrow.
