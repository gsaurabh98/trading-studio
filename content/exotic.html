EXOTIC & ADVANCED PATTERNS 10 PATTERNS

These patterns appear infrequently on intraday charts. When spotted with volume confirmation at key levels, treat them as high-conviction signals. Always wait for candle close before entering.
Why "exotic" ≠ "complicated": These patterns are rare because the specific sequence of candles needed to form them is uncommon — not because they are hard to read. Once you know the shape, you will spot them instantly. The key skill is patience: don't force-fit a pattern. If it doesn't match the template exactly, skip it.

Why exotic patterns have the highest reliability: The market rarely prints these by accident. When they do form, they signal that a large institutional player has taken a clear side — which is why their resolution rates (per Bulkowski) often cross 75–85%.
GAP-DRIVEN REVERSALS (HIGHEST CONVICTION)
Why these are the "nuclear" reversal signals: A kicker pattern requires a gap AGAINST the prior candle's direction — institutions do not open gap positions by accident. When you see a kicker at a major S/R with volume, it is one of the highest-conviction signals in all of candlestick trading.
BULLISH KICKER
MOST POWERFUL BULL
Bearish candle → gap up → Bullish candle. Complete sentiment reversal. Enter on open of 2nd candle.
→ STRONG BUY CE
BEARISH KICKER
MOST POWERFUL BEAR
Bullish candle → gap down → Bearish candle. Complete sentiment reversal. Violent move follows.
→ STRONG BUY PE
MATCHING HIGH (BEAR TRAP)
BEARISH REVERSAL
Two green candles with identical highs. Resistance double-tested at same level — reversal warning.
→ BUY PE
BREAKOUT / BREAKDOWN MOMENTUM
The cleanest trend-starting candles. These are not subtle — a single large candle closing decisively through a key level with volume. Perfect for beginners because there is nothing to interpret: price broke the level, or it didn't.
BREAKOUT CANDLE
BULL BREAKOUT
Large green candle closing above key resistance with volume. Strong CE entry.
→ BUY CE
BREAKDOWN CANDLE
BEAR BREAKDOWN
Large red candle closing below key support with volume. Strong PE entry.
→ BUY PE
COUNTERATTACK REVERSALS (SAME CLOSE FIGHT-BACK)
The "fight-back" pattern. Second candle opens strongly against the first but closes right at the first candle's close — like two armies meeting at the same frontline. Signals a transfer of momentum. More reliable than simple engulfing when they appear at major S/R.
BULLISH COUNTERATTACK
BULLISH REVERSAL
Red candle → Green candle opening lower but closing at same close as red. Bulls fighting back.
→ BUY CE
BEARISH COUNTERATTACK
BEARISH REVERSAL
Green candle → Red candle opening higher but closing at same close as green. Bears fighting back.
→ BUY PE
