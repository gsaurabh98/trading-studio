EXOTIC & ADVANCED PATTERNS 10 PATTERNS

These patterns appear infrequently on intraday charts. When you spot them with volume confirmation at key levels, treat them as high-conviction signals. Always wait for the candle to fully close before entering a trade.
Why "exotic" does not mean "complicated": These patterns are rare because the specific sequence of candles needed to form them does not happen often — not because they are hard to read. Once you know the shape, you will spot them instantly. The real skill needed is patience: do not force-fit a pattern. If it does not match the template exactly, skip it.

Why exotic patterns have the highest reliability: The market rarely prints these by accident. When they DO form, they signal that a large institutional player has taken a clear side in the market. This is why their resolution rates (according to the trading author Bulkowski) often cross 75 to 85 percent.
GAP-DRIVEN REVERSALS (HIGHEST CONVICTION)
Why these are the "nuclear" reversal signals: A Kicker pattern requires a gap that moves AGAINST the previous candle's direction. Institutions do not create such gaps by accident. When you see a Kicker pattern at a major support or resistance level WITH volume, it is one of the highest-conviction signals in all of candlestick trading.
BULLISH KICKER
MOST POWERFUL BULLISH
A bearish candle, then a gap up, then a strong bullish candle. A complete reversal of market sentiment. Enter on the open of the second candle.
→ STRONG BUY CALL
BEARISH KICKER
MOST POWERFUL BEARISH
A bullish candle, then a gap down, then a strong bearish candle. A complete reversal of market sentiment. Violent move usually follows.
→ STRONG BUY PUT
MATCHING HIGH (BEAR TRAP)
BEARISH REVERSAL
Two green candles with the exact same high price. The resistance has been tested twice at the same level — this is a reversal warning.
→ BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Bullish Kicker at Support (highest conviction signal)
Setup
The first candle is a strong bearish candle (preferably with a long body and minimal wicks). The next candle gaps UP and is a strong bullish candle. There is a clear visible gap between the two candle bodies. The pattern forms at or near a known support level. Volume on the second candle is at least 2 times the recent average.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the OPEN of the second candle. This is one of the few patterns where you do not wait for confirmation — the gap and reversal IS the confirmation. Use a market order; the slippage cost is worth it for this quality of setup.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss at the low of the second (green) candle. If price drops back to fill the gap, the entire reversal has failed and your trade is wrong.
Target
Aim for at least 3 times your stop loss distance. Kicker patterns usually trigger multi-day moves. If the pattern formed on a daily chart, consider holding for 3 to 5 days. Book one-third at 1 times risk, one-third at 2 times risk, and trail the stop on the rest.
Skip if
The gap closes within the next 30 minutes (a failed Kicker), the second candle has long wicks (showing sellers were still active), volume is below average on the second candle, or the pattern is in the middle of nowhere with no clear support.
BREAKOUT AND BREAKDOWN MOMENTUM
The cleanest trend-starting candles. These are not subtle — just one large candle closing decisively through a key level with strong volume. Perfect for beginners because there is nothing to interpret: either price broke the level, or it did not.
BREAKOUT CANDLE
BULLISH BREAKOUT
A large green candle that closes above a key resistance level with strong volume. A strong Call Option entry signal.
→ BUY CALL
BREAKDOWN CANDLE
BEARISH BREAKDOWN
A large red candle that closes below a key support level with strong volume. A strong Put Option entry signal.
→ BUY PUT
COUNTERATTACK REVERSALS (SAME-CLOSE FIGHT-BACK)
The "fight-back" pattern. The second candle opens strongly against the first, but closes at exactly the same closing price as the first — like two armies meeting at the same frontline. This signals a transfer of momentum from one side to the other. More reliable than a simple Engulfing pattern when they appear at major support or resistance.
BULLISH COUNTERATTACK
BULLISH REVERSAL
A red candle, then a green candle that opens lower (gap down) but closes at the SAME closing price as the red candle. The buyers are fighting back hard.
→ BUY CALL
BEARISH COUNTERATTACK
BEARISH REVERSAL
A green candle, then a red candle that opens higher (gap up) but closes at the SAME closing price as the green candle. The sellers are fighting back hard.
→ BUY PUT
