TREND INDICATORS 3 CORE TOOLS

Trend indicators answer "which way, and how strong?" They are the primary directional filter — if you trade against a clear trend, you are fighting the big money on the other side. Use VWAP for intraday bias, EMA structure for trend strength, Supertrend for clean flip signals.

HOW TO READ EACH INDICATOR CARD BELOW

  • WHY IT MATTERS — what edge it gives and why professionals use it
  • HOW TO READ — the 2–3 signals you actually watch for
  • BUY CE WHEN / BUY PE WHEN — the exact trigger conditions for a trade
  • SL / TARGET — where the stop and profit levels go
  • TIMEFRAME — where the indicator works best
  • AVOID — the market condition that breaks the signal
VWAP (VOLUME-WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE)
MOST IMPORTANT INTRADAY
WHY IT MATTERS: Institutional fair-price benchmark — mutual funds, FIIs and algos execute large orders around VWAP. Trading against it means trading against the day's smart-money flow.

HOW TO READ:
• Price above VWAP → buyers control the day
• Price below VWAP → sellers control the day
• VWAP slope = trend direction · distance from VWAP = overextension

BUY CE WHEN: Price pulls back down to a rising VWAP + bullish rejection candle (hammer / bullish engulfing) + volume spike on the rejection bar.
BUY PE WHEN: Price rallies up to a falling VWAP + bearish rejection candle (shooting star / bearish engulfing) + volume spike.

SL: 10–15 pts beyond VWAP on other side (Nifty); scale with ATR for BankNifty.
TARGET: Nearest pivot level (R1/S1) or prior session high/low; 1:2 RR minimum.
TIMEFRAME: 5-min only (VWAP resets daily).
AVOID: Flat / sideways VWAP days (no edge) · first 15 min (VWAP unstable).
→ Pullback to VWAP + candle + volume
20 / 50 / 200 EMA
DYNAMIC S/R
WHY IT MATTERS: Smooths noise, reveals the real trend. Alignment of EMAs (stacking) tells you whether the trend is strong or weakening.

HOW TO READ:
• 20 EMA = short-term pulse (scalping S/R)
• 50 EMA = intraday swing trend
• 200 EMA = major regime filter
• Stack 20 > 50 > 200 = strong uptrend · reverse = strong downtrend

BUY CE WHEN: Price pulls back to rising 20 EMA on 5-min (or 50 EMA on 15-min) + bullish engulfing / hammer + stack order 20 > 50 > 200.
BUY PE WHEN: Price rallies to falling 20 EMA + bearish candle + stack 20 < 50 < 200.

SL: 10–15 pts beyond the EMA used as entry.
TARGET: Prior swing high/low; 1:2 RR minimum.
TIMEFRAME: 5-min (20 EMA) scalping · 15-min (50 EMA) intraday · daily (200 EMA) regime.
AVOID: Choppy days where EMAs are flat & intertwined · news gaps through the EMAs.
→ Pullback to aligned EMA + candle
SUPERTREND
TREND FOLLOWING
WHY IT MATTERS: The simplest visual trend filter in existence — one line, one colour, one rule: green = long only, red = short only. Removes bias ambiguity.

HOW TO READ:
• Line below price, green → uptrend active
• Line above price, red → downtrend active
• Colour flip = structural trend change
• Default: 10 period, 3 multiplier

BUY CE WHEN: Supertrend flips red → green + the next candle closes above the flip candle's high + volume spike. 15-min chart.
BUY PE WHEN: Supertrend flips green → red + next candle closes below the flip candle's low + volume.

SL: Opposite side of the Supertrend line at entry (auto-trails).
TARGET: Trail with Supertrend — exit only on colour flip back.
TIMEFRAME: 15-min / 1-hour. Avoid < 5-min (whippy).
AVOID: Ranging / choppy markets (constant whipsaws). Check CPR width first — wide CPR = skip Supertrend signals.
→ Wait for flip + next-candle close + volume
