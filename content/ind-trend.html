TREND INDICATORS 3 CORE TOOLS

What is a trend indicator? A trend indicator is a small mathematical tool that you add to your chart to answer TWO simple questions:
· Which DIRECTION is price moving (up, down, or sideways)?
· How STRONGLY is it moving?

Why this matters: Trading AGAINST a strong trend means fighting the big-money players. You will lose. Trading WITH the trend means the big players are on your side. You win more often.

The 3 trend tools every Indian trader should know:
· Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) — the "fair price line" that institutions use for intraday trades.
· Exponential Moving Averages (EMAs) — smoothed price lines that show if the trend is strong or weak.
· Supertrend indicator — a coloured line that flips green / red when the trend changes.

Use them in this order: VWAP for direction, EMAs for strength, Supertrend for trend flips.

VOLUME WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE
MOST IMPORTANT INTRADAY TOOL
WHY IT MATTERS: The Volume Weighted Average Price is the "fair price" benchmark used by big institutions. Mutual funds, Foreign Institutional Investors, and trading algorithms execute their large orders around this line. Trading against it means trading against the day's smart-money flow.

HOW TO READ:
• Price above the Volume Weighted Average Price line means buyers are in control for the day.
• Price below the Volume Weighted Average Price line means sellers are in control.
• The slope of the line tells you the direction of the trend. The distance of price from the line tells you whether the move is overextended.

BUY CALL WHEN: Price pulls back DOWN to a rising Volume Weighted Average Price line, AND a bullish rejection candle forms (Hammer or Bullish Engulfing), AND there is a volume spike on that rejection candle.
BUY PUT WHEN: Price rallies UP to a falling Volume Weighted Average Price line, AND a bearish rejection candle forms (Shooting Star or Bearish Engulfing), AND there is a volume spike on that rejection candle.

STOP LOSS: 10 to 15 points beyond the Volume Weighted Average Price line on the wrong side, for Nifty. Scale this with the Average True Range for BankNifty.
TARGET: Nearest pivot level (Resistance 1 or Support 1) or the previous session's high or low. Minimum 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 5-minute chart only (the line resets at the start of every trading day).
AVOID: Flat, sideways days when the Volume Weighted Average Price line itself is flat (no edge to trade). Also avoid the first 15 minutes of the day (the line is unstable then).
→ Pullback to VWAP + candle + volume
20 / 50 / 200 PERIOD EXPONENTIAL MOVING AVERAGES
MOVING SUPPORT & RESISTANCE
WHY IT MATTERS: Exponential Moving Averages smooth out the price noise and reveal the real underlying trend. The way the moving averages are stacked (their order top to bottom) tells you whether the trend is strong or weakening.

HOW TO READ:
• The 20 period Exponential Moving Average shows the short-term pulse (use it for scalping support and resistance).
• The 50 period Exponential Moving Average shows the intraday swing trend.
• The 200 period Exponential Moving Average is the major regime filter.
• If the 20 is above the 50 which is above the 200, you have a strong uptrend. The reverse order means a strong downtrend.

BUY CALL WHEN: Price pulls back to a rising 20 period Exponential Moving Average on the 5-minute chart (or the rising 50 period on the 15-minute chart), AND a Bullish Engulfing or Hammer candle forms there, AND the moving average stack order is 20 above 50 above 200.
BUY PUT WHEN: Price rallies up to a falling 20 period Exponential Moving Average, AND a bearish candle forms there, AND the stack order is 20 below 50 below 200.

STOP LOSS: 10 to 15 points beyond the moving average you used as your entry trigger.
TARGET: The previous swing high or swing low. Minimum 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 5-minute chart for 20 period scalping. 15-minute chart for 50 period intraday. Daily chart for 200 period regime check.
AVOID: Choppy, sideways days where the moving averages are flat and tangled together. Also avoid news gaps that jump straight through the moving averages.
→ Pullback to aligned EMA + candle
SUPERTREND
TREND FOLLOWING
WHY IT MATTERS: The simplest visual trend filter ever invented. One line, one colour, one rule: when the line is green, only take long trades; when the line is red, only take short trades. Removes any uncertainty in your bias.

HOW TO READ:
• The line is below the price and green: an uptrend is active.
• The line is above the price and red: a downtrend is active.
• A colour flip means a structural change in the trend.
• Default settings: 10 periods, 3 multiplier.

BUY CALL WHEN: Supertrend flips from red to green, AND the very next candle closes above the high of the flip candle, AND there is a volume spike. Use the 15-minute chart.
BUY PUT WHEN: Supertrend flips from green to red, AND the next candle closes below the low of the flip candle, AND there is a volume spike.

STOP LOSS: The opposite side of the Supertrend line at the time of entry (this trails automatically as price moves).
TARGET: Trail your stop with the Supertrend line and exit only when the colour flips back.
TIMEFRAME: 15-minute or 1-hour chart. Avoid timeframes less than 5-minute (too many false signals).
AVOID: Range-bound or choppy markets (Supertrend gives constant whipsaw signals). Check the Central Pivot Range width first — if it is wide, skip Supertrend signals for the day.
→ Wait for flip + next-candle close + volume
When to take this trade — VWAP Pullback (the most important intraday setup)
Setup
It is between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM (the best window). The 5-minute chart shows price has been moving in one direction for at least 30 to 60 minutes. The Volume Weighted Average Price line is sloping in the same direction (rising for an uptrend, falling for a downtrend). Now price is pulling back to touch the line.
Entry
For an uptrend pullback: Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) when price touches the rising line and forms a Hammer or Bullish Engulfing candle, AND volume on this candle is 1.5 times the recent average. For a downtrend pullback: Buy a Put Option when price touches the falling line and forms a Shooting Star or Bearish Engulfing.
Stop Loss
10 to 15 points on the wrong side of the Volume Weighted Average Price line. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 35 percent.
Target
The previous swing high (for Calls) or swing low (for Puts). Minimum 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio. Trail your stop loss to breakeven once price has moved 1 times your risk in your favor.
Skip if
The Volume Weighted Average Price line is flat (no trend bias to trade), it is the first 15 minutes of the day, the rejection candle has a long wick on the wrong side, or the lunch hour (12:00 PM to 1:30 PM IST) is upon you.
