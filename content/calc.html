TRADING CALCULATORS 4 TOOLS

These are decision-support tools, not predictions. Use them before every trade to translate your capital and stop loss into real lot counts and a real rupee risk amount.
Why a trader without calculators is gambling: Gut-feel sizing is the number 1 reason retail accounts blow up. Every A+ setup has a correct lot count based on your capital and your stop-loss distance. Using the position-size calculator takes 15 seconds and ensures no single trade can wipe out your week. If you skip this step, you are trading with your eyes closed.

HOW TO USE THESE TOOLS — PRE-TRADE WORKFLOW (1 MINUTE)

  • Step 1 · POSITION SIZE — Enter your total capital, plus the maximum risk percentage per trade (1 to 2 percent is recommended), plus the premium, plus the stop loss distance. The calculator tells you how many LOTS you can safely buy. Never exceed this count.
  • Step 2 · RISK-TO-REWARD — Check whether the trade is worth taking. Aim for at least 1 to 2 — meaning the potential reward is at least twice the risk. If it is below 1 to 2, skip it, no matter how good the setup looks.
  • Step 3 · PREMIUM STOP LOSS — Convert the index-level stop loss into a premium-level stop loss that you can actually place on your broker. This is the number you enter in the Stop-Loss-Market order.
  • Step 4 · LOT VALUE & BREAK-EVEN — Know your margin requirement and your break-even point before you trade. This prevents over-leveraging when buying, and it prevents surprise SPAN+Exposure margin calls when selling.
Rule for beginners: Never risk more than 1 percent of your capital on a single option trade for your first 3 months. After 100 trades with a journal, if your win rate and risk-to-reward expectancy are positive, you can slowly scale up to 2 percent. Never cross 2 percent per trade — that is casino-level risk.

HOW TO USE THESE CALCULATORS

  • Every single trade: Run Calculator 1 first. If the maximum lots is less than 1, you cannot take the trade at this stop-loss distance — either widen the stop loss or skip the trade.
  • Before clicking Buy: Run Calculator 2 with your planned entry, stop loss, and target. If the risk-to-reward is below 1 to 2, fix your target or skip the trade.
  • After your order fills: Run Calculator 3 to place the exact Stop-Loss-Market order price at 30 to 35 percent below your fill price.
  • End of day: Run Calculator 4 to confirm your ledger matches what you expected — reconciliation catches broker-fee surprises early.
When to take this trade — The Calculator-Driven Position Sizing for every trade
Setup
You have a confirmed entry signal (a candle pattern at support / resistance with volume). You are about to click Buy. The market is open, India Volatility Index is in a normal range (12 to 22), and you have not yet hit your daily loss limit.
Entry
STOP. Run all four calculators in this order: (1) Position Size to get the safe number of lots. (2) Risk-to-Reward to confirm the setup pays at least 1 to 2. (3) Premium Stop Loss to know exactly where your protective stop will go. (4) Lot Value to confirm the blocked capital matches your plan. Only THEN place the Buy order.
Stop Loss
Use the exact rupee level returned by Calculator 3 — place this as a Stop-Loss-Market order on your broker IMMEDIATELY after your buy is filled (within 30 seconds). Never trade without an active stop loss order on the broker.
Target
Use the target price you entered into Calculator 2 (the one that gave you a 1 to 2 risk-to-reward or better). Book 50 percent at this target, then trail the rest with a stop at the previous swing low (for a Call Option) or swing high (for a Put Option).
Skip if
Calculator 1 returns less than 1 lot for your risk budget (you need a wider stop or more capital), OR Calculator 2 shows a risk-to-reward below 1 to 2 (skip the trade), OR you have already taken 3 trades today (over-trading kills your edge).
