TRADING CALCULATORS 4 TOOLS

These are decision support tools, not predictions. Use them before every trade to translate your capital and stop-loss into real lot counts and rupee risk.
Why a trader without calculators is gambling: Gut-feel sizing is the #1 reason retail accounts blow up. Every A+ setup has a correct lot count based on your capital and SL distance. Using the position-size calculator takes 15 seconds and ensures no single trade can wipe out your week. If you skip this step, you are trading with closed eyes.

HOW TO USE THESE TOOLS — PRE-TRADE WORKFLOW (1 MINUTE)

  • Step 1 · POSITION SIZE — Enter your total capital + max risk % per trade (1–2% recommended) + premium + SL distance. This tells you how many LOTS you can safely buy. Never exceed this count.
  • Step 2 · RISK:REWARD — Check if the trade is worth taking. Target at least 1:2 — meaning the potential reward is at least twice the risk. Below 1:2 = skip, regardless of how good the setup looks.
  • Step 3 · PREMIUM SL — Convert the index-level SL into a premium-level SL that you can place on your broker. This is the number you enter in the SL-M order.
  • Step 4 · LOT VALUE & BREAKEVEN — Know your margin requirement and breakeven before you trade. Prevents overleveraging on buying and surprise SPAN+Exposure margin calls on selling.
Rule for beginners: Never risk more than 1% of your capital on a single option trade for your first 3 months. After 100 trades with a journal, if your win-rate and R-expectancy are positive, you can slowly scale up to 2%. Never cross 2% per trade — that's casino-level risk.

HOW TO USE THESE CALCULATORS

  • Every single trade: Run Calculator 1 first. If max-lots < 1, you can't take the trade at this SL distance — either widen the SL or skip.
  • Before clicking Buy: Run Calculator 2 with your planned entry/SL/target. If RR < 1:2, fix your target or skip.
  • After fill: Run Calculator 3 to place the exact SL-M order price at 30–35% below your fill.
  • End of day: Run Calculator 4 to confirm your ledger matches what you expected — reconciliation catches broker-fee surprises early.
