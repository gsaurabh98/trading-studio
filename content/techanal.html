TECHNICAL ANALYSIS OPTIONS-TRADER EDITION

What is technical analysis? The study of price and volume to forecast the next probable move. It rests on three beliefs: (1) price reflects everything known about a stock, (2) prices move in trends, (3) history repeats because human emotions repeat. You are not predicting — you are estimating the probability of the next move and sizing your option buy accordingly.

Why it matters more for option buyers than for equity investors: you lose even if you are RIGHT about direction but WRONG about timing. A 10-day delay can evaporate 60% of an OTM premium via theta. Technical analysis exists to compress that timing risk.
THE 4 PILLARS OF TECHNICAL ANALYSIS
1 · PRICE ACTION
PRIMARY
Candles, trend-lines, swing highs/lows, chart patterns. Answers "where is price going and how fast?" — the single most important input for an option buyer.
2 · VOLUME
CONFIRMER
Participation behind the move. Rising price + rising volume = real trend. Rising price + falling volume = suspect. Volume confirms or rejects every price signal.
3 · MOMENTUM
OSCILLATOR
Speed of the move — RSI, MACD, Stochastics. Tells you if the trend is still alive, over-stretched, or fading. Divergence is the earliest reversal clue.
4 · MARKET STRUCTURE
CONTEXT
Higher highs / higher lows define the trend. S&R, CPR, trend-lines are the skeleton. A setup is only valid INSIDE the right market structure.
TOP-DOWN TA WORKFLOW FOR OPTION BUYERS
Never trade a stock in isolation. The index sets the weather, the sector sets the wind, the stock is the ship. If the index is trending up, fighting it with a PE is low-probability no matter how good the chart looks.
STEP 1 · INDEX BIAS
Check NIFTY / BANKNIFTY on daily + 1-hour charts. Are they above or below 20 EMA? Above CPR or below? This is your directional filter. Trade with it, not against it.
STEP 2 · SECTOR ROTATION
Open the sectoral indices (NIFTY IT, Bank, Auto, FMCG, Pharma). Which are leading? Which are lagging? Trade leaders long, laggards short — never the opposite.
STEP 3 · STOCK SELECTION
Pick a liquid F&O stock inside the leading sector. Check its daily trend, last 5-day high/low and today's gap. Only trade setups aligned with the stock's own daily trend.
STEP 4 · SETUP CONFIRMATION
At least 3 confluences: candle pattern + key level + volume / OI / RSI confirmation. Less than 3 = not a trade. This filter alone removes 80% of bad trades.
STEP 5 · STRIKE SELECTION
Choose strike from TA context: ITM for high-conviction swing, ATM for intraday momentum, slight OTM for fast breakouts only. Never deep OTM unless you accept the lottery math.
STEP 6 · EXPIRY CHOICE
Match expiry to your view horizon. Same-day move expected → weekly. 3–5 day swing → next-weekly. Multi-week trend → monthly. Never buy expiry shorter than your plan.
WHY TA IS CRITICAL FOR OPTION BUYERS (NOT OPTIONAL)
DIRECTIONAL EDGE
Options leverage direction 5–10×. Getting direction wrong half the time still profits if your winners are 2–3× your losers. TA gives you that directional hit-rate.
TIMING EDGE
A stock can take 10 days to move where you expected. By then theta has eaten your premium. TA narrows the entry window so you catch the first 60% of the move.
LEVEL-BASED RISK
TA defines where you are wrong — the level that invalidates the thesis. Your stop-loss is priced off that level, not a random 30% option drop.
STRIKE SELECTION
Support / resistance distance tells you how far price is likely to travel. That, plus delta, tells you which strike gives the best ₹-payoff per ₹ risked.
EXIT DISCIPLINE
TA-based targets (next S/R, prior day high/low, CPR edge) are objective. Without them, profit-taking becomes an emotional coin-flip between "hold more" and "book now".
CONTEXT FOR IV
TA tells you when a move is "about to happen" — exactly when IV is low. Buying options when both price AND IV are low is the highest-EV setup in the book.
6 CORE TA TOOLS YOU MUST MASTER
1 · MULTI-TIMEFRAME TREND
Use daily + 15-min + 5-min. Daily = bias, 15-min = setup, 5-min = entry trigger. Never trade if 15-min and daily disagree.
2 · SUPPORT & RESISTANCE
Previous day high/low, swing points, CPR. All TA entries must happen NEAR a level — mid-range setups are coin-flips.
3 · VOLUME CONFIRMATION
Breakouts need 1.5×+ average volume. Breakdowns on rising volume are real. No volume = no trade, whatever the chart looks like.
4 · MOMENTUM (RSI & MACD)
RSI 40–60 = neutral, >70 overbought, <30 oversold. Divergence is the gold signal — price higher, RSI lower = trend dying.
5 · CANDLESTICK AT LEVEL
A hammer or engulfing at a key level >> the same candle mid-range. Pattern + level + volume = the trifecta of a valid TA trade.
6 · OPTION CHAIN / OI
Max OI strikes, PCR, OI change & max-pain are taught in their own dedicated section → F&O Playbook → Option Chain.

PRE-TRADE TA CHECKLIST (7 ITEMS)

  • Index direction — is NIFTY/BANKNIFTY aligned with my trade?
  • Sector momentum — is the stock's sector leading or lagging today?
  • Daily trend — am I with the stock's daily higher-TF trend?
  • Key level nearby — entry within 0.3–0.5% of a prior S/R / CPR / pivot?
  • Candle confirmation — closed candle signalling my direction at that level?
  • Volume — is the breakout / rejection backed by above-average volume?
  • Option-chain agreement — OI change supporting the same directional view?

Fewer than 5 of these 7 = skip the trade. Options are expensive; half-setups are how accounts bleed.

BEGINNER PRIORITY — LEARN IN THIS ORDER

  • Price action first — candles, swing structure, S/R. 70% of your edge lives here.
  • Volume second — add volume as a confirmer; it filters 80% of failed breakouts.
  • One indicator third — pick ONE momentum tool (RSI or MACD). Resist the urge to stack 5 indicators; that's noise, not signal.
  • Option chain last — once TA is solid, overlay OI / PCR / max-pain to add conviction — never as the primary signal.
Three TA mistakes that wreck option buyers: (1) trading a setup that ignores the higher-timeframe trend, (2) stacking 5 indicators and rationalising any direction from them, (3) entering mid-range without a clear invalidation level. Fix these three and your hit-rate jumps materially.
HOW TO COMBINE INDICATORS WITH CANDLES

INDICATOR STACKING FOR A+ ENTRIES

  • Minimum confirmations: Never enter on just ONE indicator. Require at least candle pattern + 1 indicator + volume.
  • Don't stack duplicates: RSI + Stochastic + MACD are all momentum — they'll always agree. Pick ONE momentum + ONE trend + VWAP.
  • The A+ checklist: Candle pattern + at S/R + volume spike + VWAP aligned + EMA aligned + RSI not extreme. Hit 5 of 6 → high conviction.
  • Lagging vs leading: Price action & volume = leading. Moving averages & MACD = lagging. Use leading to enter, lagging to confirm direction.
  • Beware of curve-fitting: Adding a 7th indicator because your backtest failed = noise. Keep your setup to 3 tools max.
