PRE-TRADE CHECKLIST MUST FOLLOW

Why this page is the most underrated page on the entire site: Most retail traders DREAD discipline. They want to wake up, open the chart, and start clicking BUY. But the difference between a trader who survives and one who blows up is one thing — a routine.

Pro traders follow a fixed daily process. They check certain things at certain times. They take trades only in certain windows. They review their trades at the end of the day. This page hands you the EXACT routine that pros follow. Print it, paste it next to your screen, and follow it for 30 days. Your results will change.
HOUR-BY-HOUR DAILY TRADING ROUTINE
The simple truth: Consistent profits come from a consistent daily process. Most losses happen OUTSIDE these "safe" windows — when you are bored, revenge-trading, or chasing a move. Below is the exact hour-by-hour routine.

THE 9:30–10:30 RULE
Most successful Indian option buyers trade ONLY this hour. Institutional flow is cleanest, patterns are most reliable, and emotion is lowest. If you could only trade 60 minutes a day, pick this one.
THE LUNCH-HOUR TRAP
12:00–13:30 has the worst risk-adjusted returns of the day. Low volume = fake breakouts, widened spreads, news-driven whipsaws. Statistically, skipping lunch-hour trades adds 15–25% to yearly P&L.
POST-MARKET HABIT
The 30 minutes AFTER market close is where real improvement happens. Journal the WHY of every entry/exit, not just the result. Patterns in your mistakes appear after ~20 entries — invisible without this habit.
BEFORE MARKET OPENS (9:00 AM)
GIFT NIFTY CHECK
Check Gift Nifty level. Is it up or down vs previous close? How much? This sets your morning bias.
INDIA VIX LEVEL
Check India VIX. Below 13 = buy options freely. Above 20 = reduce option buying, widen SL.
MARK KEY LEVELS ON CHART
Mark Previous Day High (PDH), Previous Day Low (PDL), Previous Day Close (PDC), major round numbers, and weekly S/R levels.
CHECK ECONOMIC CALENDAR
Any RBI announcement, US Fed, CPI, results today? If yes — reduce position size significantly or avoid altogether.
CHECK 60-MIN TREND
Is price above or below 20 EMA on 60-min chart? This sets your directional bias for the day.
OPTION CHAIN SCAN
Open F&O Playbook → Option Chain and note today's key max-OI strikes. Full reading method is taught there.
BEFORE EACH TRADE (ENTRY CHECKLIST)
IS THERE A CLEAR CANDLE PATTERN?
Can you name the pattern? Is it clean and unambiguous? If you have to squint or imagine it — it's not there. Skip.
IS IT AT A KEY LEVEL?
Is the pattern appearing at PDH, PDL, major S/R, round number, or EMA? Pattern in middle of nowhere = skip.
DOES VOLUME CONFIRM?
Is volume on the signal candle above average? Low volume pattern = weak signal = skip or reduce size significantly.
DOES IT ALIGN WITH HIGHER TF TREND?
Is the 15-min signal in the same direction as the 60-min trend? Counter-trend trades have much lower win rate.
WHERE IS MY STOP LOSS?
Can you clearly define your SL BEFORE entering? CE: below signal wick low. PE: above signal wick high. No clear SL = no trade.
IS RR AT LEAST 1:2?
Calculate your risk and potential reward before entering. If RR is less than 1:2, skip the trade even if the pattern is perfect.
AM I WITHIN TRADING WINDOW?
Is it before 2:30 PM? Is it after 9:30 AM? Are you in the 9:30–11:30 or 1:00–2:30 PM window? Outside = skip.
HAVE I HIT MY DAILY LOSS LIMIT?
Have you already lost 3-5% today? If yes — close the terminal. Trading is over for the day. No exceptions.
AM I IN THE RIGHT EMOTIONAL STATE?
Are you frustrated, anxious, or desperate to make money? If emotional state is not neutral/calm — do not trade. Come back tomorrow.
WHILE THE TRADE IS LIVE (IN-TRADE CHECKLIST)
Why this matters: Most losses don't come from bad entries — they come from bad trade management. A mediocre entry with great management often beats a perfect entry with emotional management. Follow these rules while a trade is running, not after you have already lost money.
SL IS PLACED ON BROKER — NOT IN HEAD
The moment your order fills, place the SL-M order on the broker. "Mental stop loss" = no stop loss. If you cannot place it instantly, you have no business in that trade.
DO NOT AVERAGE DOWN
If the trade goes against you — accept the loss. Never double up on a losing position. Averaging down on options is the fastest route to a blown account because theta keeps eating both lots.
BOOK 50% AT 1R (1:1)
When the trade moves 1R in your favour (premium has doubled if SL is at 50% of entry), book half. Move SL on remaining half to cost. Now you have a risk-free runner.
TRAIL THE REMAINING HALF
Trail SL to below each new swing low (for CE) or above each new swing high (for PE) on your signal timeframe. Let the market stop you out — don't exit on emotion.
TIME STOP — 20 MIN RULE
If the trade hasn't moved 0.5R in your favour within 20 minutes, the setup has failed. Exit at market even if SL hasn't hit — theta is bleeding you. Redeploy capital on the next setup.
DO NOT WATCH TICK-BY-TICK
Close the P&L window. Check the chart once every 5 minutes on your signal timeframe. Watching every tick creates emotional exits — you will book winners too early and hold losers too long.
NO CHANGES AFTER ENTRY
Never widen SL, never move target further away, never increase size mid-trade. The trade plan you made BEFORE entering is the only plan. Everything you do mid-trade is emotion — and emotion loses money.
POST-TRADE REVIEW (AFTER EVERY TRADE)
The 2-minute review: Immediately after each trade closes, take a screenshot of the chart (marked with entry, SL, target, exit) and answer these 5 questions in your journal. This single habit compounds into the biggest improvement you will make.
1
DID I FOLLOW MY SYSTEM?
Yes or No. Not "sort of". If Yes — good, regardless of outcome. If No — this trade is a rule violation, not a loss. The P&L doesn't matter as much as the discipline.
2
WAS ENTRY + SL + TARGET CLEARLY DEFINED BEFORE THE TRADE?
If any of the three was decided AFTER entry, this trade was undisciplined. Write down the exact levels from your pre-entry plan for comparison.
3
WAS THE SETUP AN A+ OR A B-GRADE?
A+ = every condition met (level + pattern + volume + HTF trend + window). B / C = missed 1-2 conditions. Only A+ trades should be in your book. B-grade wins are lucky, not skill.
4
WHAT WAS MY EMOTIONAL STATE?
Calm / FOMO / Revenge / Greedy / Scared. Over 30–50 trades, patterns appear — e.g. "I always lose after a win" = revenge / overconfidence. Emotion data is more useful than P&L data.
5
WHAT IS THE ONE LESSON?
One sentence. Not "I will be more careful" — that is useless. Something actionable: "I will skip trades when volume is below 20-period average" or "I will stop trading after 2 losses". Lessons must be specific enough to execute.
WEEKLY REVIEW (EVERY SATURDAY)
Why weekly: Individual trades are noise. Weekly patterns are signal. Set aside 30 minutes every Saturday to review the full week — this is where real improvement happens.
METRICS TO CALCULATE
• Win rate (winners / total trades)
• Average win in R vs average loss in R
• Total R for the week (should be positive over 4+ weeks)
• Max drawdown during the week
• Number of rule violations
• Best setup type (count A+ wins by category)
QUESTIONS TO ANSWER
• Which day was most profitable? Why?
• Which day was worst? What went wrong?
• Which setup has the highest edge for me?
• Which setup keeps failing — should I drop it?
• Did I trade outside my window? How often?
• Did I respect daily loss limits every day?

THE ULTIMATE TRADING MANTRA

  • Protect your capital first. Profit is secondary. A trader who survives lives to fight another day.
  • No setup = No trade. Sitting on your hands IS a position. The best trades are obvious — if you are not sure, you are out.
  • Plan the trade, then trade the plan. Decide everything BEFORE entering. Never make decisions while you are in a trade.
  • Cut losses quickly, let winners run. Small frequent losses are fine. Big losses are what ruin accounts.
  • Consistency beats occasional big wins. 2 percent daily compounded beats one lucky 50 percent day followed by a 50 percent wipeout.
  • The market will always be there tomorrow. Missing a trade is FAR better than taking a bad trade.
When to take this trade — The Prime-Window A+ Setup (the only trade you take when in doubt)
Setup
It is between 9:30 and 10:30 AM IST (the prime window). You have completed every checklist item above. The market today is a clear trending day or a clear range-bound day (NOT a choppy day). The Previous Day High, Previous Day Low, Central Pivot Range, and the major support / resistance levels are all marked on your chart. The India Volatility Index is between 12 and 18. There is no major event scheduled today. The Foreign Institutional Investors flow agrees with your direction. The option chain confirms the bias (the Put-Call Ratio is above 1.0 for a bullish trade, or below 0.8 for a bearish trade).
Entry
The setup must include ALL of these: a candle pattern + at a level + with volume + with the higher-timeframe trend. You wrote down the entry, stop loss, and target price BEFORE the trade. Place a Limit order at the planned entry price (not a Market order).
Stop Loss
Use the level you wrote down on the checklist (5 to 10 points beyond the structural wick). On the option premium itself, set a hard stop at 30 percent loss. Place a Stop-Loss-Market order on the broker IMMEDIATELY after your buy is filled.
Target
Target 1 = at least a 1 to 2 risk-to-reward (book 50 percent here AND move the stop loss to your entry / break-even). Target 2 = the next major level. Trail the rest with the Volume Weighted Average Price (5-minute). Be flat by 3:00 PM IST.
Skip if
It is the first 15 minutes (9:15 to 9:30 AM — observation only), OR you have already taken 2 trades today (the maximum is 3 trades, but the 3rd is only after a confirmed A+ setup), OR your daily loss limit has been hit (-2 percent of capital — STOP for the day), OR you are between 12:00 and 1:30 PM (the lunch dead-zone — setups here are usually noise), OR after 2:30 PM IST (Theta decay accelerates and the win rate drops sharply).
