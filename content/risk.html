POSITION SIZING — THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE
THE 1-2% RULE
1-2%
PER TRADE
Never risk more than 1-2% of your total capital on a single trade.

Capital ₹1,00,000 → Max risk per trade = ₹1,000–2,000. This ensures no single trade can seriously damage your account.
MAX DAILY LOSS LIMIT
3-5%
PER DAY
Set a hard daily loss limit of 3-5% of capital. If you hit it, STOP trading for the day.

₹1,00,000 capital → Stop at ₹3,000–5,000 loss. No exceptions. This prevents account blowups.
NEVER AVERAGE DOWN
NEVER
CRITICAL RULE
If your option position is losing, NEVER buy more to average. Options decay — averaging just doubles your loss. Exit and reassess instead.
MAX TRADES PER DAY
2-3
DISCIPLINE
Limit yourself to 2-3 trades per day. More trades = more mistakes = more losses. Quality over quantity. Wait for A+ setups only.
RISK:REWARD RATIO
MINIMUM 1:2 RISK:REWARD

Only take trades where potential reward is at least 2× the risk. Even with 50% win rate, 1:2 RR keeps you profitable.

DRAWDOWN & RECOVERY MATH — WHY 2% MATTERS
THE RECOVERY TRAP
Lose 10% → need +11.1% to recover
Lose 20% → need +25% to recover
Lose 30% → need +43% to recover
Lose 50% → need +100% to recover
Lose 70% → need +233% to recover
Losses and gains are NOT symmetric. A 50% drawdown requires doubling your account to get back to break-even. Protect downside first.
CONSECUTIVE LOSSES PROBABILITY
At 50% win rate (realistic):
• 5 losses in a row: 3.1% chance
• 7 losses in a row: 0.8% chance
• 10 losses in a row: 0.1% chance
Over 200 trades, you WILL hit:
• A 7-loss streak (expected ~1.5×)
• Possibly a 9-loss streak
Your risk-per-trade MUST survive a 10-loss streak without emotional ruin. At 2% per trade = 18% max drawdown. At 5% = 40% drawdown = career-ending.
RISK OF RUIN — 2% vs 5%
50% win rate, 1:2 RR:
• Risk 1% per trade → ~0% risk of ruin
• Risk 2% per trade → ~0% risk of ruin
• Risk 5% per trade → ~4% risk of ruin
• Risk 10% per trade → ~36% risk of ruin
• Risk 20% per trade → ~77% risk of ruin
"Risk of ruin" = probability of losing 50% before doubling. Risk 20% per trade and 3 out of 4 traders blow up, regardless of skill.
COMPOUNDING AT 2%/DAY
₹1,00,000 capital, 2% daily gain:
• 5 days (1 week): ₹1,10,408
• 20 days (1 month): ₹1,48,595
• 250 days (1 year): ₹1,40,91,574
The math on paper — impossible in practice due to drawdowns, taxes, slippage, costs. But even 0.3% compounded daily = 110% in a year. Small, consistent gains win.
