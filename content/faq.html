FAQ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q1 · Why did my CE lose money even though Nifty went UP?
Three possible reasons:
• Theta decay — the spot move wasn't enough to offset time decay, especially after 1 PM on expiry day.
• IV crush — India VIX fell after an anticipated event. Your option lost vega value faster than it gained delta value.
• OTM entry — if your CE was far out-of-the-money, delta was 0.1–0.2. A 30-point Nifty move only moved your premium ₹3–6 while theta ate ₹10.

Fix: Buy ATM / slightly ITM strikes. Exit by 1:30 PM on expiry. Avoid buying when VIX is near a multi-day high.
Q2 · Why does price gravitate to Max Pain on expiry?
Market makers (institutional option sellers) have short gamma near the most-written strikes. As expiry approaches, they dynamically hedge by buying/selling Nifty futures — and the net effect pushes spot toward the strike where their loss is minimised (= Max Pain).

It's not a conspiracy — it's the mechanical result of delta hedging. Strongest effect after 1 PM on expiry day.
Q3 · Why does theta kill me on Tuesday afternoon?
Nifty weekly expiry = Tuesday 3:30 PM. ATM theta decay is non-linear — roughly:
• Mon: ₹15–20/hour
• Tue morning: ₹25–35/hour
• Tue 12–3 PM: ₹40–60/hour
• Tue 3–3:30 PM: near-vertical collapse

Rule: Don't BUY ATM Nifty options after 1 PM on Tuesday. Either exit or switch to short strangles/iron condors if experienced.
Q4 · Should I paper trade before real money?
Yes — at least 50 trades. Paper trading doesn't teach emotional control (that only comes with real skin in the game), but it teaches:
• Execution mechanics (order types, exits)
• Pattern recognition
• Setup filtering
• Journal discipline

After 50 paper trades, start with 1 lot Nifty ATM only. Don't skip this phase to "save time" — you'll pay for that time in losses instead.
Q5 · What capital do I need to start trading options?
Post-SEBI 2024–26 lot revision (Nifty 65, BankNifty 30, Sensex 20):
• Minimum realistic: ₹1,00,000 — 1 lot Nifty ATM, 1–2 trades/day
• Comfortable: ₹3,00,000 — 1–2 lots, scaled position sizing
• Ideal: ₹5,00,000+ — full diversification across Nifty + Sensex

Below ₹1 lakh, even one losing trade can breach the 2% risk rule. Paper trade until you have ₹1 lakh dedicated risk capital.
Q6 · Is trading on expiry day (0 DTE) worth it?
0 DTE = gambling disguised as trading for most retail. Premiums are tiny, theta is brutal, one small wrong move = 100% loss.

Only consider if:
• You trade in the first 30 min (9:15–9:45) when gamma still works for you
• Strict SL ≤ 30% of premium
• Max 2 attempts then stop

For beginners: skip 0 DTE entirely.
Q7 · ATM, ITM, or OTM — which should I buy?
ATM (At-The-Money): Best balance of delta + theta. Default choice for 90% of intraday option buying.
ITM (In-The-Money): Higher delta (~0.6–0.8), less theta but 2–3× more capital per lot. Use for high-conviction trades with wider SL.
OTM (Out-of-The-Money): Cheapest, but you need a BIG move to profit. Low probability trade. Avoid except in specific breakout setups.
Q8 · When should I book profit / exit?
Use a scale-out approach:
• T1 (1:1 R:R): Book 50%. Move SL to entry (risk-free trade).
• T2 (1:2 R:R): Book 30%. Trail SL below recent swing low.
• T3 (runner): Let 20% ride with trailing SL.

This gives you lower avg drawdown + the psychological relief of banking profit while still capturing big winners.
Q9 · Why did BankNifty weekly options get discontinued?
In Nov 2024, SEBI reduced each exchange to ONE weekly option to curb the option-buyer retail wipeout (regulators estimated 91% of F&O traders were net losers). Consequence:
• NSE kept Nifty weekly (moved to Tuesday, Sep 2025)
• NSE dropped BankNifty, FinNifty, MidCpNifty weeklies — monthly only
• BSE kept Sensex weekly (moved to Thursday, Sep 2025)

For retail, this means BankNifty is now a lot less useful for weekly theta plays.
Q10 · Scalping vs positional vs swing — which suits beginners?
Swing / positional (2–10 day holds): BEST for beginners. Lower emotional load, tolerates imperfect entries, longer decision windows.
Intraday (hold 1–4 hrs): Standard option-buying timeframe. Second-best for beginners once basics are solid.
Scalping (hold < 10 min): Requires microsecond discipline, low costs, full-time attention. Retail option buyers almost always lose at scalping due to bid-ask + brokerage.
Q11 · Technical analysis or fundamentals — what matters for intraday options?
For intraday index options: Technical analysis is 95% of the edge. Fundamentals move on quarterly/yearly cycles — irrelevant for a 2-hour option trade.

What matters intraday:
• Chart pattern + S/R + volume + VWAP + OI
• India VIX
• Gift Nifty + global cues (for open)
• Upcoming events (RBI, Fed, Budget, results)

Fundamentals matter only for delivery equity holdings.
Q12 · How do I know I'm ready to increase lot size?
Before scaling up, require ALL of the following:
6 months of consistent profitable trading
50+ trades with logged data, avg R:R ≥ 1.5
Win rate on main setup ≥ 45%
Max drawdown in the 6 months < 15% of capital
Didn't break risk rules even once in last 30 days

Scale by 1.5×, never 2×. Scaling too fast means the first losing streak at new size hurts disproportionately.
