PICK YOUR STRATEGY DECISION TREE · 6 PLAYBOOKS

What is this page about? A "playbook" is just a fancy name for a STRATEGY — a complete plan for one specific market situation. This page gives you 6 battle-tested playbooks for different types of trading days.

The big idea: Different days need different strategies. A breakout strategy on a sideways day = guaranteed loss. A range strategy on a trending day = missed huge profits. First, figure out WHAT KIND of day it is. Then pick the right playbook. The decision tree below shows you exactly how.
STEP 1 · WHAT KIND OF DAY IS IT TODAY?
The single biggest edge in intraday trading: Figure out WHAT KIND of day it is BEFORE you start trading it. There are 3 main types of days — trending, sideways (range-bound), and choppy / news. Each one calls for a different approach.
TRENDING DAY
DIRECTIONAL FLOW
How to recognise it:
• A narrow Central Pivot Range (less than 0.2 percent of the index)
• A gap-up or gap-down opening, with follow-through
• Price stays above (or below) the Volume Weighted Average Price for most of the day
• The India Volatility Index is between 12 and 16
• All Exponential Moving Averages (20, 50, and 200) are stacked in one direction on the 15-minute chart

Best strategies: Opening Range Breakout · Central Pivot Range Breakout · Volume Weighted Average Price Bounce · Flag Breakout · Bollinger Band Squeeze
RANGE-BOUND DAY
OSCILLATING
How to recognise it:
• A wide Central Pivot Range (greater than 0.5 percent of the index)
• Price crosses the Volume Weighted Average Price 5 or more times
• The 60-minute Exponential Moving Averages are flat and interleaved
• The India Volatility Index is between 14 and 19
• A clear horizontal ceiling and floor are visible on the chart

Best strategies: Support and Resistance Reversal + Candle (fade the extremes) · Bollinger Band Squeeze (wait for the volatility breakout). AVOID any directional breakout trade in the middle of the range.
CHOPPY / NEWS DAY
STAY OUT
How to recognise it:
• The India Volatility Index is above 20
• Consecutive candles with heavy wicks on both sides
• A major event is scheduled today (Reserve Bank of India policy, US Federal Reserve meeting, Union Budget, US Consumer Price Index)
• Price whipsaws through support and resistance without committing to either side

Best action: Do NOT trade today. Sit in cash. Protecting your capital on these days is an edge by itself — 80 percent of big drawdowns happen on choppy days.

THE 6 BATTLE-TESTED PLAYBOOKS
Each playbook below has exact entry / SL / target rules. No subjectivity. If the conditions aren't met, you don't trade. Pick ONE playbook, master it for 30 days, then expand.
1 ORB — OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT
WHEN: A trending day, with a narrow Central Pivot Range, in the first 60 minutes of the session.
SETUP: Mark the Opening Range High and the Opening Range Low from the 9:15 to 9:30 AM candle (15-minute chart).
ENTRY (Call Option): The 15-minute candle closes ABOVE the Opening Range High, with volume greater than 1.5 times the recent average → Buy an At The Money Call Option.
ENTRY (Put Option): The 15-minute candle closes BELOW the Opening Range Low, with volume → Buy an At The Money Put Option.
STOP LOSS: The opposite side of the Opening Range candle’s wick.
TARGET 1: Project 1 times the Opening Range size (book 50 percent here)  |  TARGET 2: 2 times the Opening Range size (trail the stop loss to the entry / break-even).
SKIP IF: The Opening Range is wider than 1 percent of the index · the breakout has no volume · the time is after 11:00 AM.
WHY IT WORKS: The first-hour volatility sets the rails for the day. Breaking those rails = institutional positioning for the direction.
2 CPR BREAKOUT
WHEN: A narrow Central Pivot Range day (the width is less than 0.2 percent of the index) — this is the highest-edge setup.
SETUP: Plot the Classical Pivot Point + the Central Pivot Range on the 15-minute chart before the market opens.
ENTRY (Call Option): A 15-minute candle CLOSES above the Top Central level + a retest of that level + a bullish confirmation candle → Buy an At The Money Call Option.
ENTRY (Put Option): A 15-minute candle CLOSES below the Bottom Central level + a retest + a bearish confirmation candle → Buy an At The Money Put Option.
STOP LOSS: Back inside the Central Pivot Range (below the Top Central level for a Call Option, or above the Bottom Central level for a Put Option).
TARGET 1: Resistance 1 / Support 1 (book 50 percent)  |  TARGET 2: Resistance 2 / Support 2.
SKIP IF: The Central Pivot Range is wide (above 0.5 percent) · price was rejected at the Top Central / Bottom Central levels twice · there is no retest after the break.
WHY IT WORKS: A narrow Central Pivot Range = the previous day was indecisive = today expects a directional resolution. The break of the Central Pivot Range IS that resolution.
3 VWAP BOUNCE (Trend Pullback)
WHEN: A trending day — price is clearly above (or below) the Volume Weighted Average Price on the 15-minute chart.
SETUP: Wait for a pullback TO the Volume Weighted Average Price during the trend.
ENTRY (Call Option): Price in an uptrend touches the Volume Weighted Average Price + a hammer or bullish engulfing candle forms on the 5-minute or 15-minute chart → Buy an At The Money Call Option.
ENTRY (Put Option): Mirror image in a downtrend — a bearish candle at the Volume Weighted Average Price → Buy an At The Money Put Option.
STOP LOSS: Below the bounce candle’s low (10 to 20 points away).
TARGET 1: The prior day’s high or low, or the pivot Resistance 1 / Support 1  |  TARGET 2: A 1 to 3 risk-to-reward, with a trailing stop loss.
SKIP IF: Price has crossed the Volume Weighted Average Price 5 or more times (no real trend) · a choppy day · late session (after 2:30 PM IST).
WHY IT WORKS: The Volume Weighted Average Price is the institutional fair-value line. Pullbacks to the Volume Weighted Average Price in strong trends are where the big players add to their positions.
4 S/R REVERSAL + CANDLE
WHEN: A range-bound day (wide Central Pivot Range, with price oscillating), OR a strong support / resistance level on the daily timeframe.
SETUP: Identify the major horizontal support / resistance from daily + 60-minute confluence.
ENTRY (Call Option): Price tags the major support level + a hammer / bullish engulfing / morning star pattern forms on the 15-minute chart + volume → Buy an At The Money Call Option.
ENTRY (Put Option): Mirror image at resistance → Buy an At The Money Put Option.
STOP LOSS: 10 to 20 points beyond the wick of the reversal candle.
TARGET 1: 50 percent of the range (book 50 percent here)  |  TARGET 2: The opposite side of the range.
SKIP IF: A strong trend is already in progress (don’t fade strength) · there is no candle confirmation · the support / resistance level is fresh (it needs at least 2 prior tests).
WHY IT WORKS: A well-tested support / resistance level has high memory. A reversal candle at that level = institutional absorption showing itself.
5 FLAG BREAKOUT (Momentum Continuation)
WHEN: After a strong momentum move (the pole) + a tight consolidation phase (the flag) — best after news or after a strong opening.
SETUP: Identify the pole (a move of at least 0.8 percent in less than 30 minutes) + the flag (5 to 12 candles, in a tight range, sloping against the trend).
ENTRY (Call Option): Break of the flag high + a volume surge greater than 1.5 times the recent average → Buy an At The Money Call Option (a bull flag).
ENTRY (Put Option): Break of the flag low + volume → Buy an At The Money Put Option (a bear flag).
STOP LOSS: The opposite side of the flag (below the flag low for a Call Option, or above the flag high for a Put Option).
TARGET: The pole length projected from the breakout point (the measured-move rule).
SKIP IF: The flag is more than 15 candles (it loses momentum) · the flag is wider than 50 percent of the pole’s height · there is no volume on the break.
WHY IT WORKS: Flags are pauses where smart money accumulates before the next leg of the move. The tighter the flag, the bigger the follow-through.
7 BB SQUEEZE BREAKOUT (Volatility Expansion)
WHEN: A low-volatility day, with the Bollinger Band Width at a 4-week low, after a consolidation — best on Bank Nifty mornings.
SETUP: Bollinger Bands (20, 2) visibly contracting for 10 or more candles + the Bollinger Band Width indicator is near its 4-week minimum + the Keltner Channel (20, 1.5) fully engulfs the Bollinger Bands (this is the official squeeze).
ENTRY (Call Option): A 15-minute candle closes above the upper Bollinger Band + volume greater than 1.5 times the average + the Relative Strength Index is above 55 → Buy an At The Money Call Option.
ENTRY (Put Option): A 15-minute candle closes below the lower Bollinger Band + volume + the Relative Strength Index is below 45 → Buy an At The Money Put Option.
STOP LOSS: Back inside the bands (specifically, below the middle band / 20-period Exponential Moving Average for a Call Option, or above the 20-period Exponential Moving Average for a Put Option).
TARGET 1: 1 times the band-width at the breakout (book 50 percent here)  |  TARGET 2: 2 times the band-width, OR trail to the 20-period Exponential Moving Average.
SKIP IF: The breakout happens after 2:00 PM IST (Theta decay kills the option) · there is no Keltner engulfment · the bands are already expanding · more than 50 percent of the expected move has already happened before your entry.
WHY IT WORKS: Volatility is mean-reverting. A tight squeeze builds up compressed energy. The direction of the break is the direction of the next expansion — and expansion moves are the fastest, cleanest intraday moves available.
When to take this trade — The Opening Range Breakout (the cleanest beginner-friendly playbook)
Setup
It is between 9:30 and 9:45 AM IST. The first 15-minute candle of Nifty (9:15 to 9:30) has formed. Mark its high (the Opening Range High) and its low (the Opening Range Low). The range size is less than 1 percent of the spot price (so it is a narrow range, NOT a wide one). The Central Pivot Range from yesterday is also narrow. The India Volatility Index is between 12 and 18.
Entry
Wait for the next 15-minute candle. If it CLOSES above the Opening Range High AND the volume during that candle is greater than 1.5 times the recent average → BUY one lot of an At The Money Call Option of the current-week expiry. If it CLOSES below the Opening Range Low with the same volume → BUY one lot of an At The Money Put Option.
Stop Loss
The opposite side of the Opening Range candle (the wick). On the option premium itself, set a hard stop at 30 percent loss. Place a Stop-Loss-Market order on the broker immediately after your buy order is filled.
Target
Target 1 = 1 times the Opening Range size (book 50 percent here). Target 2 = 2 times the Opening Range size (trail the stop loss for the remainder to the entry / break-even level). Exit by 2:30 PM IST regardless.
Skip if
The Opening Range size is greater than 1 percent of the spot price (then it is too wide to give a clean breakout), OR the breakout candle has below-average volume (no institutional commitment), OR it is after 11:00 AM (the Opening Range concept loses its edge), OR the India Volatility Index is above 22 (your option premium will be crushed by the Implied Volatility drop after the breakout).

THE BOTTOM LINE — PICK ONE PLAYBOOK
The number 1 mistake new traders make: trying all 6 strategies in the first week, mastering none of them. Real edge comes from repetition, not from variety.

Our suggestion for most traders:
→ Week 1 to 4: Paper-trade the Opening Range Breakout only. Log every setup (taken / skipped / outcome).
→ Month 2: Add the Support and Resistance Reversal when clear range-bound days appear.
→ Month 3 onwards: Add the Volume Weighted Average Price Bounce and the Central Pivot Range Breakout.
→ Month 6 onwards: Add the Flag Breakout after you have mastered reading momentum.
→ Month 9 onwards: Add the Bollinger Band Squeeze once you can identify true volatility contraction on the Bollinger Band Width indicator.

Consistency with ONE strategy beats mediocrity across six. Every professional trader you admire trades 2 to 3 setups, NOT 10.
