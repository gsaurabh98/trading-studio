STEP 1 · WHAT KIND OF DAY IS IT?
The single biggest edge in intraday trading: diagnosing the day before you trade it. Different days need different strategies. Trying to trade a range-bound day with a breakout strategy = guaranteed losses.
TRENDING DAY
DIRECTIONAL FLOW
How to recognise:
• Narrow CPR (<0.2%)
• Gap up/down opening with follow-through
• Price above/below VWAP for most of the day
• India VIX between 12–16
• All EMAs (20/50/200) stacked in one direction on 15-min

Best strategies: ORB Breakout · CPR Breakout · VWAP Bounce · Flag Breakout · BB Squeeze
RANGE-BOUND DAY
OSCILLATING
How to recognise:
• Wide CPR (>0.5%)
• Price crossing VWAP 5+ times
• 60-min EMAs flat and interleaved
• India VIX 14–19
• Clear horizontal ceiling + floor visible

Best strategies: S/R Reversal + Candle (fade extremes) · BB Squeeze (wait for volatility breakout). AVOID directional breakout trades mid-range.
CHOPPY / NEWS DAY
STAY OUT
How to recognise:
• India VIX > 20
• Consecutive wick-heavy candles both sides
• Major event scheduled (RBI, Fed, Budget, US CPI)
• Price whipsaws through S/R without commitment

Best action: Don't trade. Sit in cash. Protecting capital on these days is an edge — 80% of big drawdowns happen on choppy days.

THE 6 BATTLE-TESTED PLAYBOOKS
Each playbook below has exact entry / SL / target rules. No subjectivity. If the conditions aren't met, you don't trade. Pick ONE playbook, master it for 30 days, then expand.
1 ORB — OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT
WHEN: Trending day, narrow CPR, first 60 minutes
SETUP: Mark ORH and ORL from 9:15–9:30 candle (15-min)
ENTRY CE: 15-min close above ORH + volume > 1.5× avg → Buy ATM CE
ENTRY PE: 15-min close below ORL + volume → Buy ATM PE
SL: Opposite side of the OR candle wick
T1: 1× OR range projected (book 50%)  |  T2: 2× OR range (trail SL to BE)
SKIP IF: OR range > 1% of index · Breakout without volume · After 11:00
WHY IT WORKS: First hour volatility sets the day's rails. Breaking them = institutional positioning for direction.
2 CPR BREAKOUT
WHEN: Narrow CPR day (width <0.2% of index) — highest edge
SETUP: Plot Classical Pivot + CPR on 15-min chart pre-market
ENTRY CE: 15-min candle CLOSE above TC + retest + bullish confirmation → Buy ATM CE
ENTRY PE: 15-min close below BC + retest + bearish confirmation → Buy ATM PE
SL: Back inside CPR (below TC for CE / above BC for PE)
T1: R1 / S1 (book 50%)  |  T2: R2 / S2
SKIP IF: CPR is wide (>0.5%) · Price rejected TC/BC twice · No retest after break
WHY IT WORKS: Narrow CPR = previous day was indecisive = today expects directional resolution. Break of CPR = the resolution.
3 VWAP BOUNCE (Trend Pullback)
WHEN: Trending day — price clearly above/below VWAP on 15-min
SETUP: Wait for pullback TO VWAP during the trend
ENTRY CE: Price in uptrend touches VWAP + hammer/bullish engulfing on 5 or 15-min → Buy ATM CE
ENTRY PE: Mirror in downtrend — bearish candle at VWAP → Buy ATM PE
SL: Below the bounce candle low (10–20 pts away)
T1: Prior day high/low or pivot R1/S1  |  T2: 1:3 R:R with trailing SL
SKIP IF: Price has crossed VWAP 5+ times (no trend) · Choppy day · Late session (after 2:30 PM)
WHY IT WORKS: VWAP = institutional fair-value line. Pullbacks to VWAP in strong trends are where the big players add to positions.
4 S/R REVERSAL + CANDLE
WHEN: Range-bound day (wide CPR, price oscillating) OR strong S/R on daily TF
SETUP: Identify major horizontal S/R from daily + 60-min confluence
ENTRY CE: Price tags major support + hammer/bull engulfing/morning star on 15-min + volume → Buy ATM CE
ENTRY PE: Mirror at resistance → Buy ATM PE
SL: 10–20 pts beyond wick of reversal candle
T1: 50% of the range (book 50%)  |  T2: Opposite side of range
SKIP IF: Strong trend in progress (don't fade strength) · No candle confirmation · S/R is fresh (needs ≥2 prior tests)
WHY IT WORKS: Well-tested S/R = high memory. Reversal candle at S/R = institutional absorption showing itself.
5 FLAG BREAKOUT (Momentum Continuation)
WHEN: After a strong momentum move (pole) + tight consolidation (flag) — best post-news or strong opening
SETUP: Identify pole (≥0.8% move in <30 min) + flag (5–12 candles, tight range, against trend slope)
ENTRY CE: Break of flag high + volume surge > 1.5× avg → Buy ATM CE (bull flag)
ENTRY PE: Break of flag low + volume → Buy ATM PE (bear flag)
SL: Opposite side of the flag (below flag low for CE / above flag high for PE)
TARGET: Pole length projected from the breakout point (measured-move rule)
SKIP IF: Flag > 15 candles (loses momentum) · Flag wider than 50% of pole height · No volume on break
WHY IT WORKS: Flags are pauses where smart money accumulates before the next leg. The tighter the flag, the bigger the follow-through.
7 BB SQUEEZE BREAKOUT (Volatility Expansion)
WHEN: Low-volatility day, BB Width at 4-week low, post-consolidation — best on BankNifty mornings
SETUP: Bollinger Bands (20, 2) visibly contracting for 10+ candles + BB Width indicator near its 4-week minimum + Keltner Channel (20, 1.5) fully engulfs the bands (official squeeze)
ENTRY CE: 15-min candle closes above upper band + volume > 1.5× avg + RSI > 55 → Buy ATM CE
ENTRY PE: 15-min candle closes below lower band + volume + RSI < 45 → Buy ATM PE
SL: Back inside the bands (specifically below middle band / 20 EMA for CE, above 20 EMA for PE)
T1: 1× the band-width at breakout (book 50%)  |  T2: 2× band-width or trail to 20 EMA
SKIP IF: Breakout after 2:00 PM (theta kills it) · No Keltner engulfment · Bands already expanding · >50% of the expected move already happened before your entry
WHY IT WORKS: Volatility mean-reverts. A tight squeeze builds compressed energy. The direction of the break is the direction of the next expansion — and expansion moves are the fastest, cleanest intraday moves available.

THE BOTTOM LINE — PICK ONE PLAYBOOK
The #1 mistake new traders make: trying all 6 strategies in the first week, mastering none. Real edge comes from repetition, not variety.

Our suggestion for most traders:
→ Week 1–4: Paper-trade ORB only. Log every setup (took / skipped / outcome).
→ Month 2: Add S/R Reversal when clear range-bound days appear.
→ Month 3+: Add VWAP Bounce and CPR Breakout.
→ Month 6+: Add Flag Breakout after you've mastered reading momentum.
→ Month 9+: Add BB Squeeze once you can identify true volatility contraction on the BB Width indicator.

Consistency with ONE strategy beats mediocrity across six. Every professional trader you admire trades 2–3 setups, not 10.
