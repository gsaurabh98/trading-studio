VOLUME ANALYSIS THE HIDDEN INDICATOR

What is volume? Volume is simply the NUMBER of shares (or contracts) that changed hands in a given time period. It is shown as bars below the price chart on every trading platform.

Why volume matters more than any indicator: Price tells you WHAT happened (it went up or down). Volume tells you WHY — how much real MONEY was actually behind that move.

The simple truth:
· Big move + Big volume = REAL move. Real money moved. Trust it.
· Big move + Small volume = FAKE move. Just a few traders pushing price. Do NOT trust it.

The most honest indicator: Unlike most indicators that can show false signals, volume CANNOT be faked. If money actually moved, the volume bars show it. This page covers the 6 volume tools every serious trader uses.

PRINCIPLE · PRICE AND VOLUME AGREEMENT
CONFIRMATION
WHY IT MATTERS: Every genuine price move needs real money behind it. Price and volume agreement is the single most reliable confirmation tool. Professional traders confirm everything against this rule before clicking the buy button.

HOW TO READ:
• Price up + Volume up = real buying. Trust the move.
• Price up + Volume down = a weak rally. A pullback is coming.
• Price down + Volume up = real selling (distribution). Trust the fall.
• Price down + Volume down = weak selling. A bounce is likely.

BUY CALL WHEN: A bullish breakout candle forms with volume greater than 1.5 times the 20-candle average, AND price is above the Volume Weighted Average Price line.
BUY PUT WHEN: A bearish breakdown candle forms with volume greater than 1.5 times the average AND price is below the Volume Weighted Average Price line.

STOP LOSS: The opposite extreme of the breakout candle.
TARGET: 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio, or the next support / resistance.
TIMEFRAME: Works on every timeframe — always pair it with price action.
AVOID: The last 30 minutes (rollover and settlement distorts volume). Also avoid expiry afternoons.
VOLUME CLIMAX (BLOW-OFF TOP OR SELLING EXHAUSTION)
REVERSAL WARNING
WHY IT MATTERS: The final desperate burst of a trend. Retail traders pile in at the very top, while professional traders exit into all that excitement. Spotting a climax early means catching the best reversal trade of the day.

HOW TO READ:
• A volume bar that is 2.5 to 4 times the recent average.
• This must come after an extended move, never at the start of one.
• The candle is usually large with a big wick on top or bottom (the wick proves exhaustion).

BUY PUT WHEN: A buying climax forms (a big green candle with volume 3 times or more the average) at a resistance level after an extended rally, AND the very next candle closes below the climax candle's low (the confirmation).
BUY CALL WHEN: A selling climax forms (a big red candle with volume 3 times or more the average) at a support level after an extended fall, AND the next candle closes above the climax candle's high.

STOP LOSS: Just beyond the climax candle's extreme (the high for a Put trade, the low for a Call trade).
TARGET: The previous swing level, or 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 5-minute or 15-minute for intraday. Daily for swing trades.
AVOID: Never enter on the climax candle itself. Always wait for the next candle's confirmation. About 60 percent of early entries get run over.
VOLUME DRY-UP AT SUPPORT OR RESISTANCE
PROFESSIONAL SIGNAL
WHY IT MATTERS: The absence of volume at a key level is just as informative as its presence. When the big players lose interest, price gets rejected — this is the signature of a high-probability reversal.

HOW TO READ:
• Price approaches a known support or resistance level.
• Volume drops below the 20-candle average (sometimes to less than 50 percent).
• A rejection candle (Pin Bar, Shooting Star, or Hammer) prints at that level.

BUY CALL WHEN: Price tests a known support level, AND volume dries up, AND a bullish Hammer or Bullish Engulfing candle closes above the rejection bar's low.
BUY PUT WHEN: Price tests a known resistance level, AND volume dries up, AND a bearish Shooting Star or Bearish Engulfing candle closes below the rejection bar's high.

STOP LOSS: 5 to 10 points beyond the rejection candle's extreme.
TARGET: The previous swing on the opposite side, or 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 5-minute or 15-minute for intraday.
AVOID: The lunch hour (12:00 PM to 1:30 PM IST) — all volume dries up during this time, so it is not meaningful.
ON-BALANCE VOLUME
ACCUMULATION GAUGE
WHY IT MATTERS: Tracks the cumulative buying versus selling pressure over time. Divergence between this indicator and price reveals hidden accumulation (smart money quietly loading up) or hidden distribution (smart money quietly exiting) before price actually shows it.

HOW TO READ:
• The formula is a running total: it adds volume on up-candles, and subtracts volume on down-candles.
• On-Balance Volume rising means the market is in an accumulation phase.
• On-Balance Volume falling means the market is in a distribution phase.
• Divergence between this line and price is the highest-probability reversal signal it gives.

BUY CALL WHEN: Price is in a sideways or downward phase, AND On-Balance Volume is trending up (this is hidden accumulation), AND a breakout candle forms above the range high with volume confirmation.
BUY PUT WHEN: Price is at the highs or sideways, AND On-Balance Volume is trending down (hidden distribution), AND a breakdown candle forms below the range with volume.

STOP LOSS: Just beyond the breakout or breakdown candle's extreme.
TARGET: The range height projected, or 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 15-minute up to daily. Too noisy on charts below 5 minutes.
AVOID: Using On-Balance Volume on its own — always pair it with price action at a meaningful support or resistance level.
ACCUMULATION / DISTRIBUTION LINE
SMART-MONEY TRACKER
WHY IT MATTERS: Smarter than On-Balance Volume. The Accumulation / Distribution Line uses where the candle closed within its high-to-low range to weight volume. A close near the high counts as real accumulation; a close near the low counts as real distribution. It catches stealth buying and selling that On-Balance Volume misses.

HOW TO READ:
• The Accumulation line rising while price is flat means silent accumulation (buyers are absorbing all the supply).
• The line falling while price is at the highs means distribution (smart money is exiting).
• A divergence usually resolves within 5 to 10 candles.

BUY CALL WHEN: The Accumulation / Distribution Line shows a bullish divergence at support, AND a bullish candle trigger forms, AND volume confirms. Especially powerful near a key support or resistance.
BUY PUT WHEN: The line shows a bearish divergence at resistance, AND a bearish candle, AND volume confirms.

STOP LOSS: Beyond the swing high or low that created the divergence.
TARGET: 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio, or the next major support / resistance.
TIMEFRAME: 15-minute, 1-hour, or daily.
AVOID: News-event days (the line gets distorted by panic flows). Also avoid the first 15 minutes (data is too thin).
VOLUME PROFILE · POINT OF CONTROL / VALUE AREA HIGH AND LOW
PRICE MAGNETS
WHY IT MATTERS: Shows you WHERE volume traded (horizontal axis), not when it traded. This reveals the true support and resistance via actual participation — not subjective lines you draw. The best professional-grade support / resistance tool that exists.

HOW TO READ:
• The Point of Control is the price with the most volume traded (the strongest magnet for price).
• The Value Area High is the upper edge of the zone where 70 percent of volume traded (acts as strong resistance).
• The Value Area Low is the lower edge of the 70 percent volume zone (acts as strong support).
• A Low Volume Node is a thin zone where price travels through fast.

BUY CALL WHEN:
• Breakout play: Price closes above the Value Area High with volume, plus a bullish candle.
• Mean-reversion play: A bullish rejection forms at the Value Area Low, plus a candle trigger.
BUY PUT WHEN:
• Breakout play: Price closes below the Value Area Low with volume, plus a bearish candle.
• Mean-reversion play: A bearish rejection forms at the Value Area High, plus a candle trigger.

STOP LOSS: The opposite side of the Value Area High / Low, or beyond the Point of Control.
TARGET: The Point of Control (for mean-reversion plays); the next Low Volume Node or the prior extreme (for breakout plays).
TIMEFRAME: Overlay yesterday's daily Volume Profile on today's intraday chart.
AVOID: The first 15 minutes (today's profile is still being built — use the prior day's profile until then).
PRICE-VOLUME DIVERGENCE (WEAK TREND)
WEAK RALLY · TRUST THE DIVERGENCE
WHY IT MATTERS: A trend cannot continue without fresh money joining it. When price makes new highs (or new lows) on declining volume, you are watching a trend on fumes — this is one of the most reliable reversal signals in all of technical analysis.

HOW TO READ:
• Price prints progressively higher highs, but each new high bar has less volume than the previous one.
• The opposite for downtrends: new lows on shrinking volume means selling has been exhausted.
• Apply this rule over 3 to 6 consecutive swing points.

BUY PUT WHEN: An uptrend shows a bearish volume divergence (price makes a higher high, but volume makes a lower high) at a resistance level, AND a bearish candle trigger forms, AND the close confirms.
BUY CALL WHEN: A downtrend shows a bullish volume divergence (price makes a lower low, but volume makes a lower low — meaning selling is exhausted) at support, AND a bullish candle trigger forms.

STOP LOSS: Just beyond the divergence's swing extreme.
TARGET: The previous swing on the opposite side, or 1 to 2 risk-to-reward ratio.
TIMEFRAME: 15-minute, 1-hour, or daily. Too noisy on 1-minute or 3-minute charts.
AVOID: Never add positions in the direction of a divergent trend. Wait for the actual reversal candle — divergence tells you that momentum has died, not when price will actually turn.
VOLUME RULES OF THUMB
A breakout needs 1.5 times average volume to be trusted.
A reversal candle needs 2 times average volume to be trusted.
The first 15-minute volume is the strongest signal of the day.
A last 30-minute volume spike is rollover or settlement flow — ignore it.
Lunch-hour (12:00 PM to 1:30 PM IST) low volume is normal — not a signal.
Compare today's volume to the 20-day average, not to yesterday's volume.
When to take this trade — Volume Dry-Up Reversal at Previous Day High (high-probability reversal)
Setup
Nifty rallies up into Previous Day High during the day. As it approaches that level, the volume on each 5-minute candle drops (each new bar has lower volume than the one before). A Shooting Star or Bearish Engulfing pattern forms exactly at Previous Day High.
Entry
Buy a Put Option (At the Money) when a 5-minute candle closes BELOW the rejection candle's low, with that confirmation candle's volume back above the 20-period average.
Stop Loss
10 points above the rejection candle's high (above Previous Day High). On the option premium, set a hard stop at 30 percent loss.
Target
The Volume Weighted Average Price line as the first target (book 50 percent there). Then Previous Day Low or Day Open as the final target. Trail the stop after a 50-point Nifty move.
Skip if
The volume dry-up happens during the lunch hour (12:00 PM to 1:30 PM) — that's normal low volume, not exhaustion, OR there is breaking news pushing price into Previous Day High (the dry-up means nothing in news-driven moves).
