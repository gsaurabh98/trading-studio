VOLUME ANALYSIS THE HIDDEN INDICATOR

Price tells you WHAT is happening, volume tells you WHY. A pattern without volume confirmation is just noise. Volume is the only indicator that cannot be faked — if money moved, volume shows it. This section covers the 6 volume-based tools every serious trader uses, with exact entry triggers.

HOW TO READ EACH INDICATOR CARD BELOW

  • WHY IT MATTERS — what edge it gives and why professionals use it
  • HOW TO READ — the 2–3 signals you actually watch for
  • BUY CE WHEN / BUY PE WHEN — the exact trigger conditions for a trade
  • SL / TARGET — where the stop and profit levels go
  • TIMEFRAME — where the indicator works best
  • AVOID — the market condition that breaks the signal
PRINCIPLE · PRICE-VOLUME AGREEMENT
CONFIRMATION
WHY IT MATTERS: Every genuine price move needs money behind it. Price + volume agreement is the single most reliable confirmation tool — professionals confirm everything against it before clicking buy.

HOW TO READ:
• Price up + Volume up = genuine buying (trust the move)
• Price up + Volume down = weak rally (fade incoming)
• Price down + Volume up = distribution (trust the fall)
• Price down + Volume down = weak selling (bounce likely)

BUY CE WHEN: Bullish breakout candle + volume > 1.5× 20-candle avg + price above VWAP.
BUY PE WHEN: Bearish breakdown candle + volume > 1.5× avg + price below VWAP.

SL: Opposite extreme of the breakout candle.
TARGET: 1:2 RR or next S/R.
TIMEFRAME: Works on every timeframe — always pair with price.
AVOID: Last 30 min (rollover/settlement distorts volume) · expiry afternoons.
VOLUME CLIMAX (BLOW-OFF TOP / SELLING EXHAUSTION)
REVERSAL WARNING
WHY IT MATTERS: The final burst of a trend. Retail piles in at the top; professionals exit into that euphoria. Spotting climax early = catching the best reversal of the day.

HOW TO READ:
• Volume bar 2.5–4× the recent average
• Comes after an extended move, not at the start
• Candle is usually a large body with a big wick (exhaustion)

BUY PE WHEN: Buying climax (big green candle, volume 3×+ avg) at a resistance/extended rally + next candle closes below the climax candle's low (confirmation).
BUY CE WHEN: Selling climax (big red candle, volume 3×+ avg) at support/extended fall + next candle closes above the climax candle's high.

SL: Beyond the climax candle's extreme (high for PE, low for CE).
TARGET: Prior swing level or 1:2 RR.
TIMEFRAME: 5-min / 15-min intraday · daily for swing.
AVOID: Never enter on the climax candle itself — always wait for the next-candle confirmation. 60% of early entries get run over.
VOLUME DRY-UP AT S/R (HIGH-PROB REVERSAL)
PRO SIGNAL
WHY IT MATTERS: The absence of volume at a key level is as informative as its presence. When big players lose interest, price rejects — this is the signature of a high-probability reversal.

HOW TO READ:
• Price approaches a known S/R level
• Volume drops below the 20-candle average (sometimes to < 50%)
• A rejection candle (pin bar / shooting star / hammer) prints at that level

BUY CE WHEN: Price tests known support + volume dry-up + bullish hammer / engulfing close above the rejection bar's low.
BUY PE WHEN: Price tests known resistance + volume dry-up + bearish shooting star / engulfing close below the rejection bar's high.

SL: 5–10 pts beyond the rejection candle's extreme.
TARGET: Previous swing (opposite side) or 1:2 RR.
TIMEFRAME: 5-min / 15-min intraday.
AVOID: Lunch hour (12:00–13:30) — all volume dries up, not meaningful.
OBV — ON-BALANCE VOLUME
ACCUMULATION GAUGE
WHY IT MATTERS: Tracks cumulative buying vs selling pressure. Divergence vs price reveals hidden accumulation (smart money quietly loading) or distribution (quietly exiting) before price shows it.

HOW TO READ:
• Formula: running total — adds volume on up-candles, subtracts on down-candles
• OBV rising = accumulation phase
• OBV falling = distribution phase
• Divergence vs price = highest-probability reversal signal

BUY CE WHEN: Price in sideways/down phase + OBV trending UP (hidden accumulation) + breakout candle above range high + volume confirmation.
BUY PE WHEN: Price at highs/sideways + OBV trending DOWN (hidden distribution) + breakdown candle below range + volume.

SL: Beyond the breakout/breakdown candle's extreme.
TARGET: Range height projected / 1:2 RR.
TIMEFRAME: 15-min to daily. Noisy on < 5-min.
AVOID: Using OBV alone — always pair with price action at a meaningful level.
A/D LINE — ACCUMULATION / DISTRIBUTION
SMART-MONEY TRACKER
WHY IT MATTERS: Smarter than OBV. A/D uses where the candle closed within its H–L range to weight volume — a close near the high = real accumulation; near the low = real distribution. Catches stealth buying/selling OBV misses.

HOW TO READ:
• A/D rising + price flat = silent accumulation (buyers absorbing supply)
• A/D falling + price at highs = distribution (smart money exiting)
• Divergence often resolves within 5–10 candles

BUY CE WHEN: A/D bullish divergence at support + bullish candle trigger + volume confirmation. Especially powerful near key S/R.
BUY PE WHEN: A/D bearish divergence at resistance + bearish candle + volume.

SL: Beyond the divergence swing high/low.
TARGET: 1:2 RR or next major S/R.
TIMEFRAME: 15-min / 1-hour / daily.
AVOID: News-event days (A/D distorted by panic flows) · first 15 min (data thin).
VOLUME PROFILE · POC / VAH / VAL
PRICE MAGNETS
WHY IT MATTERS: Shows WHERE volume traded (horizontal axis), not when. Reveals true support/resistance via actual participation — not subjective lines. The best pro-grade S/R tool in existence.

HOW TO READ:
• POC (Point of Control) = price with the most volume (strongest magnet)
• VAH = upper edge of 70% volume zone (strong resistance)
• VAL = lower edge of 70% volume zone (strong support)
• LVN (low-volume node) = thin zones where price travels fast

BUY CE WHEN:
• Breakout: Price closes above VAH with volume + bullish candle
• Mean-reversion: Bullish rejection at VAL + candle trigger
BUY PE WHEN:
• Breakout: Price closes below VAL with volume + bearish candle
• Mean-reversion: Bearish rejection at VAH + candle trigger

SL: Opposite side of VAH/VAL or beyond POC.
TARGET: POC (mean-reversion) · next LVN / prior extreme (breakout).
TIMEFRAME: Overlay yesterday's daily profile for today's intraday trading.
AVOID: First 15 min (today's profile still forming — use prior day's).
PRICE-VOLUME DIVERGENCE (WEAK TREND)
WEAK RALLY · TRUST THE DIVERGENCE
WHY IT MATTERS: A trend cannot continue without fresh money. When price makes new highs/lows on declining volume, you are watching a trend on fumes — one of the most reliable reversal signals in all of technical analysis.

HOW TO READ:
• Price prints progressive highs, but each new high bar has less volume than the prior
• Conversely for downtrends: new lows on shrinking volume = selling exhausted
• Applies over 3–6 consecutive swing points

BUY PE WHEN: Uptrend shows bearish volume divergence (price higher-high, volume lower-high) at a resistance + bearish candle trigger + confirmation close.
BUY CE WHEN: Downtrend shows bullish volume divergence (price lower-low, volume lower-low → selling exhausted) at support + bullish candle trigger.

SL: Beyond the divergence swing extreme.
TARGET: Prior swing (opposite end) or 1:2 RR.
TIMEFRAME: 15-min / 1-hour / daily. Too noisy on 1-min/3-min.
AVOID: Never add positions in the direction of a divergent trend. Wait for the reversal candle — divergence tells you momentum is dead, not when price will turn.
VOLUME RULES OF THUMB
Breakout needs 1.5× avg volume to trust
Reversal candle needs 2× avg volume to trust
First 15-min volume = strongest signal for the day
Last 30-min volume spike = rollover/settle flow, ignore
Lunch-hour (12:00–13:30) low volume = normal, not a signal
Compare to 20-day average, not yesterday's
