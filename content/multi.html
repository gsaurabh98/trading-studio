MULTI-CANDLE PATTERNS 10 PATTERNS

What counts as "multi-candle": A curated set of rarer, book-canonical patterns — most are 3- to 5-candle reversals (with a couple of 2-candle cousins included here because they belong to the same classical family). Because of how specific their structure is, they have the highest statistical reliability when they do appear (Bulkowski rates several of them above 80% at key S/R).

Downside for intraday traders: They are rare on 5-min and 15-min charts. You will see them most often on daily / swing timeframes. When you DO spot one intraday at a major level, size up — these deliver the highest R:R of any pattern category.

MULTI-CANDLE EXHAUSTION & WARNING
Patterns that play out over 3–5 candles. These take longer to form, which is precisely why they are more reliable — the market is showing its hand slowly. Excellent for swing setups on daily / 60-min charts.
LADDER BOTTOM TOP (BULL)
5-CANDLE REVERSAL
Three bear steps, 4th with upper wick (buyers emerging), 5th big green = bottom formed.
→ BUY CE
DOJI STAR
WARNING SIGNAL
A doji that gaps away from prior candle. Signals exhaustion. Next candle determines direction.
→ WAIT for next
SHOOTING STAR GAP
STRONG BEARISH
Shooting star that gaps above prior candle. Even stronger bearish reversal signal.
