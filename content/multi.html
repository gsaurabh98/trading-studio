MULTI-CANDLE PATTERNS 10 PATTERNS

What counts as a "multi-candle" pattern: A small set of rare, classic textbook patterns — most are 3 to 5 candle reversals (with a couple of 2-candle cousins included here because they belong to the same classical family). Because their structure is so specific, these patterns have the highest statistical reliability when they actually appear (the trading author Bulkowski rates several of them above 80 percent reliability at major support and resistance levels).

The downside for intraday traders: These patterns are rare on 5-minute and 15-minute charts. You will see them most often on the daily and swing timeframes. When you DO spot one intraday at a major level, increase your position size — these patterns deliver the highest risk-to-reward ratio of any pattern category.
RARE REVERSAL PATTERNS
THREE STARS IN THE SOUTH
BULLISH REVERSAL
Three red candles in a row, each one smaller than the previous one. The selling pressure is exhausting itself — a reversal is near.
→ BUY CALL
TWO CROWS
BEARISH REVERSAL
A green candle, then a small red candle that gaps up, then a bigger red candle that engulfs the small one. A bearish top signal.
→ BUY PUT
UPSIDE GAP TWO CROWS
BEARISH REVERSAL
A green candle, then two red candles that both gap up, with the second one engulfing the first. The sellers are overwhelming the buyers.
→ BUY PUT
STICK SANDWICH
BULLISH REVERSAL
Red, then Green, then Red — with both red candles closing at the same price. The support level has been confirmed as solid.
→ BUY CALL
HOMING PIGEON
BULLISH REVERSAL
A small red candle's body sits entirely inside a larger red candle's body. It is essentially a Bullish Harami pattern made of two red candles.
→ BUY CALL
CONCEALING BABY SWALLOW
RARE REVERSAL
Four red candles in a row, where the last one engulfs a wicked third candle. The selling pressure is completely exhausted.
→ BUY CALL
When to take this trade — Three Stars in the South (rare but powerful)
Setup
Three red candles in a row, each one progressively smaller than the previous. The pattern forms after a clear downtrend at a strong support level (such as a daily swing low or weekly support). Volume is shrinking on each successive candle (showing exhausted sellers).
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) only after the next candle (the fourth candle) closes green and above the high of the third (smallest) red candle.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points below the low of the very first (largest) red candle. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 40 percent.
Target
Aim for the next major resistance level above. Minimum target is 2.5 times your stop loss distance. Three Stars patterns often signal a 5 to 15 candle reversal, so book half at the first target and trail the stop loss on the rest.
Skip if
The candles are not progressively smaller (then it is not a valid pattern), volume is growing instead of shrinking, the pattern forms in the middle of nowhere with no clear support, or the broader daily trend is strongly downward.
EXTENDED REVERSAL PATTERNS (4 to 5 candles)
BULLISH BREAKAWAY
5-CANDLE REVERSAL
Four red candles stepping lower, then a big green reversal candle. A panic-selling bottom that exhausted sellers.
→ BUY CALL
BEARISH BREAKAWAY
5-CANDLE REVERSAL
Four green candles stepping higher, then a big red reversal candle. A euphoric top where buyers got exhausted.
→ BUY PUT
LADDER BOTTOM
5-CANDLE REVERSAL
Three red candles stepping down, then a fourth red candle with an upper wick (buyers starting to push back), then a big green candle. A rare bottom reversal.
→ BUY CALL
KICKING (BULLISH)
MOST POWERFUL
A Bearish Marubozu candle, then a gap up, then a Bullish Marubozu candle. A complete reversal of market sentiment. Very rare.
→ STRONG BUY CALL
KICKING (BEARISH)
MOST POWERFUL
A Bullish Marubozu candle, then a gap down, then a Bearish Marubozu candle. A complete reversal of market sentiment. Very rare.
→ STRONG BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Bullish Kicking Pattern (the most powerful reversal)
Setup
The first candle is a full Bearish Marubozu (a strong red candle with no wicks). The next candle gaps UP and is a full Bullish Marubozu (a strong green candle with no wicks). There is a clear visible gap between the two candles' bodies. Volume on the second candle is at least 2 times the recent average.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the second candle. This pattern is so powerful that you do not wait for confirmation — the gap and full Marubozu IS the confirmation. Use a market order if needed; the slippage is worth it.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss below the low of the second (green) Marubozu candle. If price drops back to fill the gap, the entire reversal has failed.
Target
Aim for at least 3 times your stop loss distance. Kicking patterns usually trigger multi-day moves, so consider holding for 3 to 5 days if the pattern formed on the daily chart. Book one-third at 1 times risk, one-third at 2 times risk, and trail the rest.
Skip if
The gap closes within the first 30 minutes of the next session (failed kicking), the second candle has visible wicks (then it is not a true Marubozu), or volume on the second candle is below average (no real conviction behind the move).
MULTI-CANDLE EXHAUSTION AND WARNING
Patterns that play out over 3 to 5 candles. These take longer to form, which is exactly why they are more reliable — the market is showing its hand slowly. Excellent for swing setups on daily and 60-minute charts.
LADDER BOTTOM TOP (BULLISH)
5-CANDLE REVERSAL
Three bearish steps, then a fourth candle with an upper wick (showing buyers stepping in), then a fifth big green candle. The bottom has formed.
→ BUY CALL
DOJI STAR
WARNING SIGNAL
A Doji that gaps away from the previous candle. Signals exhaustion. The next candle decides the direction.
→ WAIT for next candle
SHOOTING STAR WITH GAP
STRONG BEARISH
A Shooting Star candle that gaps above the previous candle. An even stronger bearish reversal signal than a regular Shooting Star.
→ BUY PUT
