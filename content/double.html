TWO-CANDLE PATTERNS 16 PATTERNS

Why two candles are better than one: A single candle can fool you. The second candle is the confirmation that the move is real. In an Engulfing pattern, the second candle must completely cover the first candle's body. In a Piercing Line, the second candle must close above the midpoint of the first one. This "two-candle rule" filters out many of the false signals that single candles can produce.

How to use these patterns: Wait for the second candle to fully close before taking action. Enter on the open of the next candle. Place your stop loss at the lowest point of the whole pattern (for bullish setups) or the highest point of the whole pattern (for bearish setups).
ENGULFING FAMILY
BULLISH ENGULFING
BULLISH REVERSAL
A green candle's body completely covers (engulfs) the previous red candle's body. A powerful reversal signal at a support level.
→ BUY CALL
BEARISH ENGULFING
BEARISH REVERSAL
A red candle's body completely covers (engulfs) the previous green candle's body. A powerful reversal signal at a resistance level.
→ BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Bullish Engulfing at Support (the gold standard)
Setup
A clear Bullish Engulfing pattern has formed at a known support level (such as the previous day's low, the 20 period Exponential Moving Average, or a Volume Weighted Average Price line). The green engulfing candle's body fully covers the red candle's body, AND it closes above the red candle's open. Volume on the green candle is at least 1.5 times the average of the last 10 candles.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the very next candle after the engulfing candle closes. Do not wait for a pullback — engulfing patterns often run hard for the next 3 to 5 candles.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points below the lowest point of the entire two-candle pattern on Nifty. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 35 percent of the option price.
Target
Aim for the next major resistance level above. The minimum target is 2 times your stop loss distance. Book half the position at the first target and trail the stop loss on the rest.
Skip if
The engulfing forms in the middle of nowhere (no clear support nearby), volume on the green candle is below average, the green candle has a long upper wick (sellers were still active), or the broader market trend is strongly downward.
HARAMI FAMILY
BULLISH HARAMI
BULLISH REVERSAL
A small green body sits inside the body of a large red candle. The selling pressure is slowing down. Always wait for the next candle to confirm before taking the trade.
→ CALL (after confirm)
BEARISH HARAMI
BEARISH REVERSAL
A small red body sits inside the body of a large green candle. The buying pressure is slowing down. Always wait for the next candle to confirm before taking the trade.
→ PUT (after confirm)
BULLISH HARAMI CROSS
STRONG REVERSAL
A Doji candle sits inside the body of a large red candle. This is even stronger than a regular Bullish Harami because the Doji shows complete indecision after the big red candle.
→ BUY CALL
BEARISH HARAMI CROSS
STRONG REVERSAL
A Doji candle sits inside the body of a large green candle. Even stronger than a regular Bearish Harami because the Doji signals complete indecision after the big green candle.
→ BUY PUT
PIERCING / COVER FAMILY
PIERCING LINE
BULLISH REVERSAL
A green candle opens below the previous red candle's low, then closes above the midpoint of the red candle. A partial bullish recovery showing buyers are stepping in.
→ BUY CALL
DARK CLOUD COVER
BEARISH REVERSAL
A red candle opens above the previous green candle's high, then closes below the midpoint of the green candle. The mirror image of the Piercing Line.
→ BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Piercing Line at Support
Setup
A Piercing Line pattern has formed at a known support level. The green candle opens below the previous red candle's low (showing initial weakness), but recovers strongly to close above the midpoint of the red candle. Volume on the green candle is above average.
Entry
Buy a Call Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the next candle, but only if that next candle opens at or above the close of the green candle.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points below the lowest point of the green candle. For the option premium, exit if you lose 30 to 40 percent.
Target
Aim for the next resistance level. Minimum target is 2 times your stop loss distance. Piercing Line is slightly less reliable than a full Bullish Engulfing, so consider booking full profit at 2 times risk rather than holding for more.
Skip if
The green candle does not close above the midpoint of the red candle (then it is not a real Piercing Line), volume is below average, or the next candle gaps down below the green candle's close.
TWEEZER FAMILY
TWEEZER BOTTOM
BULLISH REVERSAL
Two candles share the exact same low price. The support level was tested twice and held both times — a strong floor.
→ BUY CALL
TWEEZER TOP
BEARISH REVERSAL
Two candles share the exact same high price. The resistance level was tested twice and rejected both times — a strong ceiling.
→ BUY PUT
When to take this trade — Tweezer Top at Resistance
Setup
Two candles in a row have made nearly the exact same high price (within 5 points on Nifty). The level matches a known resistance (such as previous day high, a swing high from earlier, or a round number). Ideally the second candle is red and rejected the high more strongly.
Entry
Buy a Put Option (At the Money or one strike Out of the Money) on the open of the third candle, only if it opens at or below the second candle's close.
Stop Loss
Place the stop loss 5 to 10 points above the matching highs. If the third candle pushes above this level, the resistance has broken and your trade thesis is wrong.
Target
Aim for the nearest support level below. Minimum target is 2 times your stop loss distance. Tweezer reversals usually start a clean down move that lasts 3 to 5 candles.
Skip if
The two highs are not close enough (more than 10 points apart on Nifty), there is no clear resistance at this level, or the volume on both candles was very low.
NECK / GAP PATTERNS (TREND CONTINUATION)
ON-NECK PATTERN
BEARISH CONTINUATION
A small green candle closes exactly at the previous red candle's low. The buyers tried but failed. Sellers remain in control.
→ BUY PUT
IN-NECK PATTERN
BEARISH CONTINUATION
Like the On-Neck pattern, but the green candle closes slightly inside the red candle's body. The downtrend is still intact.
→ BUY PUT
THRUSTING PATTERN
BEARISH CONTINUATION
A green candle closes below the midpoint of the previous red candle — the recovery was not deep enough to reverse the trend. Downtrend continues.
→ BUY PUT
MATCHING LOW
BULLISH REVERSAL
Two consecutive red candles close at the same low price. Buyers are actively defending the level and refusing to let price drop further.
→ BUY CALL

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Bullish and Bearish Engulfing — The gold-standard two-candle reversals. The second candle must completely cover the body of the first AND close beyond it. Reliability is above 70 percent when this happens at major support or resistance.
  • Piercing Line and Dark Cloud Cover — The "partial engulfing" cousins. Slightly lower reliability than full engulfing patterns, but still strong when they appear at support or resistance with above-average volume.
  • Tweezer Top and Tweezer Bottom — Easy to spot (just two matching wicks) and excellent at confirmed support and resistance levels. Very common at the previous day's high or low.
The squint test: Picture closing your eyes and reopening them. Can you still clearly see the pattern on the chart? If you have to squint or force-fit the pattern, skip the trade. Clean, obvious two-candle patterns are the ones that actually make money.
