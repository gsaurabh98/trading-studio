TWO-CANDLE PATTERNS 16 PATTERNS

Why two candles are better than one: A single candle can mislead — the second candle is the confirmation. In patterns like Engulfing, the second candle must completely cover the first candle's body. In Piercing Line, it must close above the midpoint of the first. This "two-candle rule" filters out many false signals that single candles can produce.

How to use: Wait for the second candle to close before acting. Enter on the next candle's open. Place SL at the low (for bullish) or high (for bearish) of the pattern as a whole.

BEGINNER PRIORITY — MASTER THESE FIRST

  • Bullish & Bearish Engulfing — the gold-standard two-candle reversals. Second candle must fully engulf the first body + close beyond it. Reliability > 70% at major S/R.
  • Piercing Line / Dark Cloud Cover — the "partial engulfing" cousins. Lower reliability than full engulfing but still strong when at support/resistance with volume.
  • Tweezer Top / Bottom — easy to spot (two matching wicks) and excellent at confirmed S/R levels. Common at previous day high / low.
Key test: Close the chart in your head — can you still see the pattern? If you need to squint or force-fit, skip it. Clean, obvious two-candle patterns are the profitable ones.
