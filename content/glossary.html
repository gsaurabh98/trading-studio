GLOSSARY & ACRONYMS QUICK REFERENCE

A – C
A/D Line — Accumulation/Distribution line indicator ADX — Average Directional Index (trend strength) AMO — After Market Order ATM — At-the-Money strike (nearest to spot) ATR — Average True Range (volatility measure) BB — Bollinger Bands BC — Bottom Central (of CPR) BO / CO — Bracket Order / Cover Order BOS — Break of Structure (price-action concept) BTST — Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow Call Option (CE) — Contract giving the buyer the right (not obligation) to BUY the underlying at the strike price on/before expiry. Buy CE when you expect price to go UP. CE — Call European (CALL option) — the style used in Indian index options; exercisable only on expiry. CHOCH — Change of Character (trend shift) CPR — Central Pivot Range. Three-line zone (TC, PP, BC) computed from prev-day H/L/C; used for day-type forecast and directional bias. CPR (Inverted / Flipped) — CPR where TC plots below BC, formed when prev-day Close < (H+L)/2. Broadcasts a bearish structural tone for today; bias flips only on a clean close above BC with volume. CTT — Commodities Transaction Tax
D – H
Delta — Option Greek: price sensitivity to spot DII — Domestic Institutional Investors DOJI — Candle where open = close DP — Depository Participant Dead Cat Bounce — After a sudden sharp decline (>15–20%), price bounces 30–50% of the drop on short-covering and bargain-hunting, then rolls over to a new lower low. Classic bear trap — fade the bounce with PE, target prior-drop low minus 0.5× drop height. DTE — Days To Expiry EMA — Exponential Moving Average Expiration Date — The date a derivative contract becomes invalid and settles. Weekly: Nifty (Tue), Sensex (Thu). Monthly: BankNifty / FinNifty — last Tue. After this date the option ceases to exist; time value = 0. Explosion Gap Pivot — A runaway (mid-trend) gap whose gap-low (for up-gaps) or gap-high (for down-gaps) becomes a pivotal support/resistance. While the level holds, trend is confirmed; target = 2× the distance from prior breakout to the gap. F&O — Futures and Options Failed Breakout — Kirkpatrick standard: a breakout that fails AND price subsequently breaks the OPPOSITE boundary of the pattern. Produces outsized reversal moves (~68% follow-through, ~1.5× measured target). Distinct from False Breakout (quick fade, no opposite-side break). False Breakout — Price breaks a pattern level then returns back inside within 1–5 bars. No opposite-side break. A range-bound outcome — fade the trap, target opposite range edge. FII — Foreign Institutional Investors FVG — Fair Value Gap Gamma — Rate of change of delta Gift Nifty — Nifty futures on NSE IX (GIFT City) GTT — Good Till Triggered order H&S — Head & Shoulders pattern HFT — High-Frequency Trading High-Tight Flag — O'Neil's most explosive bullish continuation pattern. Stock rallies ≥90% in 4–8 weeks (pole), then consolidates tightly (<25% correction) for 3–5 weeks (flag). Break above flag high = resumption; target = pole length projected up, often exceeded 2–3×.
I – M
Inside Bar — A candle whose entire range (high and low) is inside the previous candle's range. A "mother" bar contains one or more inside bars. Signals consolidation; trade the break of the mother bar's high/low. IOC — Immediate-or-Cancel order ITM — In-the-Money IV — Implied Volatility LTP — Last Traded Price Long Build-up — Price ↑ + OI ↑ (bullish) Long Unwinding — Price ↓ + OI ↓ MACD — Moving Average Convergence Divergence Max Pain — Strike where max option buyers lose at expiry MCX — Multi Commodity Exchange Measured Move — Projected minimum price target of a chart pattern, calculated by projecting the pattern's height from the breakout point in the break direction. See CHART PATTERNS → TARGET-PRICE FORMULA REFERENCE for every pattern's formula. MidCpNifty — Nifty Midcap 150 (weekly discontinued 2024) MTF — Margin Trading Facility MTM — Mark to Market MPC — RBI Monetary Policy Committee
N – R
NR4 / NR7 — Narrow Range 4 / 7: a bar whose range (high−low) is the narrowest of the prior 4 / 7 bars. Signals volatility contraction — expansion (big move) typically follows. Trade the break direction with OCO stops above/below the NR bar. NSE / BSE — National / Bombay Stock Exchange OBV — On-Balance Volume indicator OI — Open Interest ORB — Opening Range Breakout OTM — Out-of-the-Money PCR — Put-Call Ratio PDC / PDH / PDL — Previous Day Close / High / Low PE — Put European (PUT option) — Indian index option style; exercisable only on expiry. Pipe Bottom / Top — Two adjacent tall candles with near-identical lows (Pipe Bottom) or highs (Pipe Top) that protrude beyond surrounding price. Bulkowski's highest-reliability two-bar reversal (~77% follow-through weekly). Confirmed by 3rd-candle close beyond opposite pipe edge. POC — Point of Control (volume profile) POA — Power of Attorney PP — Pivot Point Premium — Price paid by the option buyer to the seller (writer) for the contract. Quoted per share; total cost = Premium × Lot Size. Premium = Intrinsic Value + Time Value. Pullback — After a downside breakout, price rallies back up to retest the broken support (now resistance) before resuming the decline. Second-chance entry — BUY PE at the retest rejection. Put Option (PE) — Contract giving the buyer the right (not obligation) to SELL the underlying at the strike price on/before expiry. Buy PE when you expect price to go DOWN. R1–R3 · S1–S3 — Pivot resistance / support levels Rectangle Shortfall — Inside a rectangle/range, price fails to reach the opposite boundary on successive swings — each high gets lower (or each low higher). Warns that a trend is forming inside the range in the direction of shortfall; precedes the eventual break ~70% of the time. Rho — Option Greek: interest-rate sensitivity RSI — Relative Strength Index
S – V
SEBI — Securities & Exchange Board of India SGX Nifty — Former name of Gift Nifty Short Build-up — Price ↓ + OI ↑ (bearish) Short Covering — Price ↑ + OI ↓ SL / SL-M / SL-L — Stop-Loss / Stop-Loss Market / Limit SMA — Simple Moving Average SMC — Smart Money Concepts S/R — Support & Resistance STBT — Sell Today, Buy Tomorrow STCG / LTCG — Short / Long-Term Capital Gains Strike Price — Pre-decided price at which the option can be exercised. Chosen from the option chain. Strike relative to spot defines moneyness: ATM (strike = spot), ITM (already profitable), OTM (needs move to profit). STT — Securities Transaction Tax TC — Top Central (of CPR) Theta — Option Greek: time-decay rate Throwback — After an upside breakout, price dips back down to retest the broken resistance (now support) before resuming the rally. Second-chance entry — BUY CE at the retest bounce. TSL — Trailing Stop-Loss UCC — Unique Client Code
V – Z & SYMBOLS
VAH / VAL — Value Area High / Low (volume profile) Vega — Option Greek: IV sensitivity VIX — India Volatility Index VWAP — Volume-Weighted Average Price Wasting Asset — Term for any instrument that loses value with time passage alone. Options are classic wasting assets — every day of holding costs you premium even if underlying doesn't move (theta decay). Writer (Option Writer) — The seller of an option contract. Receives the premium upfront. Max profit = premium received; max loss = unlimited (CE) or capped at strike × lot (PE). Typically institutions / HNIs — writing requires margin retail rarely has, which structurally favours the writer side. 0 DTE — Zero Days To Expiry (expiry day) 0-DTE trading — Buying/selling options expiring same day ₹ (INR) — Indian Rupee 1:N R:R — Risk-Reward ratio (SL:Target) R:R ≥ 2:1 — Minimum acceptable R:R → CE / → PE — Call / Put directional action ATR(14) — ATR over 14 periods EMA(20/50/200) — 20/50/200-period EMAs RSI(14) — RSI over 14 periods
