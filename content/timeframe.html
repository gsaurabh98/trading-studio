TIMEFRAME STRATEGY MULTI-TF

What is a "timeframe"? A timeframe is the time each candle on your chart represents. A 5-minute timeframe means each candle = 5 minutes of price action. A 1-hour timeframe means each candle = 1 hour. The chart itself is the same Nifty — only the "zoom level" changes.

Why this page matters: Most beginners stare at ONE chart (usually the 5-minute or 1-minute) and lose money because they cannot SEE the bigger picture. The fix is to look at THREE charts together — a big-picture chart, a signal chart, and an entry chart. Pros call this "multi-timeframe analysis". Sounds fancy, but the idea is simple: zoom OUT to find direction, zoom IN to find entry.
THE 4 TIMEFRAMES — WHAT EACH ONE IS FOR
60 MIN (1 HOUR)
TREND DIRECTION
This is your TREND timeframe. Identify whether the market is bullish, bearish, or sideways. Only trade in this direction on lower timeframes.
15 MIN
SIGNAL TIMEFRAME
Look for candle patterns here. This is your primary signal timeframe. Identify Support and Resistance levels, spot the patterns, confirm with volume.
5 MIN
ENTRY TIMEFRAME
Fine-tune your entry here. After the 15-minute timeframe gives a signal, wait for the 5-minute confirmation candle to enter with a tighter stop loss.
1 MIN
AVOID FOR OPTIONS
Too much noise. False signals everywhere. 1-minute patterns are unreliable for option buying. Use only for scalping futures.
THE 3-SCREEN SYSTEM (ELDER'S METHOD)
SCREEN 1 — 60 MIN
Ask: What is the TREND?
Price above the 20-period Exponential Moving Average → Uptrend → Only buy Call Options.
Price below the 20-period Exponential Moving Average → Downtrend → Only buy Put Options.
Price at the 20-period Exponential Moving Average → Sideways → Avoid trading.
SCREEN 2 — 15 MIN
Ask: What is the SIGNAL?
Look for candle pattern at S&R
Must align with 60-min trend
Volume should confirm
SCREEN 3 — 5 MIN
Ask: Where to ENTER?
Wait for confirmation candle
Enter on next candle open
Place SL below/above signal candle wick

WHICH TIMEFRAME SHOULD YOU TRADE OPTIONS ON?
The #1 mistake option buyers make: trading on a timeframe that doesn't match their style, attention span, or capital. Match the timeframe to YOU, not the other way around.
1-MIN CHART — SCALPING
DO NOT USE FOR OPTIONS
Hold time: 1–5 minutes
Target premium move: ₹3–8
Problem: Option bid-ask spread (₹1–3) + STT + brokerage eats ₹15–25 of your ₹5 target. Mathematically losing.
Noise ratio: Extreme — 70% of patterns are fake.

Verdict: Only for futures scalpers with co-location. Options on 1-min = gambling.
5-MIN CHART — INTRADAY SCALP
ONLY FOR EXPERIENCED
Hold time: 10–45 minutes
Target premium move: ₹15–40
Best for: Breakout trades AFTER a 15-min signal. Fine-tuning entry.
Use case: You've spotted a 15-min bullish engulfing at support → zoom to 5-min → wait for a bullish confirmation candle → enter.

Verdict: Tactical support tool, NOT your primary decision TF.
15-MIN CHART — SWEET SPOT
BEST FOR OPTION BUYERS
Hold time: 45 min – 3 hours
Target premium move: ₹40–120
Why best: Enough candles for patterns to be statistically meaningful. Enough movement to clear costs comfortably. Enough time between candles to think clearly.
Theta impact: Manageable — most trades complete before decay accelerates.

Verdict: Start here. 80% of your intraday option trades should come from 15-min signals.
60-MIN CHART — SWING STYLE
POSITIONAL INTRADAY
Hold time: 2 hours – full day / overnight*
Target premium move: ₹100–300
Best for: Strong trend days (e.g. after RBI policy, major news), breakouts from multi-day ranges, ITM option swing trades.
Theta warning: Holding options overnight near weekly expiry = dangerous. Use monthly or ITM options.

Verdict: For high-conviction setups only.
DAILY CHART — SWING TRADING
MULTI-DAY HOLDS
Hold time: 2–10 days
Target premium move: ₹200–1000+
Best for: ITM options or next-month expiry. NEVER weekly ATM — theta destroys you overnight.
Position size: Half of intraday size since you're exposed to overnight gaps.

Verdict: For traders with capital + patience.
WEEKLY CHART — MACRO VIEW
TREND READING
Don't trade options on it. Use weekly chart only to understand the macro trend that gives context to your daily / 60-min / 15-min trade.

Rule: Never take a trade against the weekly trend unless it's a proven reversal setup (H&S, Island Reversal, etc.).

HOW TO DECIDE YOUR OWN TIMEFRAME (DECISION TREE)
Q1 — HOW MUCH TIME DO YOU HAVE AT THE SCREEN?
• All day (full-time): 15-min signal TF, 5-min entry TF
• 2–3 hours: 15-min signal, 15-min entry (place orders, step away)
• <1 hour (checking phone): Daily chart swing trades only — do NOT intraday
Q2 — HOW DO YOU REACT TO NOISE / CANDLE JIGGLES?
• Panic on every red candle: Go higher TF (60-min or daily). Less noise = less emotion.
• Bored waiting for 15-min candle to close: Either stay with 15-min and build discipline, OR move to 5-min if experienced.
• Calm either way: 15-min is your sweet spot.
Q3 — WHAT'S YOUR CAPITAL? (POST-65-LOT NIFTY)
• < ₹1,00,000: Paper trade — real capital too small after SEBI changes
• ₹1–2 L: 15-min intraday ATM only, 1 lot Nifty
• ₹2–5 L: 15-min intraday + occasional 60-min swings, 1–2 lots
• ₹5 L+: Can use any TF. Scale lot with edge proven over 6 months.
Q4 — WHAT'S YOUR LOSS TOLERANCE PER TRADE?
• ₹500–2000 per trade: 15-min, ATM, current week
• ₹2000–5000: 15-min or 60-min, ATM / 1 ITM
• ₹5000+: You need a 60-min or daily TF with room to breathe, otherwise SL hits noise
• Rule: SL distance (in points) must be ≥ 1.5× ATR of your signal TF. If your SL is tighter, move to a smaller TF.

THE DEFINITIVE TIMEFRAME ANSWER FOR 90 PERCENT OF READERS

  • Use the 60-minute chart to read the trend — is the market bullish, bearish, or sideways today?
  • Use the 15-minute chart to find your signal — a candle pattern + volume + support/resistance confluence + Volume Weighted Average Price alignment.
  • Use the 5-minute chart to refine the entry — wait for the confirmation candle on the 5-minute chart after the 15-minute signal candle completes.
  • Hold for 30 minutes to 3 hours — most 15-minute signal option trades resolve in this window.
  • Stop looking at the 1-minute chart — it will only make you panic-exit a good trade, or enter a bad one.
  • Exit by 3:00 PM regardless of the trade — Theta decay accelerates sharply in the last 30 minutes.
When to take this trade — Multi-Timeframe Confluence Entry (the highest-probability intraday setup)
Setup
The 60-minute chart shows Nifty trending up (price above the 20-period Exponential Moving Average). The 15-minute chart shows a Bullish Engulfing candle at a key support level (Previous Day High, the Volume Weighted Average Price, or the top of the Central Pivot Range), with volume above 1.5 times the 20-period average. The 5-minute chart shows a clean follow-through candle confirming the move.
Entry
Buy an At The Money Nifty Call Option of the current week or next week's expiry, on the open of the next 5-minute candle. Use a limit order at the mid-price.
Stop Loss
5 points below the 15-minute Bullish Engulfing candle's low. On the option premium, set a hard stop at 30 percent loss.
Target
Book 50 percent of the position at the next 15-minute swing high. Trail the rest with a 5-minute Volume Weighted Average Price stop. Exit the entire position by 3:00 PM — no exceptions.
Skip if
The 60-minute trend disagrees with your trade direction (you must NOT fight the higher timeframe), OR the time is during the lunch-hour lull (11:30 AM to 1:00 PM IST), OR the 5-minute confirmation candle is a Doji (signals indecision, not direction).
