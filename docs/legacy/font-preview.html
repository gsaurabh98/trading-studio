Morning Star Reversal
THREE-CANDLE BULLISH PATTERN · RELIABILITY ★★★
Body paragraph (0.95rem, 1.6 line-height)

The Morning Star is one of the most reliable three-candle bullish reversal patterns in technical analysis. It typically appears at the bottom of a downtrend, where the market has been under pressure from sustained selling, and signals that sentiment is shifting. The pattern consists of a long bearish candle, followed by a small-bodied candle (the "star") that gaps lower, and finally a long bullish candle that closes well into the body of the first candle.

For Indian option traders, the key insight is where this pattern forms. A Morning Star at a prior support level or a CPR bottom carries much more weight than one appearing randomly in a consolidation range. Confirm with volume: the third candle should show noticeably higher turnover than the first two. Without volume, the setup is suspect.
